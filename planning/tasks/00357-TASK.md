# Prepare and inspect typed Planning candidates

## Outcome

An authorized planner obtains an editable, strictly validated structured candidate for the selected Planning
operation, with exact sources, accepted-answer revisions and existing-child comparisons. It remains an untrusted
proposal until the separately delivered explicit Create/Apply operation succeeds.

## Scope

- In scope: Planning-specific typed candidate validation and protected preview/explicit correction/discard contracts
  for Wayfinder maps/decisions, EPIC/TICKET/TASK changes, research notes and prototype findings; real generation.
- Out of scope: Authoritative Apply, template proposal schema, generic whole-document replacement, browser journey,
  persistent direct-edit drafts, prototype execution, promotion of prototype code or automatic decomposition.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare a candidate | Explicit exact-operation request through TASK-00355 | Current authorized target/children, accepted answers, typed owner contracts and base revisions | Committed proposal facts | Bounded generation of one structured candidate, not Planning events |
| Inspect or correct | Read preview; explicitly submit a complete corrected candidate or discard | Proposed/current versions, validation and provenance | Proposal correction/discard facts only on submission | Distinguishable durable candidate versions; unsaved edits remain volatile |
| Explain unusable output | N/A — read-only validation/status | Malformed, incomplete, denied, stale or unsupported details | N/A | Safe actionable rejection without salvaging apparent success |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00355's protected attempt/result authority and TASK-00356's accepted-answer/operation facts. Reuse
TASK-00085–00095's actual typed Planning contracts, current references and lifecycle/relationship constraints,
available through the verified authority chain. Do not copy domain policy into model prompts or transport code.
Bind the candidate to initiating human/acting Agent, Repository, exact target or new-artifact intent, operation,
base/queue revisions where relevant, answer revisions, draft/selection generation, snapshots and original attempt.

Use bounded closed type-specific payloads and semantic section/child values, not arbitrary document text, JSON
patches or executable policy. Reject unknown IDs/types, cross-scope relationships, incomplete collections and
malformed/partial output as unresolved. Narrative remains inert and cannot create tools, grants or approval.
Validate citations/evidence provenance; invented references and unchecked scratch files cannot become accepted
findings. TASK-00087 retains research/prototype semantics and verified durable artifact requirements; candidate
preparation neither runs prototype instructions nor makes code authoritative.

Inspect current artifacts and children. Recommend continuation or additions/explicit revisions, not automatic
attachment, reparenting or duplicate creation. Grill prepares one EPIC; Make Tickets/Make Tasks target their exact
eligible parent and complete intended set. Terminal/archived parents require separate owning actions. Wayfinder
resolution and new grill/decomposition remain distinct human intents. Factual preparation can proceed within the
bounded assignment, never choose unresolved product/priority/boundary/risk preferences.

Keep proposed, explicitly human-corrected, rejected, stale and later applied versions distinguishable. Explicit
correction appends a complete reviewed candidate version with expected-state checks; keystrokes and unsaved preview
changes are not persisted or promised recoverable. Late output cannot replace a newer correction, reattach to
another draft or revive discarded work. Current propose/read rights and owner-first visibility apply independently
of eventual mutation rights. Safe views exclude secrets, hidden reasoning and unauthorized private history.
