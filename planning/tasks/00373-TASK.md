# Reconcile Runner restart and expose safe recovery

## Outcome

Graceful or abrupt Runner restart reconciles original Workflow, transport, process and effect ownership before new
execution. Authorized operators inspect concrete holds and invoke existing recovery operations without duplicate
launches, lost claims, reset limits or premature cleanup.

## Scope

- In scope: Runner startup/shutdown reconciliation, bounded recovery dispatch, original-result recovery and safe
  console/application controls for existing pause/cancel/resume/takeover/failure/extension/cleanup operations.
- Out of scope: New Workflow lifecycle/claim engine, checkpoint/transcript store, automatic cross-machine migration,
  fabricated exactly-once effects, blind failed-queue replay, terminal reopening and post-publication cleanup.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restart or stop Runner | Existing owning shutdown/reconciliation and safe-resume operations | Gates, Workflow/claim/lease, transport, process/resource/Git/session/checkpoint/effect evidence | Confirmed recovery/hold facts through owner | Original work safely resumes or stays held, never inferred idle |
| Resolve a hold | Exact authorized existing Workflow recovery intent | Original attempt/outcome, current rights and consumed limits | Owning committed recovery facts | Same Workflow/limits; external effects reconcile before replay |
| Inspect recovery | N/A — read-only | Owner, freshness, queue/hold reason, attempts, limits and concrete next action | N/A | Safe actionable status without repair, provider call or hidden retry |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00371's real consumer/execution integration and TASK-00372's persistent gates. Reuse TASK-00139–00142
execution/custody/recovery contracts; no parallel scheduler claim, commitment writer or status setter. Startup
recognizes original Runner/boot/consumer identities and reconciles the actual owning participants before dispatch.

On graceful shutdown stop new admission, request bounded orderly child stop/checkpoint through the owner and retain
unfinished original transport work. On abrupt loss, inspect durable claim/lease/attempt plus actual process groups,
resources, Git, session/storage owner, checkpoints and known external effects. Lease expiry or missing heartbeat
alone cannot establish absence. Fence stale consumers at the authoritative write and protected effect boundary;
uncertain liveness, committed-but-unacknowledged effects and unavailable evidence hold in Needs Human.

Automatic safe recovery stays within existing current grant and limits. Explicit takeover resumes the same
Workflow; no new TASK attempt or budget reset. Preserve the default one initial process launch plus two safe
resumptions per phase, snapshotted when queued and separate from TASK-00310's shared repair allowance. Automatic
failure retries require a classified transient failure, reconciled original effects and remaining process allowance;
ordinary authorized pause/resume retains its safe-resumption semantics. Restart, QA repair or takeover cannot
refill either counter. Denied authority, genuine bound-input drift, missing
evidence, exhausted limits, unsafe source or uncertain effects hold rather than blindly retry. A newer future-run
policy selection alone is not drift: use TASK-00084/00341's guard against the original exact binding, current
revocation and actual owner/runtime facts. No resume adopts new policy or stage instructions.

A reasoned authorized limit extension appends to the grant, not its past history. The
[approved repair-extension rule](00496-TASK.md#allowance-precedence-and-human-continuation) permits an explicitly
authorized human to raise the total shared repair allowance up to the run's pinned installation maximum, initially
ten; consumed cycles stay counted. Recover the original appended grant/result rather than apply the extension again.
This does not change the separate process-attempt profile or time/cost/concurrency limits, widen anything during
automatic recovery, or replace policy/instructions. Terminal cancellation/failure cannot reopen; later work needs
separate authority and cannot abandon outstanding claims, uncertain effects or accounting.

Pause retains resources/claim. Cancellation releases only after complete child work stops or is proved absent.
Explicit cleanup requires owned-scope proof, retention/evidence holds and actual current authority; missing Artifact
or storage participants preserve content. Failed evidence and human checkouts survive. No cleanup hidden in boot,
retry, timeout or de-registration. Session and provider details remain behind existing owners, not raw transcript
imports, child credential exposure or operational logs containing sensitive payloads.

Use canonical state/audit/intent transactions with original command identities and external effects afterward.
Every console command authenticates operator/scope and expected state; status never mutates. Report safe current
owner/freshness, attempts/consumed limits, reason and supported next action without claiming powered-off execution,
unobserved success, precise unavailable usage or unsupported recovery.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real graceful/abrupt restart around delivery, provisioning, launch and completion reconciles original work
  without duplicate Agents, lost claims or fictional success; uncertain processes/effects remain held.
- [ ] Stale consumers cannot advance after takeover; same-Workflow recovery retains attempts, grants and budgets,
  and direct failed-queue replay cannot bypass recovery or reopen terminal state.
- [ ] Pause/cancel/failure/extension/cleanup retain distinct owning semantics; no claim release before complete
  quiescence and no deletion of failed evidence, held content or human-owned resources.
- [ ] Authorized console/application queries explain actual owner/status/limits and a concrete recovery action
  without leaking credentials/private content or performing hidden effects.
