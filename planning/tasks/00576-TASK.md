- Turn existing BootstrapSuperAdmin, InviteOrdinaryUser and AddUserRole intents into registered void handlers under
  their genuine User/installation capability ownership. Reuse canonical upstream aggregate/identity contracts;
  do not invent local User, Bootstrap or Invitation roots solely to organize orchestration.

  policy callers with no forwarding alias or duplicate policy authority.
- Replace BootstrapOperation and ConsoleUserOperation's union-dispatch facade with the actual handlers and safe
  current-state inspection. Split BootstrapProgress's mixed inspection/completion role: handler owns the separate
  completion-audit transaction; persistence does not begin an independent business transaction.
- Preserve explicit local-machine/operator admission, confirmation, ordinary-role restrictions, eligible additive
  assignment and existing-Super-Admin refusal. Do not mistake attribution for an authenticated browser identity.
- Preserve separately committed invitation, membership and owned evidence, including failure between steps and
  post-commit notification failure. No outer transaction around package handlers, automatic compensation/retry,
  ordinary-response journal or invented exactly-once/one-ever bootstrap guarantee. Safe committed-state inspection
  must still work when the write manager is unusable; a separate inspection connection is read-only.
- Use justified typed inspection results/registered reads and meaningful partial failures for known IDs and facts;
  Console owns success/error JSON, exit behavior and recovery instructions. Do not hide results in messages,
  event capture or a broad replacement facade. Remove superseded helpers/services/tests after replacement proof.
- Own the Console/bootstrap Domain/Application/persistence/entry/composition callers and affected runbooks/tests;
  generic safe Console failure handling stays with TICKET-00136. Keep needed identities, attribution and audit history.

Excludes new browser/admin/Agent operations, access grants, bootstrap serialization not previously justified,
