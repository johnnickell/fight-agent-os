# Schedule finite recovery independently of events and callers

## Outcome

A real protected schedule discovers and recovers retained Agent credential work after process restart even if
issuance events and the originating caller disappear. Each pass is finite, authentically delegated and governed
by package due-work/retry policy, with no consumer delivery queue or unbounded drain loop.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual operator-owned scheduling and bounded enumeration of registered original scope/destination
  pairs; real worker context; `AgentDeliveryRecoveryService`, `ListDueAgentDeliveriesHandler`, `nextRunAt`,
  concurrency/outage limits, restart enrollment and truthful safe diagnostics.
- Out of scope: General job platform, copied claim/backoff policy, automatic issuance/rotation, event outbox,
  browser recovery controls, maintenance paging, activation or reuse of the human credential-delivery worker.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Run one authorized recovery pass | `AgentDeliveryRecoveryService::recover(originalScope, registeredDestination)` | One bounded actual due query with current discovery authority | No required event; events may only accelerate already recoverable work | At most one real delivery call per selected item, exact original identity preserved |
| Schedule next pass after success/failure | Protected scheduler enrollment and bounded rescheduling using `nextRunAt()` | Registered pairs, safe pass outcomes, trusted next-run time | Safe operational observations, not issuance facts | Finite next attempt after restart/outage without event/caller dependence |
| Hold an unavailable or revoked worker | Normal discovery/delivery authorization | Actual worker/delegation, target and destination eligibility | N/A for denied reads | Truthful unavailable/denied state; no empty-queue success or scope impersonation |

## Validation and permissions

Workers authenticate as themselves with explicit current delegation to each original scope and server-registered
destination. Never reconstruct scopes from transient events or rewrite the originating caller ID. Enumerating
registrations requires current protected authority and bounded fair traversal; historical operations cannot silently
vanish from recovery merely because process-local state is lost. A revoked/unsupported registration remains a
truthful hold, not permission to bypass current target/slot policy or deliver obsolete work.

Use the package's one-selection/one-call-per-item recovery service and validated finite schedule. Call `nextRunAt`
after failed passes too, with bounded concurrency and outage backoff, not a sleeping package loop or busy polling.
A schedule timestamp is not cross-worker admission; claims and deadlines remain authoritative. Due repository
selection excludes obsolete reservations before applying its limit, across scopes and binding revisions, including
when a newer reservation is delayed or complete. At most one operation may currently be eligible for an exact
single-slot binding; do not refill the page by selecting obsolete work.

Discovery rechecks authority even for empty results and each target before disclosure. Read allow is not delivery
admission. Each selected item goes through TASK-00238's complete current service. Failures cannot become empty
work or confirmed delivery; there is no retry with a new operation ID or notification-retry guarantee. Restart and
new configuration cannot reset leases, attempt counts, retention or pinned policy. Existing recovery remains
available under new-work capacity pressure when its own authority/storage are valid. Diagnostics reveal safe
results and scheduling state, not material/claims/receipts/provider payloads.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual enrolled scheduling resumes after worker/process termination without events, caller retry or a
  process-local queue; both provision and rotation work survive simultaneous issuance publisher failures.
- [ ] Each pass performs bounded enumeration/selection and at most one real delivery call per selected item;
  failure reschedules through finite timing and concurrency limits without busy polling or a refill loop.
- [ ] More than a default batch of obsolete reservations cannot starve the current one. Registration/binding
  changes after selection are rechecked at delivery, never replaced by a cached discovery allow.
- [ ] Lost results/expired leases recover only original IDs/bytes when due, while unsupported/unauthorized/outage
  states remain truthful. No repeated issuance/audit, reset retention or new-operation capacity dependency occurs.
- [ ] Worker revocation and historical-destination limits remain effective across actual restart, and safe
  diagnostics distinguish attempted, confirmed, deferred and indeterminate outcomes.
