# Resolve current Agent eligibility for selected work

## Outcome

A protected read resolves current eligible Team/Agent candidates or safe hold reasons for one human-selected TASK.
It combines owning TASK/base eligibility with exact role profiles, Team/Repository scope, live authority and actual
idle capacity. It does not choose another TASK, reserve an Agent, issue a grant or authorize later execution.

## Scope

- In scope: Owned cross-boundary candidate-fact resolution and safe Application/adapter result, current authority,
  exact profile/resource suitability, relevant commitment/capacity and independence facts, coherent provenance and
  explicit unavailable/ambiguous results through existing eligibility contracts.
- Out of scope: Another canonical TASK eligibility algorithm, queue scan, priority mutation, claim/commitment store,
  fairness policy, phase admission, provider invocation, browser Planning cutover or model-based selection.

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |

| Explain an unavailable candidate | N/A — read-only | Current credential/use, commitment, base/reference and independence observations | N/A | Bounded authorized reasons with exact revision/provenance, not fabricated idleness |

## Validation and permissions

Require the human's current scoped selection/start authority and visibility for the selected TASK; independently
validate actual named Agent direct Permissions and applicable grant. Intersect current credential/activation/use,
Team/Repository association, bound Repository/context/profile, exact role/resource/model requirements and qualified
host/Runner capacity through their owners. A template title, ACTIVE row, signed request or advisory snapshot is not
eligibility. Do not sign requests, contact a provider, launch a probe session or mutate authority just to inspect.

Consume TASK-00139's owning eligibility capability and immutable Markdown TASK/dependency/context adapter before
cutover. Do not parse a second Board or reproduce lifecycle/blocker policy in this selector. Where TASK-00096's
canonical database Planning evaluator is the actual source, extend its typed fact/reason contract rather than
forking it. Database cutover is not a prerequisite for core terminal selection; its later browser/Triage consumers
retain their own actual Planning prerequisites.

A prerequisite marked Done is not sufficient: require the owning verified inclusion result for its required
change on the selected Repository's configured exact base. Unmerged, missing, retargeted, replaced-head,
inaccessible or ambiguous evidence holds unless that owner has actually established accepted inclusion under
its supported merge strategy. Do not invent GitHub/ancestry inference or equate a webhook with reconciliation.
Bind observed base/configuration/Planning revisions so later start can reject stale facts and pin the accepted OID.

Use actual admitted/reserved session, attempt and conflicting commitment state across relevant work/Repositories.
Waiting for human review or PR merge alone does not occupy an Agent. Conversely, expired leases, absent process
observations, disconnected clients and missing capability do not establish idle. Unknown liveness holds until its
owner reconciles. Required independent-review capacity uses actual contribution/subject lineage, not a different
name or new session as a substitute. Do not prematurely reserve a reviewer merely by inspecting availability.

Read a coherent exact fact set, detecting mixed generations and applying current access before candidate/reason
presentation. Stable bounded pagination must not silently lose eligible alternatives. Unknown/outage differs from
zero candidates; redact inaccessible TASK/ancestor/Agent/provider detail and all credentials, claim/lease tokens,
protected handles and private tool/session content. An eligible observation expires with its facts and grants
nothing: TASK-00263/00264 revalidate inside their real owning admission boundary.
