# Transfer idle Agents and remove associations safely

## Outcome

A currently authorized administrator can confirm an exact idle-Agent transfer or safe association/reference
removal without stranding current assignments, changing historical session provenance or racing an admitted
operation. Busy, stale, unsupported and uncertain cases hold rather than silently move or detach an identity.

## Scope

- In scope: Safe impact/status query, exact confirmed transfer, guarded Repository unlink/Team-reference removal,
  atomic default replacement where necessary, current commitment/reference checks and participation of actual
  enabled session/start writers in shared fences; protected server/API and safe original-outcome reconciliation.
- Out of scope: Forced transfer, orphaning Agents, cascade deletion/history rewriting, stopping live work as a
  hidden side effect, new scheduling/commitment/claim platform, credential rotation or browser roster UI.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preview bounded transfer/removal | N/A — read-only | Current Agent/Team/Repository, exact affected references, default and owning commitments | N/A | Safe current impact/hold reasons, not a reservation or authorization token |
| Confirm idle transfer | Owned exact expected-state transfer under current Team/Agent-management authority | Fresh source/destination association and complete relevant commitments | Owned membership-transfer/audit facts only after commit | One atomic replacement of current membership; historical session bindings remain unchanged |
| Unlink/remove safe references | Owned confirmed unlink/removal and explicit default replacement when required | Current association/default, Agent assignments and active work references | Owned association/default/removal facts after commit | Only safe references change, with valid remaining state and retained history |
| Reconcile uncertain outcome | Read original command/outcome and current authoritative state | Actual commit, current membership/reference revisions and ongoing obligations | No fabricated success or automatic inverse event | Confirmed result or truthful unresolved hold; no blind repeated transfer |

## Validation and permissions

Use TASK-00253's canonical membership/reference and cohort-first fences, complete expected state and monotonic
relevant authority epochs, on one shared connection through commit/rollback. Revalidate current intent-specific
managed administrative rights, actor/target/scope and the exact human confirmation, including no-ops. A prior
preview, a Team Lead role, browser permission state or a signed request is not current authorization.

Determine idleness from actual owning active work, admitted/reserved session/attempt and conflicting commitment
state, not a lease expiry, process absence, client timer, cached busy flag or a copied second commitment registry.
Waiting for human review or PR merge does not alone occupy an Agent; active or uncertain commitments still block
transfer. Missing participant state is unknown, not an empty set. A process with uncertain liveness cannot be
made idle by changing its Team. Every enabled admission/commitment writer must serialize with the same fences;
validate races at the authoritative write, not by polling before transfer.

Transfer preserves exactly one current Team, separately checking that the destination serves the required
Repositories and does not expand the Agent's bound Repository scope or grants. Never rewrite recorded Team,
Agent, template, context, grant or session provenance, and never automatically rotate or share credentials.
A retained historical session must not resume with its old admission after a transfer; its owner revalidates or
requires a separately authorized new continuation. Later current-use checks reject old epochs, including a
transfer back to the same Team.

Unlink/removal must leave current assignments and active work valid. If a Repository retains Teams, removing its
default requires an explicit valid replacement atomically; if no associations remain, no dangling default remains.
A surviving Team must retain its required Repository association(s). Refuse removal that would strand any Agent,
needed reference or commitment. No bare unassignment creates a zero-Team Agent. Remove a Team reference only when
its owned lifecycle/reference policy proves it safe, retaining identities/history needed by old sessions/audit;
this does not authorize deleting history or adding a broad purge/lifecycle feature.

State, required audit and relevant revisions commit together; external liveness probes/effects are outside the
transaction and cannot stand in for authoritative fenced commitment state. Stale confirmation requires refetch
and renewed human confirmation. Lost commit acknowledgement or notification failure does not prove rollback;
resolve the original outcome before dependent changes, with no automatic reverse transfer or force option.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A genuinely idle eligible Agent transfers atomically to exactly one destination Team under fresh confirmed
  authority without changing credential, grant, scope or historical session bindings.
- [ ] Real competing transfer versus enabled admission/commitment writers in both winner orders prevents double
  booking or moving active work; unknown/waiting-ambiguous liveness holds, while mere PR-wait labels do not imply busy.
- [ ] Concurrent transfers, stale confirmations, no-ops, transfer-back ABA and revoke/regrant preserve exact
  membership/epochs and deny old admission. Historical session resume cannot inherit the new Team silently.
- [ ] Unlink/default replacement/removal preserves remaining defaults, required Team associations, Agent scope,
  active commitments and history; unsafe last-link/default/Team removal refuses without partial audit or state.
- [ ] Direct/API authorization, rollback, lost acknowledgement and post-commit notification failures are safe,
  distinguishable and secret-free; no force, cascade, polling-only interlock or hidden workload cancellation exists.
