# Retain and redact history across derived copies

## Outcome

History remains useful and attributable under legitimate retention while prohibited or eligible removed content
cannot reappear through observation copies, search results or rebuild. Operators can distinguish current denial,
confirmed removal and unsupported backup/copy coverage without another lifecycle policy or deletion engine.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual observation/projection/search/reference participants in existing retention/redaction operations,
  safe tombstones, held-category handling, bounded copy reconciliation and fenced replay/restore/late publication.
- Out of scope: New retention or redaction policy, ordinary-user history deletion, rewriting source decisions,
  transcript retention, arbitrary filesystem cleanup and universal physical-erasure claims.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Protect required history | Existing owner hold/reference operations with real history participants | Actual categories, active references and owning disposition | Existing committed lifecycle facts | Atomic protection of required material, not another policy store |
| Restrict or remove affected copies | Existing TASK-00380/00379 operations using exact history-copy participants | Current generations, copy inventory and original intent/outcome | Secret-free restriction/tombstone/removal facts | Denied disclosure and bounded reconciled copy removal |
| Rebuild or inspect corrected history | Existing bounded rebuild; inspection remains read-only | Current tombstones, safe sources and actual coverage | Safe projection result only | No resurrection of removed bytes or fabricated complete erasure |
