# Stage immutable credentials in the fixed protected sink

## Outcome

A real registered protected sink stages immutable exact credential tuples, independently verifies/looks up receipts
and tombstones exact inert entries. Global identity, nondecreasing stable-slot order and durable history prevent
late or repeated calls from selecting obsolete authority or recreating removed material.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual `AgentCredentialSink`, `AgentCredentialReceiptLookup` and `AgentCredentialCleanup` adapters;
  protected storage/access, atomic stage/remove, immutable tuple/byte binding, opaque receipts, permanent deduplication
  tombstones and slot high-water preservation across restart/rebinding; current-consumer selection boundary.
- Out of scope: Delivery scheduling/admission, Agent activation or broker implementation, generic secret-read API,
  caller-specified URL/path, provider credential platform, automatic rotation, physical key destruction or deployment.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stage the admitted original invocation | `assertSupported` then `stage` through the fixed registered sink | Actual sink support and original identity/order | N/A — package state, not a new sink event, records delivery | Atomic immutable protected entry and exact opaque receipt; identical retry does not change selection |
| Verify or reconcile receipt | N/A — metadata-only `verify` / `lookupReceipt` | Independent durable exact tuple/entry evidence | N/A | Verified receipt or authoritative absence; outage never becomes absence or secret retrieval |
| Remove an inert exact entry | `AgentCredentialCleanup::remove` after owning admission | Exact original tuple and current tombstone/order evidence | N/A — cleanup acknowledgement belongs to package persistence | Atomic erasure/non-recreation tombstone, even before first arrival, preserving successor and high-water |

## Validation and permissions

Use TASK-00234's registered stable destination and immutable revision and TASK-00235's protected storage/key
boundary. Delivery ID alone is the global idempotency key across scope collisions, retries, takeover and rewrap.
Bind it to every original issuance field and exact bytes; changed tuple or material rejects without modifying
existing state. Slot order spans all Agents/scopes/binding revisions and cannot reset on reassignment or cleanup.
Identical old retries may resolve original history without making it current; tombstoned material cannot reappear.

Receipts are opaque package-valid values, not digests, paths or secret-read capabilities. Verification must inspect
independent durable evidence, not echo a format-valid token. Metadata lookup discloses no credential bytes and
must distinguish unavailable state from confirmed absence. Selected receipt-lookup support is part of this TASK's
actual profile; do not claim an unqualified alternative profile.

All stage/receipt/cleanup calls run outside the package database transaction. The sink must supply its own atomic
storage guarantees, not hide provider calls inside the business transaction. Already-admitted late effects are
inert: no arrival pointer or receipt grants activation/signing. TICKET-00061 alone supplies exact current credential/
destination selection, fresh enrollment authorization and each later use check. Closed activation remains closed
until that boundary qualifies. A cleanup removes only its permanently terminal target, never a slot or successor.

Protect invocation lifetime and sensitive concrete parameters; sanitized typed failures omit previous exceptions,
provider messages and key paths. No browser, Pi child, model or ordinary log receives sink material or internals.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual protected sink supports immutable all-field/byte matching, global deduplication and stable-slot order
  under independent concurrent callers and restart, including equal IDs in different operation scopes.
- [ ] Both arrival orders, binding reassignment, equal-order conflicting entries and delayed predecessor retries
  cannot replace/select newer authority; identical retries preserve their original receipt without resetting order.
- [ ] Real verification/lookup reject missing/swapped/lost material or receipt; outages deny truthfully without
  reinterpreting them as absence. No metadata read reveals or rematerializes a secret.
- [ ] Cleanup before/after arrival and after a lost response permanently prevents recreation, preserves exact
  receipt/tombstone/order history and cannot erase a successor. Wrong tuple cleanup has no effect.
- [ ] Real adapter calls demonstrate outside-business-transaction execution and sanitized errors. Staged bytes
  remain unavailable to launch/use until the separately owned current activation/use boundary qualifies.
