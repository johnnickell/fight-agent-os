# Verify accepted behavior through managed QA

## Outcome

A distinct managed QA Engineer challenges the technically accepted implementation, records exact-subject behavioral
evidence and prevents publication until current QA passes or has justified overall non-applicability.

## Scope

- In scope: Independent QA admission/session, scenario and applicability assessment, isolated behavioral probes,
  immutable attributed findings/disposition/capture references, publication eligibility and shared repair routing.
- Out of scope: QA source/test repair, technical self-acceptance, a second revision counter, public preview hosting,
  artifact publication, another Artifact store and the named Team integration owned by TASK-00268.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admit behavioral verification | Existing Workflow QA-phase admission | Exact acceptance, subject, role independence, scenarios and qualified resources | QA admission/hold facts | Separate scoped QA session and isolated disposable services/data |
| Record verification | Finalize subject-bound scenarios/findings/disposition | Actual runtime/source, observations, captures and required scenario coverage | Attributed pass/fail/incomplete/justified N/A facts | Immutable QA evidence, no implementation mutation |
| Gate or route repair | Owning publication eligibility and shared blocking-finding revision operations | Current acceptance, QA applicability, findings and consumed limits | Existing hold/revision/invalidation facts | Publication denied or eligible; Engineer alone repairs |

## Validation and permissions

Apply [QA standards](../../docs/engineering/QA.md) and EPIC-00008's managed requirements, not local skill completion
as runtime evidence. Require a distinct authenticated non-contributing QA Agent, fresh isolated role session and
live direct Permissions intersected with current Workflow/human, Repository/context/profile and host constraints.
Neither a role title nor prior acceptance grants tools, data access or publication authority.

Bind QA to Workflow/TASK revision, current technical acceptance, configured base/full commits, immutable manifest,
evidence digests and exact running source/image. Revalidate at disposition commit; a URL, screenshot or healthy
service does not prove subject identity. Missing tools, unavailable required scenarios and stale runtime produce
incomplete verification, not N/A. API, CLI, domain and executable-instruction behavior remain applicable without UI.

Exercise selected success, rejection/error and relevant responsive/interaction states. Preserve every required or
requested scenario until satisfied, proven reusable or explicitly excluded under authority. Record known baseline
and reviewed captures with comparable data/dimensions, source and Artifact identity; unavailable TUI image capture
uses honest transcript/recording evidence and limits. Sanitize secrets, personal data and private transport content.
Visual N/A is distinct from behavioral N/A; pure nonbehavioral changes need reasoned applicability assessment.

QA may mutate only qualified owned disposable runtime/data and QA evidence, never reviewed source or tests. Only
persisted blocking findings invoke TASK-00310's existing allowance; material repair receives fresh Review and
affected QA. Unchanged evidence needs explicit applicability, with mechanical bridges owned by TASK-00313.
Additional QA evidence alone leaves technical acceptance intact; established technical blockers invoke TASK-00311.

Commit disposition/state/audit under expected revisions; external probes/dispatch happen outside transactions.
Unknown original outcomes, cancellation or lost liveness retain honest holds and reconcile before another phase.
Failed, interrupted, incomplete, stale or absent required QA cannot pass the publication gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An actual distinct QA session exercises genuinely applicable behavior of the exact accepted runtime,
  retains scenarios and observations, and cannot repair source, accept technical work or publish.
- [ ] Relevant visual/interaction and non-UI probes supply attributable evidence; N/A is justified, and missing
  tooling/scenarios cannot be silently reclassified to pass.
- [ ] Stale/revoked/failed/incomplete QA denies publication; current PASS or valid overall N/A is required.
- [ ] Blocking findings share the existing revision allowance, material repair returns through Review and
  affected QA, and supporting evidence alone does not invalidate technical acceptance.
- [ ] Concurrent subject/authority changes and interrupted original operations preserve immutable history,
  exact disposition applicability and safe recovery without duplicate phase execution.

## Verification and evidence

Test owned admission, applicability, disposition, publication gate and repair routing, including PostgreSQL races.
Use real qualified browser/PTY/API/CLI tools for the changed behavior; qualify tools directly, not with product
configuration or screenshot-text tests. Record source/runtime, session, acceptance/QA, scenarios, captures and
limits without secrets. Run focused checks and `./bin/build`; distinguish actual probes, inherited evidence,
controlled fixtures and unverified behavior. Preserve private evidence outside disposable resources.
