# Assess the owned PHPUnit suite during architectural review

## Outcome

An authorized architectural review explicitly scoped to the entire owned PHPUnit suite can inventory and assess
all owned cases in bounded, resumable stages using the same usefulness criteria as changed-case handoff. The report
shows actual coverage, criterion counts/proportions and verified versus unverified concerns without implying test
execution, whole-suite completeness from sampling or a numerical correctness guarantee.

## Scope

- Extend [TASK-00181](00181-TASK.md)'s case identities, typed criteria, evidence handling and reporting using the
  inherited TASK-00172 staged-investigation/protected-progress contract. Remain on Pi 1.1.0; reuse the shared
  evaluator/resolver rather than a separate audit service or classifier. No dependency on incremental reuse.
- Activate full-suite mode only when the human-selected architectural-review scope explicitly includes all owned
  PHPUnit cases. Integrate the governed architecture/audit path appropriate to the actual request, preserving its
  design/compliance distinction and independent formal-review ownership. An ordinary changed-case review, tool
  installation or scheduled session does not authorize recurring/full-suite assessment.
- Pin repository, source revision/content identity, suite/configuration and ownership policy before discovery.
  Inventory owned cases across all applicable suites, preserving the declaration/dataset/execution distinctions
  defined by TASK-00181. Include shared setup/providers, inherited/trait-consumed cases and relevant dependencies
  when establishing case identity/context; record ambiguous ownership or discovery as explicit unresolved scope.
- Exclude unowned upstream/generated cases with reasons while accounting for maintained generators or deliberate
  maintained patches where applicable. Exclusion never waives other review obligations. Skipped/disabled/incomplete
  execution status is separate from whether a declared owned case can be assessed; a passed execution is not an
  assessment. Deduplicate suite aliases without losing distinct cases or conflating counts of methods and datasets.
- Prefer bounded source inventory and current qualified discovery receipts. Do not execute providers, bootstrap,
  autoloading, tests or application code just to complete inventory. Dynamic unresolved populations remain unknown;
  optional separately authorized discovery/execution follows its owning guarded path and has its own receipts.
  Never run against production or turn production observation/SQL authority into test execution or fixture access.
- Use the same observable-contract, plausible-defect, independent-expectation and refactor-resilience criteria and
  supported/concern/unknown/not-applicable outcomes as TASK-00181. Reuse current permitted related source,
  requirements and historical execution evidence with provenance, not private Engineer context or old acceptance.
- Require an explicit finite whole-assessment profile for enumeration/cases/evidence/output/retained metadata,
  elapsed active time, concurrency, upstream requests and spend. Retain shared per-file/request/stage ceilings
  while permitting many stages under a qualified aggregate allocation. Missing limits do not mean unlimited work;
  budget exhaustion yields partial coverage rather than silent sampling, raised ceilings or preferred-answer retries.
- Start/inspect/resume/cancel through the existing protected investigation lifecycle. Persist scope/case manifest,
  criterion/context/evaluator/policy identities, current progress/coverage and usage reservations without source
  bodies. On resume recheck current caller/source/disclosure authority and relevant input identities; changed suite
  membership or source cannot be merged silently into a claimed single-revision complete assessment.
- Stop/reload/cancel and late results preserve active-generation rules and uncertain billing. Resume continues only
  verified safe pending work without resetting aggregate budgets or replaying execution. Corrupt/expired/unavailable
  progress produces explicit gaps or a blocked continuation, not fabricated assessed cases or completed coverage.
- Provide bounded authorized stage inspection and a final or partial report with the requested scope/revision,
  discovered/eligible/assessed/partial/unknown/excluded populations and unknown totals where appropriate. State what
  remains, why and whether full inventory was established. No complete-suite claim while any required inventory or
  criterion coverage remains unresolved; a scoped subset must be labeled as such rather than renamed full suite.
- Report per-criterion outcome counts and proportions with explicit unit/denominator and separate coverage rates.
  Keep N/A, unknown and unavailable populations visible; avoid zero-denominator percentages and double-counted
  overlapping concerns. Model concern counts remain separate from attributed reviewer-confirmed/dismissed findings.
  Comparisons require compatible scope/criteria/counting units; no global good/bad cutoff or correctness percentage.
- Support bounded reviewer-directed inspection of candidate cases and exact expectation/behavior evidence under
  current permissions. Existing ST-04/SP-05 findings require independent verification; the report cannot delete
  tests, rewrite expectations or dispense with uniquely useful coverage. Record proposed corrections only through
  the owning architectural-review/planning handoff, not automatic execution or new acceptance authority.
- Author actual integration instructions/presets using Writing for Agents and qualify their Pi delivery. Preserve
  advisory outage handling: missing model results do not require a special waiver or prevent ordinary review,
  though absent required evidence still follows existing standards. No automatic audit schedule is introduced.
- Exclude mutation tools, seeded product failures, tests of tests, automatic test repair/deletion, production
  execution/data fixtures, recurring monitoring and broad runtime/SQL analysis.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start an explicitly scoped suite assessment | Admit a bounded review-scoped job | Owned suite/case inventory, current authority and criteria | Admitted/rejected/stage observations | Authorized source reads/provider usage and protected progress |
| Inspect or resume progress | Resume with revalidation when requested | Coverage, pending cases, input identity and remaining budget | Resume/invalidation/gap observations | Continue only permitted bounded work; no implicit PHPUnit execution |
| Inspect criterion coverage | N/A: reporting is read-only | Outcome counts/proportions, denominators and verified/unverified concerns | Existing evidence access observations where applicable | Bounded authorized report without raw source/context ingestion |

| Cancel an assessment | Cancel owned investigation | Actual progress/generation and usage state | Cancellation/uncertainty observations | Stop dispatch and stale delivery; preserve required accounting |

## Validation and permissions

The architectural-review scope selects population, not new file/provider/runtime permissions. Every stage,
progress view and reference lookup enforces current caller/source/egress rights. Protect cross-session/reviewer
boundaries and bind source populations to verified revisions. Discovery and stored references are untrusted data;
provider/test source cannot execute through inventory resolution. Historical receipts remain historical. Shared
profile and lifecycle limits prevent unbounded enumeration or resumed spending. No advisory result grants review
acceptance, test repair, publication or production access.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A real Pi 1.1.0 architectural review with explicit all-owned-suite scope assesses a representative many-stage
  corpus under a qualified finite profile; ordinary changed-case review does not start that mode automatically.
- [ ] Inventory covers the owned suite or exposes exact unresolved populations, with justified exclusions and
  distinct declaration/dataset/execution identities; dynamic discovery cannot execute code without separate authority.
- [ ] Interruption/resume/cancellation and changed source/suite/authority preserve trustworthy progress, aggregate
  budgets and explicit gaps without duplicate counting, stale completion or implicit test/effect replay.
- [ ] Reports show source scope, stage coverage and criterion counts/proportions with honest denominators, unknowns,
  N/A and verified/unverified concerns. Sampling or partial discovery never becomes a complete-suite claim.
- [ ] Candidate concerns require exact independent evidence before findings; legitimate boundary contracts retain
  their meaning, and no test is automatically deleted, rewritten or labeled absolutely unable to fail.
- [ ] Actual scoped workflow/preset delivery and provider limitations are qualified separately from instructions;
  outage gaps preserve ordinary review and required gates without a special advisor waiver.
- [ ] Representative review evidence reports usefulness, false flags, missed concerns, complete/incomplete coverage,
  available total cost and latency while preserving all test-execution and production-data boundaries.
