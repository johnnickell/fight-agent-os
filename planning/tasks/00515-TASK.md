# Synchronize current handoffs during Land publication

## Outcome

Make the shared Land workflow reconcile applicable current Board, Roadmap and README handoffs alongside
parent rollups and generated views before returning control to the human. Maintainers receive a truthful
current action while historical acceptance and delivery checkpoints remain intact. This is the first
independently reviewable slice of [TICKET-00118](../tickets/00118-TICKET.md).

## Scope

- Update the owning Land skill, landing standards and necessary shared handoff guidance to inventory the exact
  subject's current authored handoffs, reconcile their authoritative sources and refresh derived views.
- Distinguish historical acceptance/delivery-pending evidence from current instructions without rewriting
  canonical reports or claiming publication/final-head success before it is verified.
- Require a precise hold for missing evidence, contradictory handoffs or unauthorized changes; preserve
  unrelated routing, requirement ownership and existing review/QA/cleanup gates.
- Verify normal publication and refusal paths directly as part of the same change.
- Out of scope: post-merge recovery (TASK-00516), validator/live-routing implementation (TICKET-00119),
  project-local rollout (TICKET-00120), implementation defect repair, managed database Workflows, automatic
  merge, release/deployment, archive and unrelated cleanup.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare a truthful landing handoff | Existing `land` invocation and authorized planning edits/refresh | Exact subject, current authored handoffs, source records, generated views and delivery evidence | N/A — local workflow, no Domain event | Current instructions and views agree with the actual stage; historical checkpoints remain intact |
| Stop on inconsistent or missing evidence | Existing Land hold/handoff | Acceptance provenance, contradictory instructions, publication authority and verification limits | Local receipt only | Precise recovery action, no invented success or unrelated repair |

No new application Command, Query, permission, schema or event is introduced. Hosting observations use existing
Land capabilities; this slice must not depend on the future validator or rollout.

## Validation and permissions

Follow [landing standards](../../docs/engineering/LANDING.md),
[handoff rules](../../docs/engineering/HANDOFFS.md),
[planning conventions](../CONVENTIONS.md) and
[Writing for Agents](../../.pi/skills/writing-for-agents/SKILL.md).
Keep one shared owner for universal rules; qualify actual callers/bindings rather than maintaining competing
copies. Planning and documentation edits do not grant publication, merge or role authority.

Preserve accepted-candidate/administrative-closeout provenance and required final-head delivery checks. A
historical pending checkpoint must not force another metadata commit merely to certify the final head. Changes
to requirements, implementation or acceptance remain outside routine Land reconciliation. Missing authority,
unsafe checkout state, ambiguous identity or unresolved contradiction stops the affected operation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Shared Land instructions explicitly cover applicable authored current Board/Roadmap/README handoffs as
  well as source records, parent rollups and generated views for the exact subject.
- [ ] A normal-landing walkthrough shows consistent current instructions at the actual publication stage,
  without anticipating human merge or falsely claiming final delivery verification.
- [ ] Canonical review/QA reports, accepted-candidate provenance and historical completion/delivery checkpoints
  remain intact and identifiable as historical evidence.
- [ ] Missing evidence and contradictory handoffs produce precise holds rather than false success, unrelated
  repairs or acceptance toggles.
- [ ] Existing publication, review/QA and cleanup boundaries remain intact; no recurring final-head metadata
  loop, automatic merge or new hosted-CI requirement is introduced.
- [ ] Direct workflow verification, planning validation, canonical full gate and independent review/QA evidence
  identify the exact candidate, warnings and limitations.
