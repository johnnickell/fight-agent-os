# Apply authorized exposure-response purge across log copies

## Outcome

The existing privacy owner can deny and reconcile removal of exact exposed operational content across actual log
copies, preserving only safe attribution and honest coverage. This approved slice F of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) extends TASK-00380's controlled incident operation; it is not automatic
pipeline redaction or a new right conferred by reading logs.

## Scope

- In scope: Exact current-authorized operational-copy restriction, bounded participant inventory, recoverable
  removal/replacement, late-publication fences, secret-free tombstones and copy-specific status/limitations.
- Out of scope: General DLP/detection platform, raw transcript ingestion, source repair, arbitrary storage traversal,
  mutable accepted archives, new purge authority and assertions that downloads/backups can be remotely recalled.

Consume [TASK-00380](00380-TASK.md)'s actual privacy operation and TASK-00467's early restriction/tombstone/lifecycle
participants. Immediate supported denial must already be active before capture/read exposure; this TASK adds the
complete operational-copy execution, not a period where known prohibited content remains accessible until purge
ships. Ordinary retention/holds cannot justify retaining a prohibited audit body. The authorization and exact scope
of exceptional removal remain with the existing owner, not the log collector or a role-name shortcut.

Inventory exact affected pending queues/holding, live indexes/chunks, archive catalog/segments/object versions,
temporary restore scopes, caches and owned extraction copies through their owning capabilities. No generic
filesystem, bucket or backend-query proxy. Each participant reports real known/pending/unsupported coverage;
missing participation blocks complete-removal claims and keeps affected content denied. Future participants must
join the early fence before exposure, without reverse-blocking capture primitives on finished UI journeys.

Commit restriction generation, original operation and required safe audit/intent before external effects. Fence
late ingest, retry/replay, archive verification/catalog publication, reads/output, restore import/readiness and
export completion so restricted data cannot be republished. Current output checks withhold further bytes but cannot
recall released/in-flight content. Reconcile original exact target versions/generations; no stale worker may remove
unrelated, shared or successor material.

For mixed archive segments, create a distinct immutable verified replacement excluding affected content where the
owner authorizes it, preserving every non-purged protected member's original bytes, clocks and references. Verify
compressed/raw digest/size/framing/count before replacement publication and reference settlement. Never mutate
bytes beneath an accepted digest, silently recertify original evidence or leave prohibited payloads in tombstones.
Retain only secret-free identity/digest, actor or policy, time, reason and attributed replacement/restriction facts.

Use existing bounded queue/storage/catalog operations and finite reconciliation, not a second spool or unbounded
copy enumeration. Report logical denial, requested/confirmed removal, physical reclamation and actual backup expiry
separately. Unowned downloads and independently managed backups retain explicit limitations; local-only archives
are not off-machine backup. Coordinate genuine policy conflicts with the privacy owner rather than weakening the
no-pipeline-redaction or preservation contract by implication.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restrict exact exposed log content | Existing authorized expected-generation privacy operation | Actual scope, owner authority and affected copy inventory | Committed safe restriction/intent facts | Immediate supported denial without copying the prohibited body into audit |
| Remove supported copies | Owner-specific bounded original purge operation | Actual queue/index/object/restore/cache outcomes | Copy-specific pending/confirmed disposition | External effects reconciled against original targets |
| Replace a mixed archive safely | Owner-authorized immutable replacement operation | Original member references and verified replacement content | Attributed replacement/tombstone facts | Non-purged protected content survives without accepted-digest mutation |
| Inspect limits and recover interruption | Reconcile original operation; read status | Known copy coverage, current restrictions and backup limitations | Safe recovery facts | No false universal erasure or resurrection after restart/replay |

## Validation and permissions

Require actual narrow current purge authority and exact owned content scope. READ_LOGS, RESTORE_LOG_ARCHIVES,
EXPORT_LOGS and DOWNLOAD_LOG_ARCHIVES supply no purge/hold/storage power. Use ADRs
[0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md) for shared state/audit/intent and provider boundaries.
Do not emit test markers/prohibited originals in status, errors or ordinary diagnostics merely to prove removal.
Related evidence/Review applicability remains with existing owners; log purge never issues acceptance or changes
Workflow disposition. Browser/output consumers retain current authority and volatile-state rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual existing privacy authority restricts exact operational targets and denies current/late disclosure;
  ordinary log rights, stale operations and unknown ownership cannot authorize purge or hidden-target discovery.
- [ ] Real supported queue/index/archive/version/restore/cache/extraction participants honor restriction generations
  across retry/replay/publication/output; missing copy coverage is explicit and prevents complete-removal claims.
- [ ] Mixed-segment replacement preserves all non-purged protected bytes/clocks/references under a new verified
  identity, with no mutation beneath accepted digests or prohibited payloads retained as audit evidence.
- [ ] Interrupted/ambiguous effects reconcile original targets and safe tombstones; logical denial, removal,
  reclamation, external downloads and independently owned backup limits remain distinct.
- [ ] Owned privacy/authority/real PostgreSQL race tests, direct copy/storage qualification and full `./bin/build`
  pass with exact coverage and no fixture-only substitute for required participants.

## Verification and evidence

Use isolated non-sensitive exposure markers, never real credentials or transcripts. Exercise actual copy owners,
concurrent ingest/verification/restore/output, mixed-segment replacement, lost responses, stale workers and rebuild
from older state. Inspect safe status/audit and actual before/after accessibility, reporting unrecalled bytes and
unsupported backup routes. Test owned behavior and integration; qualify provider/tooling directly, not configuration,
migrations or vendor internals. Run focused wrappers and full `./bin/build`; record target inventory, warnings,
uncertainty and separately authorized destructive-test limits.
