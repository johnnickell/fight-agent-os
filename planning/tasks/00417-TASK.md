# Preview execute and reconcile explicit cleanup

## Outcome

An authorized human inspects the complete exact local/remote cleanup impact, confirms it separately and receives
verified removed/retained/uncertain results from owning adapters. Replaced, unrelated, referenced or uncertain
resources remain protected, including after interruption or lost acknowledgement.

## Scope

- In scope: Real impact inspection composition, browser preview/confirmation and controls, admitted cleanup
  dispatch/result integration and original-operation reconciliation with retained safe history.
- Out of scope: New filesystem/provider deletion adapter, automatic compensation, general garbage collection,
  snapshot/registration/profile/context deletion, credential enrollment or another attempt lifecycle.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inspect complete impact | Explicit qualified read-only impact inspection | Actual owned local/remote targets, references, quiescence and current authority | N/A | Exact fresh bounded preview or refusal; no deletion |
| Confirm and execute cleanup | TASK-00413 exact separate confirmation; dispatch to owning adapters | Bound impact and original outcome | Existing attributed consent/cleanup facts | Only separately admitted local/provider effects |
| Recover partial cleanup | Explicit original-operation reconciliation | Verified removed/retained/unknown identities and exclusion | Verified result or hold | No blind deletion retry against reused names/paths |
| Inspect cleanup history | N/A — durable read-only | Protected original consent, actual outcomes and retained resources | N/A | No live provider/path scan or cleanup replay |
