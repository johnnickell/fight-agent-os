# Supervise enrolled capture and lifecycle workers

## Outcome

An explicitly enrolled installation runs bounded capture and lifecycle processes with independent supervision and
truthful process/resource disposition. This approved slice C of [TICKET-00117](../tickets/00117-TICKET.md) supplies
the actual runtime bindings needed by capture, archive and recovery owners without reimplementing their state machines.

## Scope

- In scope: Exact selected reader/runtime/driver enrollment, scoped process/service/worker identity, independent
  supervisor and status I/O binding, fixed owner entrypoints, bounded scheduling/restart/stop and physical accounting.
- Out of scope: Native collector selection, checkpoint/queue logic, status semantics, archive verification, retention/
  purge decisions, another scheduler/job platform, arbitrary process execution or silent service repair from reads.

Use TASK-00481 authority/inventory, TASK-00482 allocations and TASK-00455's resolved native composition. Enroll only
explicitly owned application/supporting-service/managed-execution sources; project runtime logs and transcripts remain
excluded. Actual managed registration/retirement/context inputs still belong to TASK-00140/00141, not Docker labels
or broad daemon discovery. Preserve stdout/stderr and the selected native driver/read mechanisms without unsupported
private-file scraping. Privileged reader exposure requires its own security acceptance; docker-gen is not a grant.

Bind supervisor/process identity and bounded status I/O independently of the collector and search. TASK-00456 owns
five-second observations/three-miss unknown, finite control/loss summaries and producer-status semantics; supply the
actual lifecycle/storage mechanisms for those contracts, not another status database. Qualify collector death without
requiring a last self-report, and disclose shared host/storage loss limits. Preserve its reserved control budget.

Use fixed owner-scoped worker entrypoints and existing scheduler/supervisor roles, bounded counts/resources,
job-level exclusion, restart policies and shutdown deadlines. Collection retains WF-038's 512-MiB/1-vCPU, concurrency,
queue and 30-second shutdown profile; add measured finite supervisor/archive/lifecycle/backend process budgets and
headroom. A process restart does not replay business work, upgrade an image, migrate schema or settle an uncertain
operation. Owner code reconciles original claims/outcomes; scheduling never confers business/purge authority.

Provide actual scheduling/cancellation/physical observation for archive writer, backend lifecycle and recovery owners.
Unknown/unconfigured owner entrypoints remain unavailable; do not ship fake successful jobs. Keep stopped, cancellation
requested, process exited and physical storage reclaimed separate. Leftover processes/bytes count until confirmed.
Stale workers may not acquire successor resources; integrate owner generations/leases without introducing a second
claim store. Native asynchronous data fences remain TASK-00461/00467/00471's responsibility.

and business action authority remain distinct. Use [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md)

- [ ] Real selected runtime/readers and owner workers enroll only exact authorized sources/roles with narrow access,
  fixed entrypoints and bounded resources; unknown native/security prerequisites block activation.
- [ ] Independent supervision survives ordinary collector/search failure and supplies TASK-00456's finite progress/
  status contract without duplicate health authority or relying on failed-process self-reporting.
- [ ] Scheduler/restart/cancellation/stop behavior preserves owner generations and original outcomes; no business replay,
  hidden upgrades, premature resource release or stale successor control occurs.
