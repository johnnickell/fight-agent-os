# Preserve readable prose in agent handoffs

## Outcome

Keep delegated requests and continuation handoffs readable to the human supervising them. A reported handoff
joined ordinary words and labels, making its instructions and evidence difficult to inspect. This chore adds
explicit writing guidance at the shared handoff authority and its skill callers; it does not claim to identify
or repair a renderer or transport defect.

## Scope

- Add shared readability and pre-send readback rules in [HANDOFFS.md](../../docs/engineering/HANDOFFS.md#readable-handoff-text).
- Route Build stage-request writing and Writing for Agents through that rule.
- Preserve exact technical literals, required evidence, authority boundaries and existing stage routing.
- Exclude runtime changes, automated text rewriting, global skill installation and historical evidence edits.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare a delegated request or continuation handoff | Existing skill invocation; no new command | Canonical evidence and current stage requirements | N/A: instruction-only change | Readable prose and clearly separated labels, with exact technical literals preserved |
| Shorten an overlong handoff | No new command | Relevant canonical finding or section | N/A: no runtime events | Less repetition without losing blockers, authority or uncertainty |
