# Retain held evidence and purge eligible transient content

## Outcome

Evidence required by execution, active Planning or recovery remains available, while explicitly eligible transient
content can be removed through bounded recoverable operations with truthful retained-metadata versus byte status.
Awaiting review never starts terminal cleanup.

## Scope

- In scope: Evidence category/hold/reference integration, authorized policy and hold changes, terminal grace,
  bounded eligible cleanup, original deletion recovery and safe retention/tombstone status.
- Out of scope: New transcript store, rewriting Workflow lifecycle, ordinary-user history deletion, post-publication
  worktree cleanup, arbitrary object/file removal, automatic deletion on archive and universal physical-erasure claims.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain required content | Authorized exact hold/release/extension/policy operation | Current references, owning lifecycle and policy | Committed attributed hold/policy facts | Protected metadata; release alone does not delete |
| Remove eligible transient bytes | Admit/reconcile bounded original cleanup intent | Current state/age/holds/ownership and actual storage outcome | Pending/confirmed purge with secret-free tombstone | External deletion only of exact eligible owned bytes |
| Inspect availability | N/A — current authorized read | Retained history versus actual recoverability and original outcome | N/A | Honest retained/pending/purged/uncertain state, no inferred recovery |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00377's evidence and early TASK-00374 reference/hold participants, TASK-00087's active Planning
references and TASK-00354's protected Pi lifecycle seam, transitively TASK-00167 checkpoint retention. Keep Pi
reconstruction with its owner; historical local Pi receipts do not qualify managed storage or justify log rewrites.
Workflow disposition remains with existing execution owners, not observations or cleanup workers.

Retain authoritative Workflow/grant/handoff history, Git/check summaries, usage totals and Artifact metadata as
project history through their respective owners. Hold bytes while active Planning, accepted evidence, recovery,
security or explicit references require them. Preserve active, paused, recoverable and Needs Human material plus
required predecessors/descendants. Missing telemetry, process inactivity, client detach and Awaiting review are not
terminal. Apply configurable initial 30-day post-terminal grace only to resume-only Pi material and bulky transient
observations/logs after verified disposition. Unknown time/state/participants preserves content.

Require separate current authority, exact scope, expected policy/lifecycle/references and reason where applicable
for hold release, early deletion or extension. Use shared deterministic transaction-duration fences and monotonic
lifecycle generations with actual reference/hold/resume writers. Commit state, audit and deletion intent atomically;
external effects follow after commit. Define the storage deletion window explicitly: new references cannot commit
to bytes already claimed for deletion, and late results cannot delete successor/replacement material. A query before
delete alone is not a fence. Qualify each supported cross-owner route before activation.

Reconcile original deletion identity/outcome rather than resetting work after timeout or disappearance. Distinguish
logical unavailability, requested deletion, confirmed absence and backup expiry. Preserve secret-free identity/
digest, actor or policy, time and reason. No successful claim without actual storage evidence; no reconstruction of
approvals or recoverable content from tombstones. Bound batches/time and resolve only canonical ownership-proven
references; missing hold/reference/storage coverage blocks cleanup, not the capture of protected evidence.

Ordinary retention holds never justify keeping detected prohibited content exposed. TASK-00380 owns complete
cross-copy redaction; immediate supported access denial remains enforced by storage/read owners. Future observation,
projection and export writers must join these participants before their cleanup path is enabled; no whole completed
history/browser journey becomes a reverse prerequisite of early evidence capture.
