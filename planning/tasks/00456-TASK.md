# Expose collection degradation independently of logging

## Outcome

An installation operator can distinguish healthy, absent, delayed, lost and unknown coverage when the application
logger, collector or search is unavailable. Producers can signal bounded diagnostic failure without using the failed
log path. This is approved slice B of [TICKET-00112](../tickets/00112-TICKET.md).

## Scope

- In scope: Actual installation-supervision binding, bounded private producer-failure/status path, independent
  progress observation, source/destination/queue coverage states, durable bounded loss summaries and trusted local
  inspection; contracts for later protected browser/installation consumers.
- Out of scope: New dashboard/metrics/alerting platform, raw-log store, browser authorization/UI, collector queues,
  automatic repair, another installation stack, environment dumps or runtime enrollment by implication.

Implement [WF-038's independent coverage contract](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-038-define-log-collection-and-failure-recovery.md#failure-classification-and-independent-coverage-state)
using TASK-00455's mechanism and the existing installation owner. Supervision must run independently of the collector
process and search backend. Check progress every five seconds; three missed observations become unknown/unavailable.
A quiet source is not failed solely because its last-record time is old, and a live process/tail is not coverage proof.

Distinguish pending enrollment/not captured, delayed, evicted/lost, source unavailable, malformed/incomplete assembly,
accepted but not searchable, archive unconfirmed/unconfigured, expired and owner-authorized purge. Consume only facts
known by their owners; this TASK does not infer backend visibility, archival or purge from collector health.

Use the reserved 16-MiB control/loss budget within the 5-GiB collection allocation, at most 64 recent incident summaries,
and one status operation in flight with a one-second read/write deadline. Coalesce overflow into honest aggregate
counts/ranges/detail uncertainty, not an unlimited history. Expose backlog/oldest pending age and 75%/90% pressure.
Loss must remain observable independently of the overwritten destination queue. Collector failure cannot depend on
a final self-reported incident; stale/unreadable status is unknown. Shared host/storage loss may destroy both.

Provide the actual bounded producer signal consumed by TASK-00451: component, stage, time and known delivery/loss
uncertainty, never failed raw payloads, recursion or a second spool. Status failure must not replay business work,
claim health or weaken mandatory audit/evidence behavior. Count supervisor resources in installation accounting.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Report diagnostic or delivery degradation | Enrolled bounded status update, no business retry | Known component/stage/time/uncertainty | Operational status fact, not Workflow authority | Independent bounded private state survives ordinary collector failure where qualified |
| Inspect actual coverage | N/A — trusted local read | Current source/destination/backlog/loss state and freshness | N/A | Honest bounded status without raw logs or corrective mutation |
| Detect missing observations | Existing independent supervisor operation | Actual progress and last successful observation | Degraded/unknown observation | Collector death/search outage cannot remain healthy by stale assertion |
| Account for incident overflow | Bounded aggregate update | Known counts/ranges and detail uncertainty | Operational summary only | Finite control state without silently claiming exact loss counts |

## Validation and permissions

Only enrolled producers/supervision may write their defined bounded facts. Do not trust parsed producer content as
source identity or authority. Local inspection uses existing trusted installation access; future application reads
retain current permission checks under TICKET-00113/00117 and are not added here. No browser/Agent receives a spool,
Docker socket or credential. Status is private even when payload-free; exclude secret/raw payload/path/environment
leakage. Follow ADRs [0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md); these observations do not commit Workflow state.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real supervisor and producer bindings report degradation without the failed logger, collector or search path;
  no fixture-only or collector-stderr-only health substitute is accepted.
- [ ] Five-second/three-miss freshness, quiet-source handling and each applicable coverage distinction are truthful;
  unavailable facts stay unknown rather than complete-empty or healthy.
- [ ] Status deadlines, concurrency, reserved bytes and incident aggregation are enforced; queue eviction cannot erase
  its only loss account, and status failure never recurses or dumps raw data.
- [ ] Actual local inspection remains private/read-only and does not start, repair or expand collection privileges.
- [ ] Owned state/aggregation/failure behavior and directly exercised supervisor/storage mechanisms meet the contract;
  required full `./bin/build` passes with fresh evidence and disclosed shared-fault limits.

## Verification and evidence

Test owned coverage/aggregation/freshness with controlled clocks and failures, not configuration or supervisor internals.
Directly qualify the independent process/status path, bounded I/O, journal capacity and collector-death observation
using separately authorized safe targets. Exercise simultaneous logger/status failure, stale state, quiet streams,
unknown counts and incident overflow; never use real secret data. Future queue/destination slices must provide real
facts through this same contract and include their integration evidence rather than duplicate status storage.
Run focused repository wrappers and `./bin/build`, retaining counts, timing, warnings and unverified fault boundaries.
