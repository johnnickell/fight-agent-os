# Prepare verified isolated archive-search scopes

## Outcome

A valid admitted restore produces exact older content in an isolated temporary search scope, with readiness proved
by actual content/coverage queries. This approved slice B of [TICKET-00115](../tickets/00115-TICKET.md) owns the real
verify/import worker and provider lifecycle hooks, not an import-ACK shortcut or synthetic backend demonstration.

## Scope

- In scope: Enrolled bounded worker execution, qualified private temporary provisioning/query/disposal adapters,
  archive re-verification, streaming decompression/import, original-intent recovery, exact readiness publication and
  copy/lease/restriction participation with actual physical accounting.
- Out of scope: Another object store, generic provisioning endpoint, live-tail mixing, timestamp rewriting, browser
  UI, export, automatic business replay or final scheduled retirement of expired scopes.

Consume TASK-00474's admitted exact selection/current authority, TASK-00468's immutable archive identities and
TASK-00461's private Loki integration under [WF-039](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-039-define-searchable-retention-and-verified-archives.md)
and [the backend reconciliation](../wayfinder/research/loki-integration-reconciliation.md). The qualified owning
launcher, not PHP requests/browser/Agents, receives the narrow provisioning capability. Isolate temporary scope
storage/credentials/lifecycle from live ingestion and other requesters. Old held timestamps may exceed ordinary
90-day archive age; selected verified content determines supported bounds, not the historical ten-year probe cap.

Recheck current requester/session/capability/policy before execution, source access, bounded work batches and
publication. Renew/use only qualified bounded source leases, stopping when authority/restriction changes. Read back
and verify compressed/raw digests and sizes, decompression, framing/count and provenance; actual counters reject
expansion/corruption/excess rather than trusting manifests. Preserve original observation/source/fragment IDs and
collection/emitted clocks without pipeline redaction. Do not parse-and-reserialize raw bytes and call them exact.
No import can replay commands/events or enter the live tail.

Stream within the accepted seven-day/1-GiB selection, one running worker, 512 MiB/1 vCPU, 15-minute execution and
30-second storage/import request bounds. All temporary imports/indexes/staging/leftovers fit the aggregate 4-GiB
physical allocation; backend CPU/memory/resources require additional bounded measurement. Four ready scopes plus
active work must fit the supported admission profile, not four copies of an idle benchmark. No hidden spool or
unlimited process grant. Count uncertain work until reconciled and refuse new admission at exhausted capacity.

Handle partial/rejected/uncertain imports against original identities and provider outcomes; generic 2xx/flush ACK
is not complete acceptance or query readiness. Verify actual intended content/identity/coverage in the selected
scope before ready publication. A flush, if required by the qualified path, cannot affect the live ingester.
Publish only complete verified coverage or an explicitly defined verified partial subset with exact gaps; failed,
cancelled or expired scopes stay inaccessible. Unknown ownership prevents publication. Set ready expiry once to
24 hours; recovery must not reset it. Source archive verification is not restored-query verification.

Own interrupted provision/read/import/verification/publication recovery and safe disposal hooks now, including
failed attempts and stale worker generations. Persist exact residual scope identity and pending cleanup; do not
pretend resources vanished after a timeout/disconnect. TASK-00477 adds scheduled retirement/reconciliation, not
missing safety or ordinary import recovery tests for this TASK.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual verified archives restore exact bytes/IDs/clocks beyond live lookback into private isolated scopes,
  without live ingestion/flush impact, rewritten timestamps, business replay or arbitrary provisioning access.
- [ ] Complete/partial readiness is proven by actual content/coverage queries; missing/corrupt/expanded/partial imports,
  backend lag and unknown authority cannot become silently complete success.
- [ ] A real supported full-boundary 1-GiB selection becomes query-ready within 15 minutes under measured worker,
  backend and aggregate temporary resources; timeout alone is not acceptance.
- [ ] Current owner/requester/lease/restriction races and stale worker recovery prevent republication or unsafe
  disposal; fixed expiry and original operation identity survive restart/uncertain outcomes.
- [ ] Actual disposal hooks and residual accounting remain finite; owned worker/integration tests, direct provider/
  resource qualification and full `./bin/build` pass with precise unsupported failure classes disclosed.
