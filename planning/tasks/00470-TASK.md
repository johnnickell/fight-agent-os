# Inspect authorized archive coverage and availability

## Outcome

Restore and download consumers can inspect bounded private archive coverage with their distinct current permissions
and truthful verification/availability facts. This approved slice D of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) delivers protected application/API catalog operations, not their later UI
or generic object browsing.

## Scope

- In scope: Intent-specific private catalog queries, typed source/time selection, bounded estimates, stable
  policy-bound pagination, current fetch/release authorization and safe coverage/status responses.
- Out of scope: Raw object paths/URLs, public manifests, restore admission/import, archive downloads, free backend
  queries, new browser navigation, automatic data recovery or another read-admission/authority owner.

Consume TASK-00468's verified catalog and original identity/manifest facts, TASK-00467's current lifecycle/disclosure
fences and TASK-00463's reusable fresh authority/typed-query/shared read admission. Restore catalog access requires
`READ_LOGS` + `RESTORE_LOG_ARCHIVES`; download consumers receive only their necessary bounded catalog under
`READ_LOGS` + `DOWNLOAD_LOG_ARCHIVES`, without requiring restore permission. READ_LOGS alone does not expose archive
inventory. Exact catalog IDs identify targets but never confer authority or arbitrary storage access.

Separate trusted collection clock from available event-time coverage; unknown source times cannot be asserted to
match an event-time window. Expose verified, pending, unavailable, missing, corrupt, expired/purged and unknown
coverage only within authorized scope. Verified segment counts do not prove all source records were captured or
that any restore is ready. Estimated sizes/coverage retain their basis and uncertainty; no unlimited discovery when
an estimate is missing. Necessary manifest metadata is private and minimized by query purpose.

Use bounded typed requests <=16 KiB, operations <=30 seconds and pages <=500 entries/1 MiB encoded, with actual
response/scan accounting and stable tie-safe cursors bound to purpose/scope/time/filter/policy. Share TASK-00463's
two installation-wide read/export slots and one/requester, not a separate archive pool. Reject excessive work or
return truthful bounded partial/unknown coverage; do not automatically fan out a larger selection. Recheck actual
current actor/session/capability/disclosure authority before fetch and private release; a cached request principal
or issued cursor is not enough. Unknown authority withholds content and metadata.

## Validation and permissions

Use TASK-00462's protected capabilities, never Role names or browser-only checks. Follow ADRs
[0003](../adr/0003-browser-authentication-security-profile.md) and
[0004](../adr/0004-client-authority-and-runtime-state.md): same-origin authenticated transport, applicable CSRF,
no-store and volatile generation-fenced consumers. Unauthenticated/unauthorized references must not leak existence,
counts or object metadata. Errors retain the existing safe public HTTP contract; credentials/private content/search
text do not enter URLs/history/analytics. Related owner permissions remain separate. Application query behavior
follows [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md); reads do not mutate lifecycle or certify evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real private catalog/API queries enforce the two distinct permission combinations; READ_LOGS alone, stale
  authority or forged/cross-purpose references cannot reveal hidden inventory.
- [ ] Actual catalog states, original clocks, gaps and estimates remain truthful, without treating upload success
  as verification, archive verification as restore readiness or empty results as complete capture.
- [ ] Current fetch/release checks and policy-bound tie-safe cursors survive revocation/purge/expiry races; unavailable
  authority withholds metadata and no generic storage locator is exposed.
- [ ] Bounded scan/request/page/deadline and shared admission limits hold with real catalog volume and slow/error
  paths; owned query/permission/integration tests and full `./bin/build` pass.

## Verification and evidence

Exercise actual PostgreSQL/catalog/API integration over verified/pending/corrupt/missing/unknown coverage, tied
boundaries, changed policy and concurrent permission/restriction loss. Assert response bytes, query work and shared
