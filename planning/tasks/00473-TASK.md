# Recover archive coverage without reconstructing authority

## Outcome

An authorized operator can reconcile surviving archive objects, intents and damaged/missing catalog state into
truthful recovered coverage without inventing grants, holds or purge authority. This approved slice G of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) delivers bounded local archive recovery, not host-loss backup or a proof-only
exercise. TASK-00468 retains ordinary transfer crash recovery.

## Scope

- In scope: Authorized bounded original-intent/surviving-object inventory and reconciliation, content re-verification,
  catalog repair under independently recovered current owner state, persisted progress and safe coverage/status.
- Out of scope: Reconstructing authority from manifests, arbitrary object imports, whole-index snapshots, off-machine
  backup, automatic business replay, new recovery engine or declaring all local loss recoverable.

Consume TASK-00467's actual current owner/restriction/tombstone state, TASK-00468's immutable archive/verification
contract and TASK-00471's actual lifecycle outcomes. Surviving manifests can inventory candidate content, not
establish grants, current holds or absence of purge. If authority is lost, inconsistent or unavailable, keep content
private, report coverage unknown and disable unsafe cleanup/restore publication until its owner independently
recovers it. An older catalog/index must not resurrect restricted content or create current references by inference.

Use canonical enrolled namespaces/identities and original operations, not arbitrary paths/URLs or untrusted object
metadata. Reconcile at most 100 segments or 30 seconds/pass, one pass in flight, with durable progress and bounded
requests/retries. Reuse one archive verifier and accepted staging/resource allocations; no hidden recovery spool or
unbounded scan/fanout. Re-read and verify actual compressed/raw digest/size, bounded decompression, framing/count
and original provenance before recovering catalog availability. Preserve original clocks, generations and immutable
identity; contradictory objects/catalog entries remain conflict/unknown, not overwrite permission.

Distinguish surviving verified coverage, missing content, corrupt bytes, pending intent, confirmed restriction,
uncertain deletion and physical leftovers. Reconcile original deletion/publication outcomes instead of rerunning
business work or deleting unrecognized objects as presumed orphans. Unknown owner links block unsafe disposal;
account withheld leftovers against physical limits and reduce admission when necessary. Full/read-only storage,
failed catalog, unavailable credentials/keys and irrecoverable local loss remain explicit different limits.

Expose safe operator progress/coverage through the existing installation recovery/status binding, without leaking
manifest payloads or allowing log-read permissions to repair storage. Same-host duplication/compression is not
independent backup. Local-only acceptance permits total host/storage loss; demonstrate only tested restart/storage
failure classes and separately disclose inherited versus fresh evidence.

## Validation and permissions

Use existing narrow preservation/installation recovery authority and exact expected generations. Browser log
permissions, manifest possession, terminal access and a backend credential alone are not operator business authority.
Follow ADRs [0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md): commit state/audit/intent on the canonical boundary,
perform storage reads/effects outside it and reconcile original outcomes. Required audit failure semantics remain
intact. Keep tombstones/status secret-free, credentials private and runtime state under ignored `var/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual bounded surviving-object/intent/catalog reconciliation recovers exact verified coverage with original
  identities/clocks and resumable progress, distinguishing incomplete/conflicting/missing/corrupt data honestly.
- [ ] Missing or stale grants/holds/restrictions cannot be reconstructed from manifests; unavailable authority blocks
  unsafe publication/cleanup and older state cannot resurrect purged content.
- [ ] Original deletion/publication outcomes reconcile through interruption without successor deletion, false
  availability, business replay or presumed-orphan cleanup.
- [ ] Full/read-only/corrupt/missing storage, failed catalog and unavailable keys remain safe bounded states with
  actual resource/leftover accounting and explicit local-only/host-loss limits.
- [ ] Real authorized recovery operation, owned PostgreSQL/authority/recovery tests, direct storage qualification
  and full `./bin/build` pass with exact recovered coverage, gaps and warnings recorded.
