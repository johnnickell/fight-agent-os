# Retire and reclaim temporary restore scopes safely

## Outcome

Expired, revoked, failed or cancelled temporary restore work becomes inaccessible immediately and is physically
reclaimed through bounded recoverable owner operations, without hiding leftovers or resurrecting stale workers.
This approved slice D of [TICKET-00115](../tickets/00115-TICKET.md) owns scheduled retirement/reconciliation; logical
access checks already belong to TASK-00474 and ordinary import recovery/disposal hooks to TASK-00475.

## Scope

- In scope: Bounded retirement discovery, exact expected-generation cleanup intent, source-lease/work cancellation
  participation, actual private scope/process/storage reclamation, restartable original-outcome reconciliation,
  residual accounting and safe status.
- Out of scope: Primary archive expiry, new hold/purge authority, generic process/storage administration, browser
  scope-management/sharing, implicit lifetime extension and deletion based solely on an unknown object name.

Consume TASK-00474's requester/lifecycle state, TASK-00475's exact native scope/import/disposal participants and
TASK-00467's current owner/restriction fences. Pending jobs expire after one hour, execution is bounded to 15 minutes
and ready scopes expire 24 hours after actual readiness without sliding renewal. Failed/cancelled/expired work is
inaccessible; current permission/session/policy checks deny unsafe access even when the cleanup scheduler/provider
is down. Unknown authority never permits publication or unsafe deletion. Content already released cannot be recalled.

Reconcile cleanup at least every five minutes while healthy with bounded batches/deadlines and persisted progress.
Commit exact original intent/claim/audit before external effects. Fence stale imports, lease renewals, ready publication,
reads/output and successor generations across actual asynchronous cancellation/removal. Provider cancellation/HTTP
success alone does not prove the process stopped or physical bytes disappeared. Release source leases through their
owner only when actual work can no longer use them; neither a lost worker nor automatic retry can indefinitely pin
primary archives. A temporary scope is not a preservation owner or evidence substitute.

Track logical denial, cancellation requested, worker/provider stopped, removal processed and actual physical
reclamation separately. Include temporary indexes, imports, staging, process leftovers and uncertain attempts in the
4-GiB aggregate allocation and actual job/scope admission until their disposition is established. Bound backend/
worker resources too. Refuse excess new work rather than use a hidden spool, free a slot prematurely or remove
unrelated/unrecognized data. Reconcile original operation identities after timeout/restart, never recreate expired
scopes or reset readiness age. Missing owner state leaves explicit private/unknown residuals for authorized recovery.

Native removal participates in TASK-00472's authorized restriction/purge, but consumes early TASK-00467 authority,
not that completed all-copy operation. Coordinate archive-reader leases with TASK-00471 without importing its whole
cleanup worker as a prerequisite. Restore retirement cannot delete the primary verified archive or release its holds.
Use existing independent operational status for finite pending/blocked/failed cleanup and physical pressure, not raw
payloads or a successful browser heartbeat.
