# Resolve material classification conflicts with human disposition

## Outcome

An authorized human can inspect a paused material declared-kind/Jev conflict and record a bounded,
attributed, revision-bound routing disposition without changing Planning metadata or granting execution. An
unsafe, stale or unauthorized disposition cannot be consumed by the later normal-route catalog. This owns one
independently reviewable conflict-resolution interaction, not Workflow start.

## Scope

- Present TASK-00201's safe typed recommendation, declared kind, suggested category, relevant evidence,
  uncertainty and current revisions to a human with authority to resolve the routing question. Explain the
  effect of the choice without displaying hidden TASK facts or claiming either classification is correct.
- Record the human's explicit supported category disposition and bounded reason against the original subject,
  Planning/context/eligibility, rubric/evaluator and classification revisions. The human can confirm the
  declared kind for this request, choose another permitted supported category explicitly, or revise TASK
  requirements through the separate authorized Planning path and request fresh classification. The
  disposition never silently amends TASK kind, status, scope, priority or Board order; a category choice is
  not a grant to implement changed scope or skip canonical eligibility.
- Provide a safe query/inspection path and typed outcome for [TICKET-00052](../tickets/00052-TICKET.md) to
  consume at route selection. A changed subject, evidence, authority or rubric invalidates the old choice and
  requires a new decision; no stale choice is silently reused. Keep one attributed history, not a competing
  queue. Do not make a human confirmation routine for non-conflicting normal classification or fallback.
- Out of scope: route catalog and branching configuration, TASK amendment, Workflow start/claim, emergency
  confirmation, release routing, override of denied egress and Provider/Jev correction. TICKET-00052 owns the
  authoritative final eligibility/grant/route recheck and atomic start.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resolve a material classification conflict | Record scoped human disposition | Inspect current permitted TASK, conflicting recommendation, evidence and revisions | Attributed disposition fact, not a Workflow start | A reusable *current* routing choice for later catalog validation; no TASK mutation |
| Inspect or reject an old decision | N/A — read-only inspection or refused write | Compare recorded subject/rubric/evaluator/authority revisions with live facts | Safe refusal where needed | Stale or unauthorized disposition cannot select a route or grant a start |
| Amend TASK instead | Separate authorized Planning operation, outside this TASK | Rerun classification on revised TASK as a new selection | Existing Planning lifecycle facts only from its owner | No hidden metadata edit or reuse of obsolete classification |

Semantic labels do not imply a new Planning lifecycle status, approval engine or duplicate route selector.

## Validation and permissions

Require a human principal with actual scoped disposition authority and access to the visible TASK/evidence,
not just a Triage template or a UI button. Recheck live grants and the exact conflict and subject revisions
when recording and reading a disposition. Do not expose hidden evidence to an unauthorized resolver. Limit the
choice to a supported category and scope; unsupported or contradictory TASK facts, stale recommendation,
revoked authority or an unresolved scope change stop safely. The receiving operation must revalidate eligibility,
route/catalog and claim authority independently; this disposition cannot turn a Planning-ineligible TASK
executable or authorize a hotfix based on Jev priority. Preserve idempotent history for duplicate requests
without treating the recorded fact as a permission to execute.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A materially conflicting usable classification pauses with safe reasons; only a currently authorized
  human can record an explicit category disposition, attributed to the exact recommendation and TASK facts.
- [ ] The choice never edits Planning kind/priority/status or issues a claim, start grant or emergency approval;
  ordinary non-conflicting classification does not require this extra human step.
- [ ] Stale TASK, context, eligibility, rubric/evaluator, permission or recommendation identities cannot be
  reused for routing, and conflicting duplicate decisions do not overwrite an accepted current disposition.
- [ ] The typed current outcome and redacted history are inspectable by permitted actors and consumable by
  TICKET-00052 without leaking restricted TASK evidence or adding a competing queue.

## Verification and evidence

Test owned disposition/inspection with current and stale revisions, duplicate/conflicting choices, permission
revocation, hidden evidence and explicit rerun after authorized TASK revision. Assert non-mutation of Planning
metadata and absence of Workflow/claim side effects. Verify important persistence/transaction and transport
contracts where implemented; do not test the planning records, prompts, generated views or harness wiring as
product behavior. Run focused checks and the canonical `./bin/build`, recording fresh evidence, warnings and
unimplemented consumer obligations before publishing.
