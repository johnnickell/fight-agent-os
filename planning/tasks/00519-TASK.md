### Required behavior

- Qualify Fight Common TASK-00176's current implementation as the reference. A bounded read-only delivery check
  resolves an exact MERGED PR for an already-`done` subject only after proving its repository, PR, title/subject,
  base/head, merge OID/time and local integration. Routing then continues without a routine cleanup request.
- At normal authorized Work, Land or substantive planning intake, explicitly reconcile those resolved markers,
  retain exact merge/integration evidence, roll up eligible parents and refresh generated views. Inspect and
  reconcile applicable authored current Board/Roadmap/README claims as well. No extra prompt or correction-only
  PR is required for routine retirement already covered by the invocation's authority.
- Keep ordinary checks/builds and ordinary view refresh offline. Read-only routing, Review and QA never write,
  fetch, merge or dispatch Land. Open PRs remain pending; closed-unmerged, nonterminal, unintegrated, mismatched,
  malformed or unavailable evidence retain precise holds. Do not infer acceptance from a merge or `done`.
- Repeated reconciliation is a no-op. A later merge between intake and handoff is resolved read-only, with marker
  retirement at the next authorized mutation. No background watcher, automatic merge or promise that stopped
  agents update Markdown is introduced. Historical references cease generating requests after retirement.
- Land resolves the target repository's live default branch and verifies it against local policy and the
  existing subject PR before publication. Use that bound target throughout push/PR checks; do not blindly import
  the provider's `develop` literal or trust stale `origin/HEAD`. A policy/PR conflict needs an explicit disposition.
- Land completes publication, document synchronization and eligible local resource retirement as separate
  evidenced obligations. Consume TASK-00534 and the independently qualified TASK-00537 follow-up for cleanup-only
  resumption, safe-checkout continuation after worker exit, VM-reader qualification and orphan-stack discovery.
  Never hold safe cleanup merely to obtain a metadata correction PR; never claim clean completion with held
  resources. Preserve actual active/dirty/shared resources, credentials, evidence and durable data unless its
  deletion is separately authorized. Validate target-local overrides, not only the shared skill text.

John's selection authorizes isolated adoption work; it does not itself assert independent acceptance, publication,
merge or fleet completion. Keep per-project changes scoped to this central subject and expose prerequisites.
