- Out of scope: stopping or recreating main or shared services, modifying main data or mounts, bypassing guards, manually deleting source or generated state, changing force/ref policy, or republishing [PR #324](https://github.com/johnnickell/fight-agent-os/pull/324).

Read-only macOS Docker diagnostic: `docker inspect fight-agent-os-web-1` showed parent binds at `/app` and `/app/var` and a configured read-only tmpfs at `/app/var/worktrees`. `docker exec fight-agent-os-web-1 sh -c 'grep " /app/var/worktrees " /proc/self/mountinfo; realpath /app/var/worktrees/fight-wt-6866bbeb8f01/output'` observed the covering tmpfs and a permission-denied lookup.
