# Retain exact check receipts

## Outcome

Existing handoff and Review owners receive durable attributable check results for an exact immutable subject,
including warnings and incomplete verification, without mistaking process exits or model claims for fresh evidence.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual owning check-invocation/result binding, bounded receipt capture, source/environment applicability,
  output Artifact references, contribution recording, original-result recovery and safe receipt inspection.
- Out of scope: A new executor/check scheduler, arbitrary shell endpoint, unsafe-target verification, handoff or
  acceptance decisions, seeded quality-tool failures, Review/QA verdicts and unrelated check reruns.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain an actual check | Owning execution intent/result supplies exact receipt | Current subject, qualified target, actual invocation/result and original identity | Committed verified receipt or incomplete-result facts | Durable metadata plus private safe output references |
| Inspect applicability | N/A — read-only | Exact source, target/tool identities, time, exit/outcome, warnings and limitations | N/A | Fresh/inherited/stale/missing/uncertain evidence facts, not acceptance |
| Recover missing acknowledgement | Resolve original check/receipt operation | Existing attempt/effect and verified output | Original result only when established | No blind rerun or fictional passing result |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00376's immutable subject and transitively TASK-00374 storage, TASK-00375 contribution and TASK-00140
qualified resource custody. Extend existing execution-owned check operations with early receipt participants; do
not depend on completed TASK-00141 or create another phase/process/claim engine. Check execution requires its own
current authority and safe target; evidence preservation alone grants neither shell nor provider access.

Bind receipt to original operation, Workflow/TASK revision, exact source/base/head/manifests, actual producer/role/
session, relevant context/Harness/environment/tool identities and command/check identity. Preserve bounded safe
invocation metadata, start/end and actual result/exit, verification freshness, warnings, limitations and selected
output Artifact digests. Unsafe command arguments/paths/environment are not ordinary metadata; omit or protect them
without inventing a sanitized command as proof of a different invocation. Record incomplete identity honestly.

Detect source/target changes affecting applicability across actual execution. A successful check of dirty or different
bytes cannot certify the submitted immutable subject. Missing exits, partial output, interrupted processes and
unavailable tools remain incomplete/unknown; historical/inherited receipts are labelled, never promoted to fresh.
No automatic rerun follows a missing acknowledgement or failed read. Reconcile actual original execution/effects
before any separately authorized retry through the owning operation.

Commit receipt, exact verified Artifact references, contribution facts and required audit under current expected
state/fences on one canonical PostgreSQL transaction. Storage/process effects occur outside it. Duplicate results
are idempotent only for the same original identity/payload; conflicting results fail closed. Later corrections append
attributed facts; they do not rewrite the original receipt, issue acceptance or advance a Workflow.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual bounded checks retain attributable exact invocation/subject/target/result and warnings with verified
  output references; handoff consumers can distinguish fresh evidence from inherited or incomplete receipts.
- [ ] Modified source, wrong target, forged producer, stale generations and changed payload retries cannot certify
  a different subject or produce contradictory completion.
- [ ] Interruption/uncertain outcomes preserve original attempts and do not trigger blind reruns or false passing
  results; receipt/reference/contribution/audit writes commit or roll back together.
- [ ] Recording evidence creates no Review verdict, acceptance, unsafe check authority or Awaiting review transition.
