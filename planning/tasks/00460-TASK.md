# Deliver captured logs independently to verified archives

## Outcome

Captured operational records continuously reach independent verified archives despite search failure, with
recoverable original pending work and honest confirmation state. This is approved slice F of
[TICKET-00112](../tickets/00112-TICKET.md), not a second archive/catalog implementation.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual collection-to-archive intake binding, independent destination scheduling/backlog, original-identity
  retry/reconciliation, verified receipt consumption, correct queue dispositions and independent status.
- Out of scope: Archive format/catalog/readback implementation, storage deployment, retention/purge authority,
  restore/download UI, an extra archive spool, search export as archival, re-aging or rewritten content.

Consume [WF-038](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-038-define-log-collection-and-failure-recovery.md),
[WF-039](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-039-define-searchable-retention-and-verified-archives.md) and
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md)'s early archive-intake/verified-receipt contract. The archive route is
continuous and independent of search acceptance or the day-14 index window. Preserve original identities, source/
fragment provenance and trusted collection clocks through retries; duplicate/uncertain results are reconciled,
not granted a new identity or retention age.

Use TASK-00457's existing 2-GiB archive destination queue within the same 5-GiB physical collection allocation.
There is no hidden handoff spool or second queue outside that accounting. Collector requests share the maximum two
in flight and <=1-MiB encoded batch, 3-second connection/10-second attempt and 1–60-second jittered retry profile.
The archive owner's segment/open/verification limits also apply; a stricter bound wins at the shared boundary.

Separate collector persistence, intake acknowledgement, upload, verification and catalog publication. Only the
qualified archive-owner receipt proves verified content; HEAD/ETag/upload ACK or a successful intake call does not.
Do not release the sole pending bytes based on an ambiguous/partial response. Settle queue work only with its actual
durable disposition: verified transfer where required, or independently recorded owner-authorized purge/eligible
loss, never falsely labelled archival. Verify that the receipt covers the original submitted identities/content.

Reconcile interrupted/uncertain handoff against the same original operation and preserve pending work until its
outcome is known or qualified capacity disposition applies. Archive authentication/configuration/storage failures
remain visible, with bounded retry/holding and no credential exposure. Search must continue when archive is blocked,
and archive must continue when search is blocked, subject to real shared faults and preservation pressure.

Archive-full handling reclaims only owner-eligible archives; queued-data newest-context policy cannot prematurely
delete verified archives or held sole copies. Unknown preservation/receipt authority blocks unsafe release. No
ordinary live-index retention extension is invented to conceal missing archives. Report unconfigured/unconfirmed/
failed/gapped state rather than promise older retrieval or total host/storage-loss survival.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Continuously submit pending archive data | Enrolled bounded intake operation | Original queue identities and current archive admission/result | Archive-route disposition, not Workflow acceptance | Independent pending delivery without waiting for search |
| Confirm verified handoff | Reconcile original intake/receipt | Archive owner's exact verified coverage and durable outcome | Confirmed archival disposition only where proved | Correct queue settlement without treating upload ACK as verification |
| Recover interrupted or partial intake | Retry/reconcile same original operation | Existing intent/result, pending coverage and authority | Recovery/uncertainty facts | No re-aging, changed identity, silent missing suffix or blind overwrite |
| Handle archive outage/full storage | Bounded enrolled retries and eligible queue policy | Destination pressure/status and current preservation eligibility | Explicit unconfirmed/lost/blocked state | Search/capture can progress independently within accepted finite limits |

## Validation and permissions

Only enrolled collection/archive participants exchange private data under scoped credentials and current owner
rules. Caller object paths, public URLs or producer claims cannot establish receipt/hold authority. Preserve original
bytes without pipeline redaction and keep failed payloads out of status. Browser log powers confer no storage,
queue repair, hold or purge rights. External archive effects remain outside business transactions under ADRs
[0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md); logs never become authoritative execution evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual continuous archive intake preserves original identities/bytes/provenance/clocks independently of search,
  using the existing bounded archive queue with no hidden spool or duplicate capacity allocation.
- [ ] Interrupted/partial/uncertain intake reconciles the same original operation; only qualified verified receipts
  certify archival, and pending bytes are not released on upload/intake success alone.
- [ ] Receipt mismatch, authentication/TLS failure, storage rejection/corruption and unavailable authority leave
  honest bounded pending/blocked/uncertain state without false complete coverage or unauthorized copy disposal.
- [ ] Paired outages with TASK-00459 demonstrate each destination's progress while the other is unavailable; actual
  shared resource/failure limits remain explicit and retry/pressure rules do not bypass preservation.
- [ ] Owned intake/receipt/disposition tests and direct selected archive integration evidence pass with full
  `./bin/build`; archive/catalog durability and independent operator recovery retain their separate owners.
