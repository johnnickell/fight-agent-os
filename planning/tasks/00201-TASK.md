# Classify every selected TASK with bounded Jev and safe fallback

## Outcome

For each authorized human-selected TASK, Triage calls the qualified bounded Jev capability and returns a
revision-bound, explainable category and advisory priority. An unusable judgment yields only a labeled
supported declared-kind **normal-route** fallback with unknown priority; a material mismatch pauses for human
resolution. This delivers one classification/inspection path, normally one independently reviewable PR.

## Scope

- Reuse [TASK-00200](00200-TASK.md)'s current permission-filtered selected-TASK facts and distinct Triage
  identity and [TASK-00159](00159-TASK.md)'s qualified typed Jev questions, provider access, budgets and
  redacted receipts. Check egress Permission separately from local read; ask the bounded category and priority
  question on **every** authorized selection, even if declared kind seems clear. No second semantic readiness
  evaluation, queue scan or Jev route selector.
- Own a versioned triage rubric and validated typed result: initial routing `feature`, `bugfix`, `chore` or
  `unknown`/ambiguous; advisory `low`, `medium`, `high`, `urgent` or `unknown` priority. `enhancement` refines
  `feature`; Planning `bug` maps to `bugfix` in routing vocabulary only. No TASK kind/status/order changes.
  Treat all TASK prose and Jev output as untrusted data, not instructions or authority.
- Apply deterministic reconciliation of usable category with the current supported declared kind. Pause a
  material route-changing conflict and show bounded evidence. On timeout, refusal, malformed/incomplete
  result, unavailable Jev or explicit unknown category, return a labeled declared-kind *normal-route*
  fallback only where declared facts are current and supported. Priority is unknown on fallback; never infer
  urgency/hotfix, confidence, executable status or a different kind. Denied disclosure, stale/contradictory
  facts, unsupported kind and exhausted safety limits stop rather than fall back.
- Return safe typed provenance to the selected human and downstream [TICKET-00052](../tickets/00052-TICKET.md):
  exact TASK/Planning/context/eligibility and rubric/evaluator revisions, declared and proposed category,
  priority, relevant bounded reasons and source identities, Jev or fallback outcome and staleness boundary.
  Preserve attributed redacted recommendation/failure history without persisting source bodies; it is advice,
  not a Workflow transition or claim. The receiving start must revalidate live authority and revisions.
- Out of scope: route catalog, base/branch strategy, Workflow claim/start, release-kind addition, emergency
  confirmation and human conflict disposition (owned by [TASK-00202](00202-TASK.md)). A paused conflict is a
  complete safe result until the disposition slice exists.

## Validation and permissions

Server-side policy checks current actor/Triage direct Permissions, subject scope, disclosure/egress, source,
question/time/spend and revision limits before calling Jev or disclosing a result. Jev's permission to evaluate
does not grant Planning edits or a Workflow start. Refuse a stale, denied or unsupported request before fallback;
a qualified but uncertain judgment is not deterministic Planning ineligibility. Bind result reuse and display
to exact subject and rubric/evaluator identity; do not retain sensitive TASK text in receipts. Limits are at
most the qualified shared Jev ceilings and may be tighter for Triage; do not import file-screening/tool-guard
thresholds. An urgent recommendation is advisory, never a hotfix permission or Board priority change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each valid selected TASK makes one bounded Jev classification attempt even with an obvious declared kind;
  enhancement/feature and bug/bugfix distinctions preserve Planning metadata and stable typed provenance.
- [ ] Category/priority values and unknown/ambiguous outcomes are validated; priority remains advisory and
  cannot reorder the Board. A usable route-changing conflict is visible and pauses without silent rerouting.
- [ ] Unavailable, timed-out, invalid, refused and unknown Jev classifications use only a clearly labeled
  supported normal-kind fallback with unknown priority; unsupported/missing/inconsistent kind stops. No urgent
  fallback becomes a hotfix.
- [ ] Stale revisions, revoked Permission, failed egress authorization, disallowed facts or exhausted limits
  cannot dispatch Jev or yield a fresh reusable recommendation; source text/secrets stay out of receipts.
- [ ] A result conveys enough safe provenance for later catalog selection and human explanation without
  starting a Workflow or inventing a second eligibility operation.
