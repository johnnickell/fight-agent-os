# Request inspect and recover protected proposals

## Outcome

An authorized caller requests and inspects a protected typed proposal and safely stops or recovers its original
attempt. This bounded Harness capability removes TASK-00302's reusable proposal-contract blocker independently
of database Planning cutover, final browser Planning or template publication, normally in one PR.

## Scope

- In scope: Exact proposal intent and consumer validation boundary; durable candidate/result references, safe
  status, cancellation and original-attempt recovery through existing conversation/provider owners.
- Out of scope: Another queue, conversation/transcript store, provider client, Planning mutation, template schema,
  Feature policy, editor/publisher, browser draft persistence or automatic regeneration.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Request a proposal | Explicit bounded proposal admission composed with original submission | Current target/new-artifact intent, generation, authority, snapshots and limits | Committed proposal intent facts | Recoverable exact request before external work |
| Inspect a candidate | N/A — authorized read | Original attempt, typed result, provenance and uncertainty | N/A | Inert candidate, never accepted content or a new provider request |
| Stop or recover | Existing original-attempt stop/reconciliation with proposal correlation | Current owner, cancellation, outcome and safe next action | Confirmed owning lifecycle facts | Original result or truthful hold without duplicate paid work |

## Validation and permissions

Bind intent to initiating human, distinct acting Agent, Team/holder, Repository/target or exact new-artifact intent,
operation, base revision, draft/selection generation, conversation/submission/attempt, immutable snapshots and
requested provider/model. Preserve reported model/usage as attributed observations. Do not infer authority from
profile text, schema validity, selected model or a claimed approval. Require current independent propose/read,
Agent delegation and provider disclosure/budget authority; reuse TASK-00344/00345 through TASK-00351.

Use TASK-00349's actual transaction participants and one attempt/result authority; proposal-specific state extends
that ownership with exact references rather than duplicating dispatch/claims. Commit required intent/audit before
external work on the shared connection. No nested transactions, provider calls in transactions, event-only recovery
or credential-delivery queue reuse. Actual stop/process reconciliation remains TASK-00353's responsibility.

Bound envelope and payload sizes/collections; require exact consumer/schema identity and strict whole-result
validation. Consumers own their typed payload/domain rules: TASK-00302 retains prompt/complete Permission-set
validation, while TASK-00357 owns Planning candidates. Reject malformed, partial, unsupported or mismatched output,
not salvage it into success. Return protected inert data with safe provenance, never executable policy or raw
provider errors, secrets or hidden reasoning. Status/result reads neither call models nor repair/replay mutations.

Duplicate admission and lost responses resolve original identities. Changed draft/base/selection, cancellation,
revocation and stale owners fence result adoption; an attributable late result may remain protected history but
cannot overwrite human edits, attach to another draft, revive work or grant acceptance. Unknown liveness, billing
or effects retain limits and bounded needs-human state. Explicit regeneration is new admission, not uncertain-call
replay. Disconnect is not cancellation. Reuse protected retention/access owners rather than inventing cleanup.
