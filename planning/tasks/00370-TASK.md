# Deliver and reconcile original Workflow dispatch intents

## Outcome

A committed Workflow dispatch intent reaches a dedicated PostgreSQL Messenger Agent transport recoverably, while
duplicate/lost delivery, queued cancellation and failed-queue replay preserve the same authoritative Workflow.
Transport never becomes a second claim, lease or transition authority.

## Scope

- In scope: Qualified Fight Common asynchronous-command integration, dedicated Agent/failed transports, bounded
  durable-intent discovery/reconciliation, original delivery correlation and safe dispatch/hold diagnostics.
- Out of scope: New Start/outbox authority, replacement credential/Jev queues, consumer execution, automatic
  Workflow retries, provider calls, alternate broker service and browser controls.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deliver committed intent | Existing original dispatch reconciliation/send operation | TASK-00139 intent, original delivery and current disposition | Owning confirmed dispatch facts only | Workflow-ID command delivered at least once after business commit |
| Reconcile uncertain delivery | Explicit bounded original-intent reconciliation | Durable original result and transport observations | Confirmed original delivery/hold facts | Retry only where safe; no new Workflow or claim |
| Inspect or replay failed transport | Read-only status; authorized replay through existing recovery boundary | Current Workflow/lease/grant and original failure | Recovery owner facts, not queue-derived transitions | Cancelled/terminal work is a no-op; unresolved recovery stays held |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00139's committed Workflow/grant/claim/idempotency/dispatch intent. Use the exact selected Fight Common
asynchronous-command contracts and PostgreSQL Doctrine Messenger integration; directly qualify installed support,
serialization, acknowledgement and keepalive semantics. Unsupported package contracts require explicit owner
resolution, never vendor patches or an invented alias. Dispatch can queue while no Runner is available and does not
require completed enrollment, consumer execution, role journeys or browser Planning.

Use deterministic bounded discovery and recover required work without relying on a post-commit callback. Sender
state, audit and required intent share the canonical connection; transport interaction occurs after commit with
original correlation. At-least-once delivery is honest: no external exactly-once promise or second idempotency key
that creates another Workflow. Queue rows and failed transport placement are observations, not semantic completion.

Every effect requires authenticated system/operator scope and current intent authority. Payloads carry bounded
Workflow identity and approved metadata, never credentials, grants-as-bearer-tokens, raw transcripts or arbitrary
serialized executable objects. Missing/stale/malformed data denies safely. Protect queue access separately from
Agent tools; do not expose a general message publisher or unrestricted failed-queue CLI to Agents.

Cancelled-before-delivery work becomes an idempotent no-op after owner revalidation. Delayed/repeated delivery
cannot recreate claims, reset attempts, bypass disabled admission or reopen terminal Workflows. Failed-queue replay
must consult the same owning recovery/lease/authority operation; unknown process/effect state stays Needs Human.
Keep polling, backoff, reconciliation batches and transport resources finite. Safe queries never replay or repair.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Committed original intents survive sender interruption and absent immediate callbacks; lost send/ack results
  reconcile to the same Workflow without another grant, TASK claim or dispatch authority.
- [ ] Duplicate delivery and delayed queued cancellation preserve authoritative results; failed-queue replay cannot
  bypass current recovery, gates, limits or terminal state.
- [ ] Actual PostgreSQL Agent and failed transports are separated from ordinary work; keepalive and delivery facts
  never replace the application lease or imply execution while no compatible Runner exists.
- [ ] Bounded authorized diagnostics expose safe pending/failed/uncertain reasons without secrets or mutation.
