# Expire eligible live and archived copies safely

## Outcome

The installation reclaims genuinely eligible live/archive data through bounded owner-fenced operations while
preserving protected copies and reporting actual deletion progress. This approved slice E of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) implements ordinary expiry, not exposure-response purge or a storage TTL.

## Scope

- In scope: Current logical eligibility, bounded cleanup discovery/admission, durable exact deletion intent,
  asynchronous Loki/private-object deletion participation, outcome/reclamation reconciliation and capacity status.
- Out of scope: New hold authority, autonomous backend/bucket age TTL, queue eviction policy, restore expiry worker,
  arbitrary storage cleanup, emergency retention cuts and claims that API deletion means physical erasure.

Use TASK-00467's current ownership/eligibility, TASK-00468's verified receipts/catalog, TASK-00469's effective policy
and TASK-00461's native backend lifecycle adapter under
[WF-039](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-039-define-searchable-retention-and-verified-archives.md). Keep trusted original
collection clocks across retries/late ingestion. Ordinary 14-day live expiry and 90-day verified-archive eligibility
are independent, subject to accepted configuration and current holds/grace. Mixed segments use latest/strictest
eligibility; do not discard protected members to reclaim space.

Logical query expiry is distinct from physical cleanup. Before releasing a protected last staged/searchable copy,
verify a currently owned surviving alternative; unknown verification/reference/authority blocks affected disposal.
Ordinary unheld live expiry need not wait indefinitely for an archive outage: expose the gap and retain surviving
bounded pending copies. Verified archives remain protected through their own eligibility. TASK-00457's newest-context
queue eviction never authorizes early archive deletion. Held-only pressure remains finite and visible, not an
unlimited reserve or justification for unsafe purge.

Persist exact operation, target versions/generations, current owner claims and required audit before external
effects. Actual hold/release/policy/purge/restore-lease/late-ingest participants must fence the full asynchronous
storage/backend deletion window. New references cannot commit to an already-deleting copy; stale workers cannot
remove successors. A short database lock, precheck or provider cancellation interval alone does not establish this.
Qualify concrete supported mechanisms; if unavailable, keep unsafe cleanup disabled and return the conflict to its
owner rather than substituting unbounded retention or weaker preservation.

Use bounded passes of at most 100 segments or 30 seconds, one pass in flight, with persisted progress and bounded
provider requests under the applicable archive/backend profile. Track requested, processed, confirmed absence and
physical reclamation separately, including object versions, delete markers and compaction/vacuum overhead. Count
unreclaimed storage against actual physical allocations. Reconcile original operations after timeout/restart; no
blind deletion by reused key or fabricated completion. Report used/eligible/pinned bytes and 75%/90% pressure via
the existing status owner without exposing payloads. Runtime recovery cadence/enrollment belongs to TICKET-00117.

## Validation and permissions

Enrolled cleanup uses existing preservation/installation authority, not log reading or Role-name shortcuts. Only
canonical owned objects/backend scopes are targets; no caller paths, raw LogQL or arbitrary delete endpoint. Consume
TASK-00379's owner policy through TASK-00467/00469 without duplicating it. Follow ADRs
[0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md): canonical state/audit/intent transaction, then
provider effect and original-outcome reconciliation. Keep secret-free tombstones/status; ordinary cleanup neither
redacts the pipeline nor certifies Workflow/evidence completion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Original-clock live/archive expiry, mixed-member eligibility, holds/grace and protected alternatives govern
  actual cleanup; archive outage cannot conceal ordinary gaps or prematurely delete verified archives.
- [ ] Real PostgreSQL and asynchronous backend/storage races with hold/policy/purge/restore/late ingestion cannot
  cause unsafe deletion, stale successor removal or accepted dangling references.
- [ ] Unknown authority or missing participation blocks unsafe effects, with finite admission and visible held-only
  pressure rather than autonomous TTL, unbounded storage or emergency policy changes.
- [ ] Actual object versions/backend indexes and physical reclamation reconcile under original operations; requested,
  processed, absent and reclaimed remain distinct through restart, read-only/full storage and lost responses.
- [ ] Owned lifecycle/race/recovery tests, direct provider qualification and full `./bin/build` pass with exact
  supported participant inventory, pressure/physical evidence and unsupported routes disclosed.

## Verification and evidence

Exercise real owner participants with controlled clocks/independent PostgreSQL connections and actual Loki/private
storage effects on separately authorized isolated targets. Include mixed held/unheld segments, sole copies,
verified alternatives becoming unavailable, late writes, restore leases, concurrent policy changes and asynchronous
delete interruption. Directly qualify native delete/version/compaction behavior and physical accounting; do not
product-test configuration, migrations or provider internals. Run focused wrappers and full `./bin/build`; record
before/after identities, bytes, current ownership, measured reclamation, warnings and uncertainty. A successful
small deletion probe cannot certify all-copy protection or complete physical erasure.
