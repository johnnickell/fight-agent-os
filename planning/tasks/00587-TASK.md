# Deliver local Sync from accepted TASK to refreshed PR

## Outcome

Deliver one usable `/skill:sync TASK-NNNNN` workflow that integrates current `origin/develop` into a selected,
previously accepted TASK branch and automatically refreshes its existing PR through Land. Reuse Engineer and
Land roles without routine Review/QA reruns for integration-only changes. This TASK owns the complete outcome
and one independently reviewable implementation PR under [TICKET-00137](../tickets/00137-TICKET.md) and
[EPIC-00024](../epics/00024-EPIC.md).

## Scope

- In scope: Discoverable Sync skill/coordinator; real coordinator/child role binding; accepted-subject and
  existing-PR intake; fresh sequential Engineer and Land contexts through existing qualified dispatch;
  fetch/merge and intent-preserving conflict resolution; authoritative planning regeneration; normal build
  execution/reuse; automatic non-force existing-PR update; ordinary Land verification/cleanup; concrete stop,
  interruption and partial-effect handling; participating instructions and focused discovery/documentation;
  direct native/workflow qualification and independent acceptance of Sync itself.
- Out of scope: Routine Review/QA stages for integration-only input TASKs; unrelated repairs, new behavior or
  requirement changes in those TASKs; rebase/history rewriting or force-push; PR approval/merge or replacement
  of an ended PR; release/deployment, remote-branch deletion or other-TASK sweeps; managed/database/browser
  expansion or default-checkout synchronization; a parallel role launcher, execution engine, resource registry,
  permanent delivery queue or post-merge planning chore; global installation/profile changes without separate
  operator authority.

- [ ] **AC-07 — Recovery and cleanup:** Blocked/interrupted/partial cases inspect actual outcomes before retry,
  preserve completed effects and avoid duplicate dispatch/publication. Reuse normal eligible cleanup and verify
  its postconditions; publication, cleanup, protected-resource holds and human merge remain distinct with finite

- [ ] **AC-08 — Complete implementation:** Align actual participating instructions and discovery/docs with this
