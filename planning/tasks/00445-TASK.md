# Record independent managed Reviews

## Outcome

Continue an eligible Awaiting review Workflow into a fresh independent Senior Engineer assessment and retain an
immutable, attributable Review. This supplies TICKET-00083's first managed review path, not another local skill.

## Scope

- In scope: Same-Workflow review admission, exact subject validation, fresh isolated reviewer dispatch/recovery,
  immutable Review identity, ordered findings and criterion dispositions, finalization, bounded inspection and
  append-only annotations through shared application operations and terminal/API adapters.
- Out of scope: Implementation repair, semantic acceptance, QA, publication, generic session/evidence storage,
  another Workflow engine and the named Team integration owned by TASK-00267.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admit independent review | Intent-specific review-phase admission on the existing Workflow | Current handoff, authority, contributors, subject and original attempt | Committed review admission/hold facts | One fresh qualified Senior Engineer session |
| Finalize assessment | Record findings/dispositions and finalize exact Review | Criteria, checks, immutable evidence and current expected revisions | Append-only Review/finding/finalization facts | Attributable verdict, never automatic acceptance |
| Inspect or annotate | Attributed append-only annotation; reads are queries only | Exact Review, subject manifest, findings and annotations | Annotation/supersession facts only | Preserved original bytes and inspectable corrections |

## Validation and permissions

Consume EPIC-00008's exact-subject and independence rules. Preserve Workflow/TASK identity, claim, grants,
implementation branch/worktree and session lineage. Bind Review to TASK revision, configured base, full base/head
OIDs, complete changed-tree/status manifests, selected ignored Artifact digests, checks, criteria and reviewer
Agent/session. Re-read authoritative facts at admission and finalization; branch names, mutable diffs, model claims
and process exits cannot substitute. Reject stale, partial, superseded, unavailable or ambiguous evidence.

Require a distinct authenticated reviewer with live direct Permissions intersected with current delegated scope,
Repository/context/profile and resource limits. Use TASK-00264's guard and TASK-00250's immutable role session.
The reviewer cannot have contributed to design, implementation or submitted acceptance evidence; unknown history
holds. Every attempt starts a fresh conversation, including re-review by the same Agent. Permit target reads and
qualified verification plus Review-owned reporting only, never target repair or source-host credential access.

Findings have stable identity, severity, affected requirement/criterion, traceable evidence, required disposition
and blocking classification. Preference without an accepted requirement/invariant violation cannot block. Conflicts,
scope expansion, missing required evidence and exceptions enter Needs Human. Finalized bytes/events are immutable;
corrections are separate attributed annotations, and acceptance-affecting corrections invoke the acceptance owner.

Persist state/audit/intent atomically under ADR 0002 before external dispatch. Duplicate or interrupted operations
reconcile their original identity before another session/effect. Paused, cancelling and uncertain work retain
claims/evidence under the existing lifecycle; no missing heartbeat establishes quiescence. Protected session
content remains with Harness owners, not Review records or Dashboard transcripts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One actual eligible Awaiting review subject receives a non-contributing fresh Senior Engineer assessment
  without replacing its Workflow, claim, grant history or implementation evidence.
- [ ] Complete immutable subject and contribution checks reject stale/partial subjects, unknown independence,
  credential/role reuse and target repair; re-review starts a new conversation.
- [ ] Finalized findings, dispositions and verdict remain immutable and inspectable; annotations never rewrite
  history or transition acceptance by themselves.
- [ ] Duplicate admission/finalization, authority races, interruption and uncertain launch preserve one original
  attempt/result or explicit hold, not duplicate assessment or fictional success.
