# Retain and narrowly redact protected conversation content

## Outcome

Authorized lifecycle/retention operations preserve needed conversation recovery and selectively remove eligible
protected content with truthful availability, redaction and deletion status. Closing a client or abandoning a
journey never becomes blanket permission to delete history.

## Scope

- In scope: Protected terminal disposition/retention/hold operations, configured grace, bounded eligible cleanup,
  prohibited-content redaction, original deletion recovery and safe availability/tombstone inspection.
- Out of scope: New transcript/Artifact store, Planning history purge/cutover, general file deletion, Workflow
  lifecycle, checkpoint-policy duplication, ordinary-user permanent history deletion and backup-erasure promises.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain terminal/recoverable state | Authorized disposition/hold/extension operation | Actual lifecycle, exact references, policy and current authority | Committed disposition/hold facts | Protected retention metadata, not automatic deletion |
| Remove eligible owned content | Narrow authorized original cleanup intent | Revalidated terminal age, holds, references and actual storage owner | Confirmed cleanup/tombstone or pending facts | Only eligible owned bytes removed through supported lifecycle operations |
| Redact prohibited content | Narrow authorized exact-content redaction | Actual copies/projections and supported deletion/backup scope | Secret-free attributed correction/redaction facts | Inaccessibility/removal within verified capability; no retained prohibited payload as audit |
| Inspect retained availability | N/A — current authorized read | Safe metadata, retained/purged/uncertain status and original outcome | N/A | Honest recoverability without reconstruction or cleanup as a read side effect |
