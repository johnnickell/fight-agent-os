# Preserve pending deliveries within bounded hold-aware queues

## Outcome

Pending operational logs survive qualified restarts within finite storage, while exhaustion favors newer eligible
context without deleting protected sole copies or disguising loss as delivery. Search and archive pending work stay
independent. This is approved slice C of [TICKET-00112](../tickets/00112-TICKET.md).

## Scope

- In scope: Selected native persistent destination queues and raw/rejected holding, durable pending/settled
  dispositions, restart recovery, qualified oldest-eligible eviction, physical allocation, preservation fences and
  independent loss reporting through TASK-00456.
- Out of scope: A custom queue/plugin/serializer, source discovery, backend/archive implementation, invented holds,
  replacing authoritative evidence, automatic operator backfill and unbounded memory/DLQ fallback.

Apply [WF-038's finite profile](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-038-define-log-collection-and-failure-recovery.md#initial-finite-profile):
2 GiB per destination and at most 4 GiB ordinary queued data; 256 MiB raw/rejected holding; 5 GiB enforced total
physical collection allocation including overhead/checkpoints/temporary files and the shared 16-MiB control reserve.
Do not allocate a second archive spool. Count aggregate 512-MiB/1-vCPU dedicated collection resources with the reader;
logical payload limits and monitored free space do not prove physical enforcement.

Keep original receipt/fragment/source identity and trusted collection clocks in durable pending state. Native
persistence/disposition ordering must support TASK-00458's safe checkpoint progression; a one-second configured
flush cadence is not an unconditional RPO or per-record fsync receipt. Recover uncertain surviving work after restart
with original identities, and expose corrupt/lost/read-only storage honestly.

At pressure, report 75%/90% thresholds and evict oldest eligible pending entries/chunks by destination queue age.
Qualify actual native granularity and counts; neither global event-time order nor exact per-record loss is assumed.
Persist independent loss dispositions before treating eviction as settled, and prevent replay from repeatedly
refilling the queue with deliberately evicted old data. One destination's settled/evicted route cannot erase the
other destination's still-required copy. Physical reclamation is separate from removal of a route/reference.

Consume current preservation ownership/fences. Protected sole copies, active/recoverable material, holds and grace
are ineligible for routine eviction without a verified surviving alternative or separate owner authorization.
Unknown authority disables unsafe disposal. A full protected-only queue remains bounded and visibly blocks/adversely
affects incoming coverage; do not promise unlimited preservation plus unlimited new output. Never evict verified
archives early or turn queue acknowledgement into release of authoritative evidence.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain captured pending data | Enrolled bounded native queue admission | Current capacity, original identity and durable state | Pending disposition, not business acceptance | Recoverable per-destination work within the physical budget |
| Resume after interrupted persistence/delivery | Native reconciliation of original operation | Surviving pending/settled dispositions | Recovery fact with explicit uncertainty | Retry original work without invented confirmation or checkpoint-ahead gaps |
| Admit newer context at exhaustion | Qualified oldest-eligible eviction | Current owner fence/eligibility and verified alternatives | Independent durable loss disposition | Newer eligible data can progress; protected sole copies remain intact |
| Handle protected-only or unavailable storage | No unsafe deletion or unlimited fallback | Current capacity/preservation and known loss risk | Blocked/degraded/unknown status | Finite resource use with honest incoming gaps and owner escalation |

## Validation and permissions

Queue/holding/checkpoint files remain installation-private and unwritable by managed projects/Agents. Operators
alone repair/replay storage; automatic retries stay inside enrolled capability. Validate native framing/actual
sizes and trusted provenance, not producer-supplied identity/hold claims. Preservation consumes existing owner
participants, not a query-only precheck or copied hold database. Follow ADRs
[0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md); logs do not establish Workflow/evidence completion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Native queues/holding enforce all aggregate physical/resource bounds with no hidden spool or unlimited fallback;
  both destinations retain independent pending/settled state.
- [ ] Restart at persistence/disposition boundaries recovers exact surviving data/original identities or reports
  explicit corruption/loss uncertainty; checkpoint consumers cannot treat mere admission as durable persistence.
- [ ] Actual exhaustion demonstrates oldest-eligible order/granularity, independent durable loss, no replay refill
  loop and no accidental deletion of another destination's pending copy.
- [ ] Current owner-fenced hold/grace/verified-alternative races and unavailable authority prevent protected sole-copy
  eviction; protected-only pressure remains finite and visible without premature archive/evidence deletion.
- [ ] Owned eligibility/disposition integration and direct native persistence/physical exhaustion qualification pass,
  with real owner participants rather than fixture-only preservation claims and full `./bin/build` evidence.

## Verification and evidence

Use direct owning tools for native queue/disk enforcement, crash recovery and actual occupancy; use owned behavior
and important owner integration tests for dispositions, eligibility/fences and concurrency. Do not product-test
configuration, filesystem quota tooling or collector internals. Controlled non-sensitive corpus includes duplicate
legitimate messages, malformed/binary fragments, mixed protected/ordinary chunks and separate destination routes.
State crash/storage fault classes actually tested; no power-loss or host-loss guarantee follows from SIGKILL alone.
Run focused repository checks and `./bin/build`, recording source identities, byte/count granularity, warnings and
uncertainty. All destructive probes require separately approved owned targets and finite cleanup authority.
