- [ ] Run two TASKs concurrently with separate source, test databases, temporary storage, credentials, processes and artifacts; demonstrate cross-access denial.

- [ ] Demonstrate stale-owner/message/write fencing, snapshot/check-result invalidation, bounded retries, pause/cancel and safe owned cleanup; preserve failed evidence and human-owned resources.

- [ ] Close/reopen the terminal and restart the owning Harness during queued input, a pending human answer and

  client disconnect alone never reports cancellation. Browser qualification remains with its later owners.
- [ ] Record remaining platform limitations and repeatability instructions, with no alternate runner, certification endpoint or product-suite tests of infrastructure.
