# Drain or halt Runner admission explicitly

## Outcome

An authorized operator can separately Disable or Halt one Runner or the installation, inspecting committed gate
state and actual drain/pause progress. Gates persist through restart and apply to later enrollment without equating
pause requests, credential revocation or transport loss with confirmed quiescence.

## Scope

- In scope: Four intent-specific gate operations, expected-state concurrency, durable safe-pause intents through
  existing Workflow owners, controlled gate reopening, console/application status and operator guidance.
- Out of scope: Another Workflow pause/cancel engine, implicit cancellation or credential revocation, force-release,
  process-control bypass, browser start/control surface, deletion and post-publication cleanup.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drain one or all | Disable Runner / Disable All with exact authority and revision | Current gate, active work and original outcome | Committed gate/audit facts | New admission stops; existing work drains without cancellation |
| Halt one or all | Halt Runner / Halt All plus owning safe-pause intent | Current active ownership, pending pauses and uncertainty | Committed gate/intent facts; actual pauses use Workflow events | Close admission and request safe pauses, not fictional quiescence |
| Inspect or reopen admission | Read-only status; separately authorized exact gate reopening | Current applicable gates, capability and recovery holds | Gate-change facts only when committed | Opening one gate cannot bypass another gate or resume paused work implicitly |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00369's shared admission/gate participants and TASK-00141's actual Workflow execution controls, including
TASK-00140 custody transitively. Use existing enrollment/lifecycle authority, current intent-specific operator rights,
exact installation/Runner targets and expected revisions. No role name, shell access or cached preview is authority.

Commit gate state, audit and complete required pause intent on one canonical PostgreSQL transaction; perform process
or transport effects afterward through existing owners. Serialize gates with admission and configuration/enrollment
writers. Installation-wide gates apply to any later Runner and cannot be undone by enabling one Runner. Repeated or
lost-response operations reconcile the original outcome; stale confirmations and changed-away/back generations deny.

Disable drains active work. Halt additionally requests pause through the same grant/claim/lease/custody operations,
retaining resources, limits and history. An accepted Halt acknowledges durable intent, not stopped children. Safe
status distinguishes requested, pending, paused, failed and uncertain outcomes. In-flight effects and unreachable
hosts remain outstanding; never infer stop from heartbeat loss, force-release claims or reset attempts.

Credential rotation/revocation and de-registration remain TASK-00368 operations, not synonyms for Halt. Pause,
cancel, resume, takeover, failure and cleanup retain Workflow semantics. Reopening admission does not renew grants,
resume paused work, bypass authority holds or permit terminal Workflows to reopen. No automatic service mutation
from status queries and no broad process/signal endpoint for Agents.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All four controls retain distinct committed gate and operational outcomes; Disable does not cancel and Halt
  requests existing safe pauses without claiming unobserved quiescence.
- [ ] Controlled admission versus gate changes in both winner orders cannot admit stale work; restart and later
  enrollment honor installation-wide gates independently of per-Runner enablement.
- [ ] Duplicate/lost acknowledgements preserve one original result and pause intent; unavailable hosts and uncertain
  effects remain visible with retained claims/resources and a concrete authorized next action.
- [ ] Gate reopening, security lifecycle and Workflow recovery remain separate explicit operations under current rights.

## Verification and evidence

bounded production drain/Halt/pause on separately authorized work, recording original effects, child custody and

safe console examples, current-authority denials, operator restart/recovery guidance, focused checks and
