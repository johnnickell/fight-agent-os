# Record attributable contribution provenance

## Outcome

Existing eligibility, role and Review owners can inspect attributable design, implementation and submitted-evidence
contributions without treating missing history, different names or new sessions as proof of independence.

## Scope

- In scope: Protected contribution recording, source/subject lineage and completeness facts, expected-state/fenced
  writer participation, bounded authorized inspection and append-only attributed corrections.
- Out of scope: Reviewer eligibility policy/verdicts, session creation, phase engine, raw conversation ingestion,
  invented complete history, arbitrary contributor declarations and final role/Workflow journeys.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Record an actual contribution | Owning producer records exact actor/source/subject provenance | Current assignment/Workflow, source revision, original result | Committed attributable contribution facts | Durable metadata and required audit, not a verdict or grant |
| Inspect lineage | N/A — bounded read | Exact TASK/source/subject references, actor lineage and coverage/unknown state | N/A | Safe facts for existing independence owners |
| Correct attribution | Authorized expected-state append-only correction | Original fact, correction basis and current source scope | Attributed correction/supersession fact | Preserved secret-free history and visible changed applicability |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00139's real Workflow/grant identities and TASK-00348's early assignment/conversation binding, never
completed TASK-00141 or TASK-00250 role integration. Manual contributions retain their own assignment identity;
no fabricated Workflow or execution session is required. This supplier does not launch or certify a producer.

Bind facts to authenticated actual actors, available Team/role/assignment/session provenance and exact approved
TASK/source revisions or immutable content references. Pre-implementation design evidence need not invent a future
commit; TASK-00376 later binds exact Git subjects to the retained source lineage. Validate references with their
owners instead of trusting caller-supplied Agent names, Git authors, model claims or unsigned source labels.
Unknown source provenance/coverage stays unknown and cannot establish non-contribution.

Provide real transaction participants for owning design/build/evidence writers and independence/admission readers.
Use current authority, complete expected-state and deterministic shared subject/contributor generation fences on
one canonical PostgreSQL transaction with required audit/intent. A contribution racing admission must not silently
escape the owner's current independence check. Later discovered facts trigger the existing owner revalidation path,
not a verdict/acceptance change inside this supplier. External observations remain outside business transactions.

Retain original operation identity so duplicates, changed payload retries and uncertain commits cannot fabricate
another fact or erase a contributor. Corrections append actor/policy, reason and source references without rewriting
history or silently transferring an assessment to a changed subject. Actual contributing writers must participate;
unsupported/missing source coverage remains explicit and holds independence-dependent use. No read repairs history.

Expose only authorized bounded metadata, not private design bodies, source transcripts, hidden reasoning, unrelated
TASKs or credentials. Fresh sessions/new credentials do not erase prior design/build/evidence contributions.
TASK-00264 and Review owners retain the policy deciding disqualification and fresh-session requirements.
