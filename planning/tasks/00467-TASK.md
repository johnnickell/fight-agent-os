# Bind operational logs to preservation and disclosure authority

## Outcome

Collection, archive and search owners can obtain current original-clock eligibility and participate in actual
preservation/disclosure fences without inventing another hold authority. This approved slice A of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) supplies early operational-log lifecycle behavior, not the final cleanup
worker or a document-only promise that later consumers will prevent races.

## Scope

- In scope: Operational-copy identity/owner registration, current eligibility and verified-alternative validation,
  original-clock policy consumption, reference/hold/restriction generation participation, bounded read/disposal
  admission and recoverable original intent/outcome integration with existing authority.
- Out of scope: Another Artifact per log, copied Workflow/hold authority, log ingest/queue implementation, policy
  administration, final backend deletion, archive writer, generic fencing framework or new actor permissions.

Consume [WF-039](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-039-define-searchable-retention-and-verified-archives.md) and
[TASK-00374](00374-TASK.md)'s early reference/hold participants. Bind actual current owner state, including active,
paused, recoverable and Needs Human material, applicable configurable 30-day post-terminal grace and explicit holds.
Awaiting review, missing telemetry or process inactivity is not terminal disposition. Unknown owner/time/reference
state denies unsafe disposal and private publication; finite admission still applies.

Ordinary live/archive defaults are independently 14/90 days from trusted original collection time. Keep event time
separate and flagged when absent/malformed; retry, import and replacement never re-age records. Mixed segments use
the strictest/latest member eligibility unless an owner-authorized verified split preserves protected members.
Logical live-query expiry differs from byte purge. Ordinary unheld live expiry can expose an archive gap rather
than extending the index indefinitely; a protected last staged/searchable copy needs an independently verified,
currently owned surviving alternative before release. A submitted digest or search ACK alone is not that proof.

Implement actual transaction participants and monotonic lifecycle/restriction state consumed by copy writers,
readers and deletion claimants. Bind bounded leases/expected generations and original operations without external
effects inside transactions. Claims, new references, hold/release/purge/policy changes and late publication must
agree on the deletion/disclosure window. A query-only precheck or an asynchronous cancellation opportunity is not
a fence. Unsafe routes stay unavailable until their actual adapter participates; this early owner does not certify
Loki/storage/native-queue behavior on their behalf. Establish early denial/tombstone consumption before exposure,
not only when TASK-00472's complete cross-copy purge integration is delivered.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Register an owned operational copy | Exact owner-bound reference/lifecycle participation | Current identity, source clocks, category and owner generation | Committed owned copy/reference facts | Recoverable association, not new Workflow or Artifact authority |
| Decide eligible disclosure/disposal | Bounded admission/claim where required | Current policy, holds, restriction, leases and verified alternatives | Committed claim facts only for mutations | Allowed exact scope or explicit blocked/unknown state |
| Race a hold or late copy with cleanup | Existing owner transition plus shared participant | Expected lifecycle/reference generation | Existing owner facts | No stale claim, dangling accepted reference or prohibited republication |
| Reconcile an uncertain operation | Resolve original claim/outcome | Surviving owner state and exact operation | Attributable reconciliation facts | No fabricated grant, alternative verification or successor deletion |

## Validation and permissions

Accept only enrolled copy owners and canonical exact references; producer-supplied hold claims, caller paths and
possession of a log ID confer nothing. Existing [TICKET-00094](../tickets/00094-TICKET.md) owns preservation/privacy;
log read/restore/export/download rights do not confer policy, hold, purge or storage powers. Do not infer operator
business authority from terminal access. Follow ADRs [0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md)
and [0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md): inward policy, one canonical transaction for
state/audit/intent, external effects later and original-outcome reconciliation. Keep raw payloads out of lifecycle
status/audit/tombstones. Ordinary content is preserved without pipeline redaction.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real existing reference/hold/restriction participants govern operational copies; no parallel hold database,
  fabricated Workflow state or permanent Artifact per record is introduced.
- [ ] Original-clock defaults, protected categories/grace, unknown state and mixed-member eligibility behave as
  accepted; verified-alternative checks cannot be satisfied by a caller flag, upload ACK or ordinary search receipt.
- [ ] Real PostgreSQL reference/hold/policy/restriction/claim races reject stale outcomes and unsafe disclosure;
  deletion-window semantics are implementable by downstream adapters, not only a precheck.
- [ ] Early current denial and bounded admission remain effective without completed archive/cleanup consumers;
  unqualified native effects remain gated and cannot be advertised as safe.
- [ ] Owned policy/authority/concurrency tests and actual owner integration pass with full `./bin/build` and explicit
  participant/unsupported-route evidence.

## Verification and evidence

Use real PostgreSQL owner participants and controlled independent connections/clocks, not sleeps or fixture-only
holds. Exercise rollback, stale generations, unknown owner state, protected sole copies and real verified-alternative
references. Record exact participant inventory and deletion/disclosure ordering. Consumers qualify their native
asynchronous effect separately; no fake adapter proves storage fencing. Test owned behavior, not configuration,
migrations or third-party internals. Run focused repository wrappers and full `./bin/build`; disclose warnings and
unqualified effects. No live hold/purge/content access is authorized by this planning record.
