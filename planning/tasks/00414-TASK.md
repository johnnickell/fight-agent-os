# Advance and inspect the actual creation stages

## Outcome

One confirmed attempt advances through its selected real stage adapters and exposes durable truthful progress.
Original-effect reconciliation survives interruption without turning a partial local project into registered
completion or a transport/process observation into success.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual deterministic stage composition, normal advancement, original-stage recovery integration,
  safe protected progress/resource inspection and failure/Needs Human transitions.
- Out of scope: New effect adapters, general workflow engine, browser form/recovery controls, cleanup,
  final receipt/Dashboard handoff, AI-chosen actions or a coding Workflow/TASK claim.
