# Deliver and reconcile one exact credential attempt

## Outcome

Compose the actual package delivery service with current worker authorization, PostgreSQL, cipher and fixed sink
so one exact original credential attempt is claimed, admitted, invoked and acknowledged safely. Receipt-first
reconciliation resolves lost outcomes without unnecessary decryption or changing the original operation.

## Scope

- In scope: Protected `AgentCredentialDeliveryService::deliver` composition for original key/destination/delivery
  ID; real worker identity, trusted time, separate claim/admission/outcome transactions, receipt-first recovery,
  finite pinned policy, safe outcome interpretation and current authority races.
- Out of scope: New delivery policy/queue, automatic issuance, periodic scheduling, activation/use, browser
  lease/receipt endpoints, caller-specified sinks or completing the cohort/restoration qualification owned elsewhere.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attempt exact original delivery | Package `deliver(key, destination, deliveryId)` as the authenticated delegated worker | Fresh cohort, scope, original target/credential/slot and key state | No new delivery event; package persisted outcome is authoritative | Claim then separate confirmed admission; fixed sink invocation outside all business transactions |
| Reconcile accepted but unacknowledged work | Same exact package delivery entry, never a new issuance | Exact receipt lookup/verification and fresh persisted claim/admission/authority | N/A — no replayed issuance fact | Original receipt acknowledged only through current full expected-state transaction |
| Inspect terminal or recorded completion | Same package entry or authorized original-operation status | Recorded history and independently verified receipt where required | N/A | Safe recorded result, unavailable or reconciliation-required; no rematerialization |

## Validation and permissions

Use the real worker and explicit current delegation, never impersonate the original caller. Apply package default
finite limits unless validated overrides are selected; first claim pins policy and original issuance determines
retention. Trusted time and authority expiry cannot be extended by restart/configuration or clock rollback.

Claim reserves work only. A separate confirmed admission transaction binds exact operation revision, claim token/
fence, current credential, worker/policy/binding epochs and earliest deadline. An uncertain or late-returning
admission commit grants no decryption/invocation. Recheck cohort/support/deadline outside transactions before
receipt lookup/materialization and before stage. Sink invocation and receipt verification must never be enclosed
in a database transaction. Outcome reauthorizes and compares current exact authority and persisted state.

A recovered still-current admission permits receipt reconciliation only; absence defers without another stage.
Fresh admission looks up/verifies an existing receipt before decryption. Lookup outage is not absence. A delivered
record with lost material/receipt returns reconciliation-required, never authentication-envelope fallback, automatic
rotation or history rewrite. Revoke/regrant, worker changes, destination reassignment, retirement or rewrap reject
stale acknowledgements. Already-admitted late bytes may remain inert; rollback does not recall them.

Only safe package results leave the protected operation: delivered, retryable, terminal/expired, retired, deferred,
rejected, unavailable, indeterminate or reconciliation-required as applicable. They expose no secret, material,
claim, receipt, key path or dependency exception. New-issuance capacity does not block existing recovery. Actual
activation and every use remain TICKET-00061's separately fenced authority, not a consequence of a receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual claim, admission and outcome transactions are separate and share required canonical fences; sink
  invocation/verification occurs outside them. Claim or uncertain admission never permits materialization.
- [ ] Independent competing workers, lease takeover and expiry preserve original bytes/ID/order; stale token,
  revision, epoch, deadline or authority cannot acknowledge even when another worker recovered the same receipt.
- [ ] Real sink acceptance/response loss and both commit-uncertainty outcomes resolve truthfully with the original
  operation; receipt-first recovery needs no key access when an exact valid receipt remains available.
- [ ] Current authority changes before admission deny without decryption; after admission only permitted inert
  effects survive, with no stale success. Rewrap/retirement cannot be undone by delayed delivery outcomes.
- [ ] Delivered-secret loss, malformed receipt, unavailable keys/sink and temporary/permanent failures preserve
  package distinctions and finite original retention; no new issuance or material fallback is attempted.
- [ ] Actual traces/results are secret-safe; scoped delivery acceptance remains separate from full activation/use,
  writer exclusion and restoration qualification.
