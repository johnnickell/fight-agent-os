# Integrate fail-closed lifecycle readiness and operator recovery

## Outcome

Complete the real protected operational composition, supported enrollment/recovery guidance and runtime
qualification checks for Agent credentials. Only the exact fully qualified participants can satisfy shared cohort
readiness; uncertain or missing evidence stays unavailable. Integrate existing writer/switch/restore and current
activation/use owners rather than introducing another certification system or managed role workflow.

## Scope

- In scope: Final actual protected operator/service composition and supported host/key/sink/schedule profile;
  identity-bound qualification consumed by TASK-00228's admission boundary; finite routine recovery, safe exceptional
  hold/reconciliation guidance, exact source/lock/runtime provenance and integrated credential revocation/use evidence.
- Out of scope: New registry/queue/proof product, package or starter release, physical key destruction, automatic
  live enrollment/deployment, UI, complete Explorer/eight-role journeys, duplicate Harness/Runner or restore engine.

## Validation and permissions

Consume the actual selected tagged package, committed manifest/lock and concrete consumer participants; the
historically inspected v0.5.0 guide and inherited TASK-00126 tests are not fresh qualification. Bind persisted
qualified profile to the actual local composition and independently attested exact storage incarnation. Removed,
substituted, incompatible or restarted participants must fail closed without a cached readiness assertion.
No service invocation bootstraps a cohort or copies a stored generation into a trusted witness.

Keep issued, delivered, activated and usable outcomes distinct. Activation needs confirmed exact delivery and
fresh fenced enrollment authority; lost activation acknowledgement reconciles its own exact state. Each broker
use independently checks current credential/destination, eligible direct Permissions, enrollment and relevant
human/Workflow/Team/Repository/host constraints through TICKET-00061. A receipt, signature or session snapshot
cannot authorize late/revoked/obsolete material, including before the sink learns about a replacement.

Full writer, old-binary exclusion and controlled physical-restore evidence come from TASK-00231–00233 at the
actual integrated candidate. Check content/participant provenance and rerun affected scenarios after changes;
do not relabel inherited receipts as fresh runs or duplicate their implementation. Actual tooling and component
behavior evidence together qualify the profile, not an in-memory suite or readiness flag. Unsupported host,
key/sink, origin or sandbox properties block affected work without fallback. Test-only scenarios cannot be used
to publish positive production capability declarations.

Use explicit safe target/effect authority for qualification; no real Team-free product Agent or dummy role is
created to claim EPIC acceptance. TICKET-00063 supplies Team-bound product creation and TICKET-00065 retains real
role-use integration. Routine authorized recovery uses finite defaults without new manual approval; exceptional
restoration and production enrollment require distinct operational authority. All user-facing/operator artifacts
are secret-free, truthful about indeterminate results and free of raw claims/receipts/provider paths.
