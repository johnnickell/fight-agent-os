# Resume stop and abandon Planning conversations

## Outcome

A planner resumes the same durable conversation at its unresolved question, accepted answers and pending result,
requests Stop, or separately confirms Abandon without losing history. Browser interruption and process replacement
never imply cancellation, replay or permission to start another operation.

## Scope

- In scope: Browser attach/resynchronization, progressive protected history, coherent question/proposal/conflict/
  retry status, Stop and confirmed Abandon, active-work removal and retained artifact-linked activity.
- Out of scope: Another transcript/queue/runtime owner, automatic mutation retry, provider switching, history purge,
  browser-persistent drafts, simultaneous free-form editing and final named role composition.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reopen conversation | Existing authorized attachment lifecycle | Durable baseline, unresolved question, accepted input, pending result and paged history | Attachment observations only | Same conversation without another Agent, turn or snapshot |
| Request Stop | Explicit expected-generation cancellation through TASK-00353 | Original attempt, actual liveness and uncertain effects | Owning cancellation/request and confirmed lifecycle facts | Fence new admission, not a false claim of quiescence |
| Abandon planning | Separate confirmed expected-state abandonment composed with lifecycle owner | Current operation, authority and original effects | Committed owning abandonment/audit/intent facts | Remove from active Planning work while preserving history and outstanding liabilities |
| Inspect prior activity | N/A — authorized query | Completed/abandoned operation and original outcome | N/A | Discoverable protected history, not a paid retry or resumed operation |

## Validation and permissions

Use TASK-00361's registered browser scope and TASK-00352/00353's shared attachment/lifecycle operations. Reload the
committed baseline before optional progress observations; duplicate/missing/reordered notifications cannot become
authority. Progressive pages are bounded and scoped; hide internal Pi-session boundaries while retaining authorized
lineage. Exact question, accepted answers, proposal/conflict and retry state survive replacement; unsaved form edits
do not. Attach from supported terminal/API clients remains with the owning capability, not a separate browser store.

Closing a tab, logout or disconnect detaches and retires local private data; it never silently closes the conversation
or cancels an admitted turn. Current authorization controls each later read/use. Keep unknown liveness/effects held;
no idle/success inference from transport failure. Stop acknowledgement is distinct from process exit and provider
liability. Show actionable denied/unavailable/interrupted states and original-outcome inspection before new work.

Abandon is a separate explicit human operation with exact target, current authority and expected state. Compose
owning lifecycle fencing, active-work disposition, audit and required intent atomically, with external stop effects
outside transactions. Preserve in-flight uncertainty and holds; abandonment neither proves quiescence nor starts
retention grace or erases debt. Stale callbacks cannot revive active work. Duplicate/lost responses inspect the
original disposition rather than abandon another generation. Do not create a rival Planning work queue.

Completed and abandoned operations stay discoverable through existing artifact activity and scoped intake history
when no artifact exists. Public progress/accepted artifacts remain distinct from private transcript/drafts. Use
ADR 0003/0004 no-store/strict decoding/current-generation fences; stale successes and errors after logout, revocation
or scope change cannot restore content. No mutation-replay interceptor or browser-persistent business state.

- [ ] Progressive history/status tolerate missing/reordered observations and enforce current visibility on every
  read; logout/revocation retires private content and late responses cannot restore it.
