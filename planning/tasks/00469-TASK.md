# Preview and apply independent log-retention policies

## Outcome

An authorized operator can preview and deliberately change independent ordinary live-search/archive retention while
preserving existing holds, grace and original clocks. This approved slice C of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) extends the existing owner's policy operation, not a new settings authority.

## Scope

- In scope: Bounded current-policy/eligibility preview, independent duration validation, explicit expected-revision
  apply, effective revision/audit persistence and truthful changed/future eligibility reporting.
- Out of scope: New installation-admin permissions/UI, hold release, immediate content deletion, storage repair,
  automatic capacity-driven retention cuts, execution lifecycle changes and unbounded full-catalog scans.

Consume TASK-00467's original-clock eligibility and [TASK-00379](00379-TASK.md)'s authorized policy-change operation.
Live and archive default independently to 14 and 90 days; require positive whole days <=3,650 per setting. Increasing
that engineering ceiling needs separate capacity acceptance. Holds may exceed ordinary retention; configurable
30-day post-terminal grace remains with its owner. Awaiting review is not terminal and shortening ordinary age
cannot shorten protected retention. Retry/import/replacement never resets trusted collection clocks.

Preview against current policy/references and bounded available inventory, distinguishing exact affected coverage
from partial/unknown estimates. A preview cannot promise a full installation count from one page or certify storage
capacity. Apply with current narrow authority, exact intended settings and expected policy revision; reject stale
or changed requests rather than silently applying an old preview. Hold/reference changes remain fenced and current
at use, not frozen by a preview. Commit effective policy revision and required audit atomically; no provider deletion
runs while saving configuration. Subsequent disclosure/cleanup owners consume that revision and recheck authority.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preview retention change | No business mutation | Current settings/revision and bounded affected eligibility | N/A | Honest policy impact, protected/unknown categories and coverage limits |
| Apply exact settings | Existing authorized expected-revision policy operation | Current actor, revision and valid durations | Committed policy revision/audit | Future eligibility changes, not content deletion or hold release |
| Resolve stale or uncertain apply | Reconcile original operation or request a fresh preview | Actual committed revision/outcome | Existing owner facts | No blind replay or conflicting success claim |

## Validation and permissions

Log READ/RESTORE/EXPORT/DOWNLOAD capabilities grant no policy power. Actual operator/actor binding belongs to existing
preservation/installation owners; terminal access, a Super Admin role name or a client button is not permission.
Use strict bounded input and safe policy status with no private content or arbitrary paths. Follow ADRs
[0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md). Preserve owner audit failure semantics; logging
failure never retries the policy mutation. No new browser settings surface is implied by this TASK.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual authorized preview/apply changes live/archive durations independently with correct defaults, bounds,

- [ ] Late data, time boundaries and mixed segments retain original age; protected/unknown state and terminal grace
