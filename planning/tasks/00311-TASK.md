# Authorize exact-subject acceptance

## Outcome

Make independent technical acceptance a semantic PHP decision about one immutable reviewed implementation,
not a model verdict, projection, process exit or mutable branch. Later QA and publication consume that exact fact.

## Scope

- In scope: Authorized acceptance operation, exact eligibility query, expected-revision concurrency, immutable
  acceptance provenance, invalidation and attributable correction handling through existing Workflow operations.
- Out of scope: Performing Review, Engineer repair, QA verdicts, publication, generic status setters and claiming
  human PR approval, defect-free software, merge, release, deployment or certification.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accept reviewed implementation | Semantic exact-subject acceptance | Current finalized Review, authority, criteria, checks, findings and limits | Accepted fact only after successful revalidation | Current technical acceptance for one immutable subject |
| Explain eligibility | N/A — read-only | Exact acceptance and failed/missing prerequisites | N/A — queries do not accept | Safe actionable reasons without exposing private evidence |
| Invalidate current acceptance | Authorized semantic invalidation on established material change/blocker | Current applicability, later findings and requirement/evidence revisions | Attributed invalidation linked to retained acceptance | Later phases held; original Review and acceptance history preserved |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00445's finalized immutable Review. Re-read Workflow/TASK and criterion revisions, configured base,
full base/head commits, complete tree/status and selected Artifact digests, check identities/results/freshness,
findings/dispositions, contributor independence, current grants/live authority, limits and expected aggregate
revisions. Every criterion must be satisfied or validly waived by separately authorized exact evidence, all
required checks passed and no blocking finding unresolved. A verdict or observation alone cannot transition state.

Use package-owned direct Permissions and current delegated Workflow/Repository authority; Reviewer reporting
permission does not itself grant acceptance. Missing, stale, mismatched, partial or unavailable evidence denies.
Commit state and required evidence/audit atomically through ADR 0002; competing requirement, subject, authority or
finding changes cannot race past acceptance. Duplicate commands resolve the original result with its subject.

Material implementation, test, configuration, requirement or reviewed-evidence changes, unresolved integration
uncertainty and later established blockers invalidate current applicability. Preserve original finalized bytes and
events; annotations alone do not silently change acceptance. Team Lead/authorized owning coordination uses semantic
invalidation and the existing revision/Review route when appropriate; only persisted blockers authorize repair.

Changed OIDs, rebase/merge or mechanical documentation conflicts alone are not evidence of semantic invalidity.
Until TASK-00313 establishes a validated applicability bridge, a changed subject cannot be treated as the original
accepted subject. Expose unresolved applicability rather than forge review provenance. Additional supporting QA
artifacts alone do not invalidate technical acceptance; a newly established technical blocker does.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Only the authorized semantic operation accepts a current finalized independent Review after complete
  subject, authority, criteria, checks, finding and limit revalidation.
- [ ] Partial/stale subjects, missing required checks, unresolved blockers, invalid waivers and authority loss
  reject, including controlled PostgreSQL races and duplicate command delivery.
- [ ] Acceptance remains bound to the original immutable subject and separate from QA, Planning completion,
  PR publication, human approval and merge.
- [ ] Material change or an established later blocker invalidates applicability without rewriting history;
  annotations and new supporting QA evidence do not fabricate acceptance or unnecessary re-review.
- [ ] Unknown reconciliation applicability holds consumers rather than treating changed OIDs as proof either
  of current acceptance or of a newly demonstrated implementation defect.

## Verification and evidence

Test owned acceptance decision tables, exact evidence binding, authorization, invalidation and transactional races
through Domain/Application and real PostgreSQL contracts, plus important terminal/API behavior. Exercise acceptance
of an actual managed Review and retain denied/current outcomes with source, Review, subject and command identity.
Do not test skill prose, generated files, migration text or quality tools in the product suite. Run focused checks
and `./bin/build`; distinguish fresh verification from inherited reviewer evidence and incomplete live proof.
