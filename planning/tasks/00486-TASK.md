# Inspect and recover observability through the existing operator guide

## Outcome

Operators can diagnose degraded observability and follow exercised authorized recovery from the existing capability
view and task-based guide, including when search is unavailable. This approved slice F of
[TICKET-00117](../tickets/00117-TICKET.md) is the actual operator journey, not a second Dashboard or recovery engine.

## Scope

- In scope: Additive protected capability/status presentation and guide integration, independent trusted local
  inspection, explicit enrollment/retention/recovery instructions, original-outcome recovery and safe partial states.
- Out of scope: New authority/status/job stores, automatic browser repair, raw logs in general diagnostics, duplicate
  collection/archive/purge/restore state machines or claiming full release fit from a guide screenshot.

Extend TASK-00074's safe installation View and TASK-00075's single versioned guide. Consume TASK-00456's independent
status, TASK-00460's archive delivery, TASK-00469's policy operation, TASK-00472's privacy reconciliation,
TASK-00473's catalog recovery and TASK-00477's temporary cleanup. Their transitive suppliers provide the actual runtime,
storage, transfer and operator bindings; this guide must not become a reverse prerequisite for them.

Present exact platform/version/support labels, configured/enrolled sources, current pressure/pinned/residual bytes,
pending work and freshness only where the current actor may know them. General installation diagnostics remain
secret-safe/minimized under installation-information authority; raw/source coverage and log-specific private details
retain their separate current log permissions. Trusted local inspection has its separate installation boundary and
can report independent degradation when application/search is unavailable. Stale or unknown never means healthy.

Every task states target, prerequisites/authority, exact human action, expected effects, read-only verification,
partial outcome, recovery/rollback limits and primary sources. Preflight cannot pull images, start/repair services,
assign permissions or perform credential/DNS/mount mutation. Runtime enrollment and log capability reconciliation
remain explicit owner operations, not steps silently executed by opening the guide. No sudo/credential collection.

Exercise original-operation recovery for source/replay and independent destination outages, partial acceptance,
archive intent/verification/catalog recovery, unknown owner state and cleanup pressure. Preserve original clocks,
identities, holds and purge restrictions; manifests cannot reconstruct authority. Display requested, processed,
absence-confirmed and physically reclaimed separately. Never repair missing logs by rerunning business commands.
Logical restore denial precedes delayed cleanup; retention-policy saving does not delete or release holds.

Use actual authorized owner operations, not generic destructive buttons. Explain unrecoverable gaps, unsupported
backup/physical routes and local host-loss limits. Preserve incomplete evidence and stop on unknown ownership rather
than claiming universal recovery/erasure. No hidden uploads or raw content in copied support reports. Browser state is
volatile and generation-fenced; fresh authority governs private fetch/release and late results cannot revive data.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inspect current/degraded installation | None; protected query or trusted local inspection | Existing safe View and authorized independent status | None from reads | Honest bounded pass/warn/unknown/unsupported facts |
| Follow an enrollment/policy/recovery task | Explicit existing owner operation after human checks | Current target/authority/original outcome | Existing owning lifecycle/audit facts only | Attributed bounded effects or explicit unsafe stop |
| Verify recovery or explain residual loss | No automatic retry/repair | Actual source/destination/catalog/cleanup outcomes | None from inspection | Known coverage, gaps and physical residuals distinguished |
