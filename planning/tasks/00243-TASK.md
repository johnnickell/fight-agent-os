# Activate only the exact currently authorized delivered credential

## Outcome

Implement a protected durable activation use case for the exact enrolled holder and currently authorized delivered
credential/destination. Activation has its own confirmed or indeterminate result and recovery; issuance, a receipt
or authentication never implicitly activates a holder or permits later tool use.

## Scope

- In scope: Agent OS-owned activation/reconciliation crossing package delivery, holder enrollment and current
  authority; immutable original binding, explicit confirmation, safe status, current enrollment revocation and
  transaction-duration activation/authority fences; hold behavior during cohort switch/restoration.
- Out of scope: Package delivery policy changes, browser activation/secret endpoints, automatic rotation, independent
  credential store, broker/tool implementation, Team creation or complete managed Agent product readiness.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activate exact delivered credential | Protected holder activation under current enrollment authority | Exact original issuance, confirmed package delivery, verified sink evidence and current Agent/destination/holder | Owned activation fact only after confirmed commit, no duplicate issuance event | Durable exact activation under current shared authority, not inferred delivery success |
| Resolve lost activation acknowledgement | Reconcile original activation identity/state, never new issuance | Authoritative activation outcome and current exact binding/authority | No repeated success fact solely from retry | Confirmed original state or truthful indeterminate/hold; no assumed activation |
| Revoke or hold enrollment/use eligibility | Protected owning enrollment operation and cohort hold controls | Current activation/binding and relevant authority epochs | Attributable owned state change only after commit | Old activation cannot confer subsequent signing/use, including revoke/regrant or restore |

## Validation and permissions

Use TASK-00234's real holder/operator enrollment and TASK-00242's request authentication where applicable.
Authenticate holder identity without treating pre-activation authentication as use permission. Select the exact
current package credential ID/revision, original delivery identity and registered destination/binding, never the
sink's arrival pointer/high-water mark. Current valid enrollment authorization is independently mandatory even
when an exact receipt and confirmed delivery exist. Pending/retryable/indeterminate delivery cannot activate.

The owning Application operation commits activation state and required audit atomically on the canonical connection,
sharing cohort-first credential/holder/destination/authority fences and monotonic epochs. Sink receipt verification
remains outside the business transaction; revalidate exact current state under the fence before activation. A stale
external observation cannot authorize a changed binding. Unknown/missing evidence or inadequate atomic seams block
exposure; no nested transaction, local ready boolean, in-transaction sink call or fabricated package API.

Use a durable exact activation identity/expected state for retries. Lost acknowledgement requires reconciliation of
activation's own result before use; an exception does not prove rollback. Fresh current authority applies to retry.
Rotation, revocation, holder loss, destination reassignment, expired/revoked delegation and epoch ABA invalidate
stale activation. Already admitted late sink bytes remain inert, including when the sink has not learned a replacement.
Lost delivered material, terminal keys or tombstones cannot prompt envelope fallback, predecessor restoration or
automatic reissuance. No new raw material is returned by activation/status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual delivery plus fresh fenced enrollment authority produces one separately confirmed activation of the
  exact holder/credential/destination. Issuance, authentication, receipt alone or incomplete delivery cannot do so.
- [ ] Real concurrent activation versus rotation/revocation/reassignment and revoke/regrant serializes safely;
  wrong holder, stale receipt/binding and unsupported cohort deny without partial activation/audit.
- [ ] Lost activation commit acknowledgement in committed and rolled-back cases remains indeterminate until
  authoritative original-state resolution; retry neither duplicates issuance nor assumes a usable holder.
- [ ] Activation survives legitimate restart with exact provenance but grants nothing during unreconciled storage,
  lost enrollment/material or stale authority. Protected hold/revocation can prevent new use independently of events.
- [ ] Actual activation reads/results/traces are secret-free and distinguish confirmed, denied, unavailable and
  uncertain state. Each later broker/tool use still requires TASK-00244's current authorization.
