# Publish the immutable qualified reference bundle to the five starter maintainers

## Outcome

The five starter maintainer destinations receive the same identifiable, accessible qualified Agent OS reference
snapshot with verification instructions and honest caveats. This completes EPIC-00018's actual handoff rather
than stopping at local documentation. One unranked chore owns packaging, authorized delivery and a usable successor
procedure; it is not three infrastructure projects or a shared starter release pipeline.

## Scope

- In scope: One versioned `.tar.gz` archive of the approved Markdown matrix/references with a JSON identity
  manifest, safe evidence retention, explicit publisher/recipient/channel qualification, authorized publication,
  actual delivery/access receipts and manual successor instructions.
- Out of scope: Another matrix/reference guide/Swagger implementation, product code or publication service,
  general-purpose archive generator, automatic release/notification pipeline, repeat consumer certification,
  starter edits/PRs/tests/certification/tags/releases, forced adoption and automatic Planning cleanup/archiving.

### Archive and manifest identity

Assemble one versioned `.tar.gz` archive with Markdown guidance, the approved matrix and a JSON machine-readable
identity manifest. Choose and document the concrete layout/version identifier during implementation; it is a
bundle identity, not an Agent OS/AccessControl/starter SemVer release or an invented package-version prerequisite.
Use existing tools and a bounded documented procedure, not a general packaging framework.

The manifest identifies every delivered component by a unique safe relative path and immutable content digest,
with its role, actual source/provenance and necessary access classification. Include actual package/tag/source,
lock identity/reference, qualified implementation versus documentation identities, OpenAPI source/content
hashes, bundle version, scope/dispositions, and per-capability scenario/receipt provenance and limitations.
A controlled external evidence reference needs immutable content identity/location, retention/access ownership
and enough provenance to verify the claim; a moving latest URL, local scratch path or signed link alone is not
durable evidence. Do not place access credentials or bearer URLs in the manifest.

Define the checksum boundary without recursion: hash the delivered component bytes in the manifest, excluding
its own bytes from that table with an explicit explanation; include the manifest in the archive and record the
complete archive SHA-256 in a detached checksum/publication receipt. Enumerate all archive components, including
the manifest's role, and document how verification covers them. Nothing embeds its own whole-content hash.
The detached digest must be obtained through the authorized trusted handoff/channel; a checksum alone does not
establish publisher authority or authenticate an arbitrary replacement archive. No new signing service or
unapproved release-signing requirement follows from this design.

Inspect the complete archive member list and extracted content with the owning tools. Reject unsafe/absolute/
traversing or duplicate paths, unexpected links/files and unmanifested payloads; do not package a checkout or
`.runs/` directory wholesale. Verification must not execute bundled code or overwrite a recipient workspace.
Keep ephemeral assembly/inspection under ignored `.runs/` subfolders, but retain final evidence with the bundle
or through verified immutable controlled references outside ephemeral scratch.

Explicitly state whether authorized private OpenAPI/source/receipt material is included or referenced at its
controlled location. Verify that recipients are permitted to receive/read it and can resolve what is required;
no assumption that their maintainer title grants deployment Swagger access. Keep credentials, raw transcripts,
sensitive personal data, private operator/provider configuration and unapproved source/receipt bodies out of
archives, screenshots, manifest fields, delivery messages and logs. Redaction preserves truthful observations
and limitations, not fictional sanitized results.

### Authorized publication and complete delivery

After candidate content, source applicability, redaction and required checks are accepted and publisher authority
is present, publish the exact version/content through the verified channel. Retain its immutable locator, safe
publication provenance and detached archive digest. Independently retrieve/inspect actual uploaded bytes and
compare the complete archive and component identities, rather than trust a successful upload response or filename.
Never overwrite a published version. Different content at an existing identity is a conflict, not a retry target.

Deliver the same snapshot identity, verification instructions, credential-free prerequisites and limitations to
all five destinations. Verify effective authorized access to the artifact and required references for each
resolved recipient, not solely publisher access, a sent invitation or repository visibility. Use the selected
channel's supported access evidence and appropriately authorized retrieval checks; do not impersonate readers,
request their credentials or bypass restrictions. Check that the selected artifact/material remains restricted
to its approved audience through direct channel/access inspection.

Record actual publication and each destination's delivery/access outcome with safe receipts. Missing access,
unavailable required evidence, partial upload, digest mismatch or unverified delivery keeps acceptance incomplete;
never publish private content openly to work around it. Maintainer acknowledgement, adoption, adaptation,
certification and release are not required, but successful send/upload alone is insufficient access evidence.

For timeouts/disconnects, inspect the exact remote object/version/content and retained outcomes before any
separately authorized retry. Reuse an already verified identical publication without claiming a new upload; do
not create replacement identities to hide uncertainty, overwrite bytes, assume failure means no remote effect
or mark all destinations complete from one result. Record partial/unknown state and resume only unresolved
steps. This is a manual artifact lifecycle, not an application transaction/outbox or exactly-once delivery claim.
Publication/delivery receipts can be appended as separate attributed records without modifying the sealed bundle.

### Preserve versions and describe successor handling

Document a practical manual successor procedure: on package/lock/consumer/API or relevant configuration/evidence
changes, identify affected claims, retain applicable unchanged evidence with its original provenance, obtain
owning requalification, refresh the complete coverage/reference snapshot, then assemble and authorize a newly
identified complete bundle. Include change rationale and predecessor identity. Corrections to published material
also receive a new identity rather than changing old bytes. Preserve old published content and its historical
subject without implying it qualifies the new consumer.

No automatic publication on a source change, shared five-starter update pipeline or artificial second release
is needed to accept this first handoff. Directly walk the successor instructions against the real manifest and
owner/change boundaries without inventing live releases, new provider calls or seeded failure tests. Separate
future publication authority remains required. Starter owners independently decide adaptation/security/testing/
certification/releases; EPIC-00009's per-starter immutable stable SemVer >=1.0.0 eligibility stays unchanged.
