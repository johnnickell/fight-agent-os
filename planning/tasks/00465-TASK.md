# Follow live logs with bounded pause and recovery

## Outcome

An authorized operator follows real live logs, pauses to inspect content and resumes with honest coverage under
finite server/browser resources and current authority. This is approved slice E of
[TICKET-00113](../tickets/00113-TICKET.md), not an unqualified long-running PHP request or polling substitute.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual private backend tail translation through same-origin authenticated fetch streaming; current
  lease/batch authorization, bounded admission/backpressure/cancellation, native Follow/Pause/Resume and coverage-aware
  reconnect, shared browser/detail budgets and real ingress/worker/socket qualification.
- Out of scope: Token URLs, browser-to-Loki access, new credential store, raw logs in Mercure, another Dashboard,
  durable exactly-once tail claims, automatic business/restore/export replay and silent polling-only fallback.

Follow [WF-041's finite stream contract](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-041-select-the-authorized-log-browser-integration.md#initial-finite-browser-and-transport-envelope).
Use the existing same-origin Bearer profile with authenticated fetch streaming; the private Adapter translates Loki
transport and owns its credentials. Qualify frame decoding, ingress buffering, cancellation and actual shared worker
occupancy before enrollment. TASK-00389's Mercure topics/backlog remain safe Workflow notifications, not log transport.

Limit tails to four/installation, two/requester and one/view. Leases last at most 30 seconds or remaining credential
validity, whichever shorter; recheck actual current actor/session/capability/disclosure policy on every lease and
before each bounded output batch. Cached request authority or a still-valid lease is not a fresh release check.
Unknown authority releases no payload. Already released/in-flight bytes cannot be recalled.

Batch at most 64 KiB encoded and queue at most 1 MiB output/stream; flush eligible records within one second when
healthy. Slow clients receive explicit interruption/gap and disconnect rather than unbounded buffers. Healthy idle
heartbeats are bounded non-sensitive signals at most every five seconds, not collection-health proof. Aggregate
admission includes abandoned work until actual cancellation/completion is known, not merely browser disconnect.

Retain at most 2,000 observations/8 MiB encoded per view including detail and paused arrivals, rendering at most 200
rows. Pause freezes the visible reading position/context; backlog is at most 500 observations/2 MiB within that same
budget. Overflow stops follow and shows a gap without evicting frozen context. Only explicit resume/navigation or
required authority clearing releases that frozen view.

Reconnect uses jittered 1–30-second backoff and at most five failed attempts before explicit Retry. Reauthorize and
reconcile the stopped interval through TASK-00463's bounded search before claiming caught up; continuation may be
partial/unknown and cannot jump to now labelled continuous. Preserve stable observation identities and distinguish
browser-buffer gaps from collector loss/archive gaps. Do not deduplicate legitimate identical messages by content.
No reconnection extends retention, session authority or a restored scope; live tail does not mix restored data.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Follow current logs | Read subscription only | Current authority, finite lease and bounded backend tail | Transport observations, not Domain events | Real authorized live records within aggregate resource limits |
| Pause to inspect | Local view control only | Existing frozen context/detail | Client state only | Stable focus/view with finite backlog and honest overflow interruption |
| Resume/reconnect | Bounded read reconciliation, no business mutation | Fresh authority and known search/tail coverage | Transport/coverage observations | Catch-up only when established; unknown intervals remain explicit |
| Revoke or disconnect | Existing cancellation/revocation processing | Current authority and actual provider cancellation/completion | Operational status only | Further release denied; resources reclaimed/accounted without recall promises |

## Validation and permissions

Require current `READ_LOGS` plus applicable source/time/disclosure policy throughout fetch/release. Reuse
TASK-00463's authoritative resolver/fences and the existing authentication coordinator, never a second refresh loop,
role-name check or persistent token. Follow ADRs [0003](../adr/0003-browser-authentication-security-profile.md) and
[0004](../adr/0004-client-authority-and-runtime-state.md): no-store, applicable same-origin/CSRF controls, volatile
state and generation fences. Refresh/logout/expiry/invalidation/identity/scope changes retire streams and private
buffers; late successes/errors cannot revive them. Stream tokens/search text never enter URLs/history/telemetry.
Malformed transport frames terminate honestly; raw log bytes remain inert data under TASK-00464's rendering rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual authenticated browser-to-application-to-private-backend streaming works through the real ingress without
  token URLs, unqualified buffering, Mercure reuse or silent polling substitution.
- [ ] Lease and per-batch fresh authority checks deny revoked/expired/unavailable authority during output; stale
  request snapshots and live connections cannot prolong access.
- [ ] Real aggregate connection/batch/queue/worker/socket limits, healthy flush and cancellation accounting hold
  with slow/abandoned clients; serialized budgets are supplemented by measured process/browser fitness.
- [ ] Pause overflow preserves frozen context, stops follow and exposes the gap. Resume/reconnect reauthorize and
  reconcile coverage, respect retry bounds and never fabricate continuous/caught-up state or replay mutations.
- [ ] Arrivals preserve keyboard/focus; explicit controls, reduced motion, non-color severity, bounded announcements,
  zoom/narrow/light-dark behavior and private-buffer clearing work in the actual browser.
- [ ] Owned protocol/state/authority tests, direct backend/ingress/transport evidence and full `./bin/build` pass,
  including measured WF-042 healthy visibility objectives and disclosed degraded behavior.
