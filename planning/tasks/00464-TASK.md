# Inspect exact diagnostic content and surrounding context

## Outcome

An authorized operator can inspect a selected observation's emitted diagnostic details and nearby same-source
activity without losing authority/time constraints or mistaking a preview for the original record. This is approved
slice D of [TICKET-00113](../tickets/00113-TICKET.md), a complete private API-to-browser context/detail journey.

## Scope

- In scope: Bounded source-generation context queries, exact raw detail/fragment continuation, native exception/
  context/extra presentation, clear preview/coverage semantics, inert rendering and accessible row/detail interaction.
- Out of scope: New diagnostic emission, stack arguments/locals/object snapshots, whole-object dumping, cross-source
  invented adjacency, bulk export/download, tail and another query/authority/cache owner.

Use [WF-041's context/detail contract](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-041-select-the-authorized-log-browser-integration.md#search-context-and-exact-detail)
and TASK-00463's current-authority, typed-query, policy/cursor and shared-admission boundary. Start context at up to
50 observations on each side within five minutes each side. Explicitly label any relaxation of text/severity filters;
never relax current authority, source-generation, time eligibility or restore isolation. Correlation across sources
is an explicit relationship filter, not manufactured neighboring chronology. Further paging/window changes are
explicit bounded reads with tie-safe traversal, not automatic fanout.

Expand only actually emitted context/extra and exception class/message/file/line/frames/chained causes/release data.
Native normalization markers and missing fields remain visible. Render text inertly, including HTML, ANSI/terminal
controls and URL-like content; no executable markup, automatic external resources or synthesized local variables.
Preserve reversible representation and trusted fragment provenance for malformed/binary/oversized records.

Raw detail is at most 64 KiB raw per fragment and 1 MiB encoded response, with explicit continuation bound to the
same observation/source and current policy. One detail is expanded per view. A labelled preview/partial continuation
is not a complete record; manual copy of a preview must identify that limitation. Do not silently redact/truncate
stored originals or automatically load every cause/fragment/related event.

Context/detail share the same installation-wide two-read/export and one-requester admission, 30-second deadline,
500-event/1-MiB page bounds, and 2,000-observation/8-MiB total browser budget with search/paused data. Retire private
state and late work on authority/scope change. Large raw content never forces an unbounded DOM or steals focus.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inspect neighboring activity | No mutation | Bounded exact source-generation context | N/A | Explicitly scoped neighbors with labelled filter relaxation and honest gaps |
| Expand diagnostic detail | No mutation | Current-authorized exact observation/fragment | N/A | Actual emitted fields rendered inertly, not a new object dump |
| Continue a large/binary record | Explicit bounded read, not export | Same observation/source-bound continuation | N/A | Reversible content and provenance within finite budgets, labelled incomplete until established |
| Lose authority during inspection | No automatic replay | Fresh current authority/policy plus client generations | Client invalidation only | Further release withheld and private details cleared; released bytes cannot be recalled |

## Validation and permissions

Require current `READ_LOGS` at fetch and private release under TASK-00463, never possession of a cursor/event ID.
Unknown or unauthorized targets must not reveal existence or counts. Retain same-origin authenticated/applicable-CSRF
transport, no-store and volatile authority/query generations from ADRs
[0003](../adr/0003-browser-authentication-security-profile.md) and
[0004](../adr/0004-client-authority-and-runtime-state.md). No raw detail belongs in URLs/history/analytics or ordinary
error responses. Preserve [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) inward ownership and
separate evidence/related-entity permissions; exact raw access is not another execution or storage grant.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real context navigation retrieves same-source/generation neighbors with tie-safe traversal, explicit bounded
  filter relaxation and unchanged current scope/time/authority; gaps/late coverage remain honest.
- [ ] Actual emitted exception/cause/context/extra fields are inspectable without invented locals or hidden original
  truncation; missing/normalization markers and previews retain their true meaning.
- [ ] Binary/malformed/large continuations preserve exact captured bytes and fragment attribution; forged/stale/cross-
  observation continuations deny safely and cannot bypass limits or disclose hidden targets.
- [ ] HTML/ANSI/URL-like payloads remain inert, with no external resource load; shared server/browser/detail bounds
  and current-authority clearing hold under slow/late/error responses.
- [ ] Actual keyboard/focus, wrapping/zoom/narrow/light-dark/reduced-motion behavior works with one expanded detail;
  owned API/client/security tests, direct provider checks and full `./bin/build` pass.
