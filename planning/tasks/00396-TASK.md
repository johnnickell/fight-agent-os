# Inspect attributable execution usage

## Outcome

Authorized operators can inspect actual Workflow/phase-attributable usage through protected API/console queries
and rebuildable projections, without duplicate cumulative reports, invented prices or a competing accounting ledger.
This early measurement supplier enables TASK-00387 independently of completed Dashboard, quota and forecasts.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual request-to-Workflow attribution, safe provider/runtime fact integration, bounded rollups and
  queries, explicit completeness/provenance, original correction handling and projection/lifecycle participation.
- Out of scope: New provider authentication/transport, request reservation or settlement policy, grant enforcement,
  transcript ingestion, quota inference, pricing service, browser components and execution/forecast decisions.
