# Complete the supported live-and-archived operator journey

## Outcome

The supported installation has an exercised complete operator handoff: diagnose a real failed operation/run, inspect
live context, restore older content and extract permitted material with measured capacity and recovery boundaries.
This approved slice G of [TICKET-00117](../tickets/00117-TICKET.md) owns composed product delivery qualification and
its supported configuration/guide reconciliation, not a proof-only infrastructure demo or another parent-closeout gate.

## Scope

- In scope: Complete actual operator journeys, declared supported installation/profile, integrated workload/fault/
  resource evidence, supported configuration and operator-guide reconciliation, truthful release limitations.
- Out of scope: Reimplementing earlier owners, deferring their tests here, generic benchmark/monitoring platform,
  automatic production enrollment, new scope/permission/retention policy, or using synthetic receipts as production facts.

Consume TASK-00486's exercised operator guide/recovery, TASK-00466's real failure/run diagnosis and TASK-00480's real
restored export with their transitive suppliers. These dependencies cover all earlier observability TASKs, including
independent archive delivery, privacy/lifecycle and native archive download. Earlier slices must already own their
behavior/security/accessibility/native qualification; this TASK challenges their actual composition rather than
substituting one final test run for missing local acceptance.

Use the full [WF-042 workload/objectives/failure matrix](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-042-bound-observability-delivery-and-acceptance.md#proposed-measurable-acceptance-profile)
without weakening WF-038/039/041 bounds. Declare exact OS/architecture/runtime/driver/collector/backend/storage/image
identities, actual sources, narrow privileges, provenance/security/license acceptance and finite physical/process
allocations. Reconcile measured supported configuration and guide labels; unsupported platforms/operations remain
explicit. Human signing/publication/merge/deployment/enrollment are separate from these delivery receipts.

Run separately authorized controlled baseline of one hour at 10 records/second, approximately 2 KiB mean original
record size, and a separate 60-second 1,000-record/second burst. Include all actual source families, eight native levels
including DEBUG, varied non-repetitive content, exception/cause shapes, ties, legitimate duplicates, nested/non-HTTP/
no-subscriber activity, late records, short-lived sources and malformed/binary/large fragments. Generate identifiers
through owning factories/libraries. Manifest exact expected identities/digests; never use live secrets or transcripts.

Exercise four tails, two shared live/restored read/export/download operations and one restore, including paused/slow
clients. Populate representative retained history around day 14/day 90, grace/holds and old restored clocks. Controlled
fixture clocks prove eligibility, not long-term durability or authority to rewrite production time. Qualify a real
supported 1-GiB decompressed restore within 15 minutes by actual content/coverage queries, not upload/flush or timeout.

Measure healthy baseline live collection-to-display p95 <=5 seconds/p99 <=15 seconds; search/context/detail first-result
p95 <=3 seconds with >=100 varied requests/class; archive independently verified publication p95 <=60 seconds;
non-exhausted burst backlogs settled within five minutes without unexplained loss. Report producer-to-capture lag,
sample counts, cold/warm/scanned volume, clock uncertainty, exact byte/multiplicity dispositions and process resources.
Normal latency objectives do not replace degraded/exhaustion contracts or promise arbitrary shared-fault survival.

Qualify complete producer-failure isolation, checkpoints/replay/retirement, independent and paired sink outages,
partial rejection/HTTP 260/WAL faults, exhausted/protected-only storage, archive/catalog corruption/recovery, current
hold/purge/policy/late-publication/asynchronous deletion races, immediate logical restore expiry and physical leftovers.
Verify fresh authority at fetch/release and every bounded output batch, cross-requester denial, tied traversal,
reconnect/pause/slow clients and late UI clearing. No false complete/caught-up/healthy/verified state is acceptable.

Measure total runtime/collection/live/archive/temporary/browser/worker/socket resources and actual supported retention
fit, not only serialized limits. Rough raw load is 23 GiB/14 days and 148 GiB/90 days before overhead/holds; 50-GiB archive
admission is not a 90-day guarantee. Compression must be representative. Return measured conflicts to the owning
engineering/security/preservation authority; no silent shorter retention, suppression of DEBUG, protected-copy loss,
custom substitute or unlimited held growth. Preserve all incomplete findings until resolved or explicitly amended.
