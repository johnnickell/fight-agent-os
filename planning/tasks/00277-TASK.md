# Operate scheduled notice recovery and safe inspection

## Outcome

Operate both old-mailbox notice stages through a real qualified provider and bounded restart-independent
schedule, with protected safe inspection, explicit retention and honest failure/recovery. Supply a qualified
operational boundary to later email-change exposure without depending on completed downstream browser journeys.

## Scope

- In scope: Real shared mail-provider integration/enrollment for notices; protected bounded discovery/recovery
  and schedule; safe status, failure disposition, retention and operational readiness evidence for both stages.
- Out of scope: New credential queue, generic scheduler/job platform, public worker/retry API, browser account
  management, new-mail credential handling, automatic deployment/enrollment authority or broad observability UI.
