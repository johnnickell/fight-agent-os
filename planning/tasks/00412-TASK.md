# Fence original stage admission delivery and outcomes

## Outcome

A confirmed creation attempt supplies actual durable stage admission, original delivery and verified-result
participants to its effect adapters. Restart, duplicate delivery and stale reports preserve the same attempt,
stage and resource ownership instead of launching uncertain work or advancing from transport observations.

## Scope

- In scope: Protected stage admission/result contracts, exact state transitions and resource generations,
  bounded durable discovery/delivery/claims, original outcome recovery and safe pending/held inspection.
- Out of scope: Local/provider effects, general job platform, separate scheduler/claim authority, browser controls,
  full stage composition, credential delivery policy or coding Workflow/TASK Start.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admit next exact stage | Current-authorized expected-state stage admission | Original plan, prerequisites, owner generation and current policy | Committed stage intent facts | One durable original stage intent/resource exclusion |
| Deliver committed work | Bounded original-intent discovery and delivery | Pending intent, live claim and prior delivery disposition | Safe owning delivery facts | At-least-once delivery after commit, not execution success |
| Accept verified result | Expected-state original-stage result recording | Exact operation, actor/Runner lineage and evidence | Verified stage/attempt facts | Atomic result/state/audit; stale outcomes cannot advance |
| Reconcile delivery loss | Explicit original-operation recovery | Durable intent/result and bounded transport observations | Original disposition or hold | No new attempt or blind effect replay |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00411 immutable plan, consent, references and initiating-human scope. Define the closed creation
stage/transition contract and narrowly authenticated producer/Runner result boundary used by TASK-00407 and later
adapters. Known IDs, enrolled Runner credentials, queue messages and a historical grant are not effect authority.
Verify current stage-specific rights, exact eligible Runner/capability generations, complete expected state and
current resource ownership before admission. Preserve TASK-00407/00409's actual host/process holds; this slice
implements neither sandbox nor executor. A missing qualified route remains pending/held.

Reuse Fight Common messaging and the existing qualified installation/Runner transport boundary where its contracts
fit. TASK-00370's Workflow-ID protocol is not permission to fabricate a Workflow or reuse its claims for creation.
Qualify selected package/transport support directly; unsupported required behavior stops for its owner rather than
vendor patches, a new broker or a general job platform. Keep discovery, batching, payloads, waits, retries and
capacity finite; no unrestricted message publishing or arbitrary executable serialization.

State, required audit, original intent, resource/reference exclusions and outcome use ADR 0002's shared connection
and deterministic lock order. Commit claims before external delivery; accept outcomes only from the matching
original operation and current owner generation. External transport/process/filesystem/provider work remains
outside business transactions. A post-commit callback is an optimization, not recovery authority. Acknowledgements,
keepalive, lease expiry and process absence are observations, never stage completion or proof of safe relaunch.

Separate delivery retry from effect retry. Repeated delivery resolves the original durable stage; uncertain
external effects require their owning adapter's reconciliation before any repeat. No second directory/commit/remote,
new attempt, budget reset or exactly-once promise. Safe outcome recording distinguishes verified success, failure,
skip, pending, uncertainty and Needs Human without turning an injected report into trusted evidence.

Provide shared gates for cancellation, later revised continuation and cleanup exclusion; TASK-00416/00413 compose
their explicit operations. Cancellation winning before admission prevents the next effect; an already admitted
operation retains truthful running/unknown facts. Late success may reconcile original effects but cannot reopen
cancelled admission or authorize a successor. Retain immutable plans and attributed changes rather than rewriting
history. Missing or contradictory evidence holds conservatively.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual protected admission/result participants commit exact stage/resource state, audit, intent and original
  outcome atomically and reject stale authority, generation, prerequisite or producer identity.
- [ ] Real PostgreSQL and bounded transport integration survive commit/send/ack interruption and duplicate
  delivery without another attempt, claim authority or automatically repeated uncertain effect.
- [ ] Cancellation/cleanup exclusion and stale-result races cannot admit subsequent effects or erase original
  uncertainty; no transport/process observation establishes success.
- [ ] Safe durable queries launch nothing, reveal no secrets and distinguish unsupported/unavailable execution.
