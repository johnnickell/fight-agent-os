# Review and publish guidance at an authorized target

## Outcome

A currently authorized Repository or Workspace steward assesses a pending proposal against independently
checked underlying evidence, rejects it with safe retained rationale, or publishes its **own new** attributed
entry at the target scope. An Engineer cannot directly publish beyond its assigned scope, and a source note
cannot approve itself. This TASK delivers a complete target-review/publication operation across Domain,
Application, guarded persistence and the qualified first-party access path, not a broad UI dashboard.

## Scope

- Use [TASK-00190](00190-TASK.md)'s pending proposal and existing private/shared source identity. Resolve
  source read authority and target Repository/Workspace write ownership **independently at decision time**;
  confirm current steward assignment, effective Permission, source status/revision, ancestry and applicability.
  Human or Agent stewards use their actual qualified principal and target authority, never a role title,
  suggested target ID, remembered prose or a prior handoff as a grant. An accepted proposal does not itself
  perform a target write without revalidation.
- Let the steward inspect safe proposal rationale and currently permitted sources plus checked independent
  code, published Planning, Workflow evidence or other relevant underlying evidence. Record what was checked,
  its observed revision and limits, applicability, author, decision and contradictions. A private source
  reference never grants the steward `READ_PRIVATE_AGENT_MEMORIES`; if that source cannot be read under
  current authority, do not claim it independently verified or publish from it. Permit safe rejection or
  explicit unavailable/unknown assessment rather than fabricate proof. Current code establishes behavior;
  published Planning establishes approved intent, and a discrepancy is reported rather than rewritten.
- Rejection retains attributed rationale, target, proposal/source link and safe history but does not declare
  the source false or expose rejected prose to ordinary guidance. Acceptance and separately attributed
  publication create a **new** Repository- or Workspace-owned entry linked to proposal, source and checked
  evidence; original source remains unchanged at its scope. Direct Repository → Workspace proposals do not
  require an intermediate publication. A properly authorized steward needs no additional human confirmation.
- Make same-command accept, reject and publish retries idempotent and protect against competing conflicting
  decisions, stale grants, source revision changes and target mismatches. A single coordinated operation may
  atomically record acceptance and the target entry if it still keeps proposal/entry identities and facts
  distinct; otherwise record durable authorized decision and revalidate before publication. No half-published
  guidance, duplicate target entries or optimistic client authority after process interruption.
- Return only published current guidance to ordinary scope reads under current Permission and target access;
  proposals and rejected rationale remain in a separately permissioned history. Keep contradictory published
  claims attributed and independently assessable, not automatically merged. TICKET-00050 owns the basic
  human UI and prohibited-content removal, not publication authority.
- Out of scope: source-change notifications/reassessment (TASK-00192), Jev lookup (TICKET-00049), automatically
  importing private reasoning, new generic Role administration or a second Planning approval path.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Review target proposal | Accept or reject under current target ownership | Read permitted proposal/source and independently checked evidence | Proposal accepted or rejected after commit | Rejection remains history; acceptance records evidence and eligibility for own publication |
| Publish own target lesson | Publish new linked Repository/Workspace entry from accepted proposal | Revalidate current target owner, source, evidence and decision | Broader entry published after commit | Original source unchanged, one attributed target entry |
| Inspect current broader guidance | N/A — read-only | Read permitted published current entries under target scope | N/A — no business mutation | Only authorized published material, never pending/rejected proposals |
| Reject stale/forged authority | Rejected assessment/publication | Resolve current actor, target and source, including revisions | Safe failure observation where required | No new permission, changed source or partial publication |

Semantic names do not fix whether the safe accepted-publication transition is one atomic command or two.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A real independently authorized Repository steward assesses an Engineer proposal with current source
  access and underlying evidence, then publishes its own attributed Repository entry. The Engineer cannot
  write it directly or inherit target authority from the proposal.
- [ ] A qualified Workspace steward separately publishes at Workspace scope from a permitted direct-target
  proposal; wrong Repository/Workspace, stale assignment or revoked Permission denies it.
- [ ] A reasoned rejection remains safe attributed history, neither deleting/declaring false its source nor
  appearing in ordinary MCP guidance. Acceptance links proposal, checked evidence and original source to a
  distinct new target entry without relocating or editing it.
- [ ] Same-command retries cannot duplicate decision or publication; competing decisions and changing
  sources/authority cannot leave inconsistent partial state. Similar/contradictory proposals remain
  independently attributed rather than auto-merged.
- [ ] Checked-evidence/applicability limits and code-versus-Planning discrepancies remain explicit; private
  source text does not enter an unauthorized review or become a general Workspace bank.
