# Establish and prove independent design review

## Outcome

The repository has a concise project-local `design-review` skill and supporting guidance that independently challenges disposable design evidence, separates objective defects from subjective critique, and returns a traceable `accept`, `revise`, or `reject` verdict. A bounded paired demonstration proves the `design` and `design-review` workflow before production authentication or dashboard design relies on it.

## Scope

- In scope: Add `.pi/skills/design-review/SKILL.md`; add concise project-owned design-review guidance; require explicit independence and a complete review target; render artifacts where possible; inspect the brief, alternatives, provenance, responsive states, interactions, keyboard/focus behavior, semantics, contrast, copy, error/recovery paths, evidence limits, and production handoff; demonstrate a meaningful challenge or seeded omission against an artifact produced through TASK-00005.
- Out of scope: Revising the artifact during review, production UI implementation, planning creation/decomposition, accepting the reviewer’s own design as independent evidence, product-decision authority, implementation review, merge, release, and deployment.

## Validation and permissions

Stop or qualify the verdict when the brief, approved requirements, alternatives, source/asset provenance, runnable artifact where expected, claimed evidence, required states, baseline, or reviewer independence is missing or ambiguous. A blocking finding must cite an unmet requirement, reproducible interaction/accessibility failure, unsafe provenance or tooling issue, or material handoff omission. Taste and optional polish remain explicitly non-blocking unless an approved design requirement makes them objective.

Render the artifact rather than relying only on source or screenshots when practical and safe. Record viewport, state, browser/tool version, automation output, manual observation, reproduction steps, and unverified boundaries. Do not infer WCAG conformance from screenshots or automated tools; disclose untested assistive technology, browsers, content, viewports, interactions, forced colors, zoom/reflow, and other material limits.

Review evidence remains under the owning `.runs/prototypes/<scope>/` or another explicitly authorized ignored path. No application permission model applies. The reviewer has read, render, check, and report authority for the bounded artifact, not authority to edit it, change planning/product scope, implement production code, accept their own prior design, merge, release, or deploy.
