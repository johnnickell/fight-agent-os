# Inspect and reconcile original generation outcomes

## Outcome

Authorized consumers inspect and reconcile the original generative attempt, its attributed usage and remaining
uncertainty after cancellation, interruption or lost response, without duplicate paid work or fabricated savings.
This is the reusable provider-outcome contract needed by durable sessions and linked continuations.

## Scope

- In scope: Owned outcome/accounting transitions and protected inspection, bounded explicit original-attempt
  reconciliation, streaming normalization, late observations, cancellation/uncertainty and safe provenance.
- Out of scope: Another generic queue/session store, provider SDK implementation, credentials, automatic paid
  retry/failover, proposal schema validation, hidden reasoning telemetry or standalone billing Dashboard.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Record an original observation | Authenticated exact attempt/provider-correlation observation | Current original attempt/generation and qualified observation semantics | Attributable committed outcome/usage facts | Protected original outcome and reservation accounting, never a new request |
| Inspect outcome/accounting | N/A — authorized read | Scoped exact status, provenance and measured/estimated/unavailable usage | N/A | Safe facts; no provider polling, generation or repair as a read side effect |
| Reconcile or request stop | Explicit bounded original-operation reconciliation/stop via owning participants | Current authority, original identity, actual supported provider facilities and liveness | Confirmed owning observations only | Known outcome or bounded pending/needs-human state, not proof of unsent work |
