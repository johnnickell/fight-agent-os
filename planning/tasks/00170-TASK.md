# Inspect compaction history and annotate recovery incidents

## Outcome

Authorized users can inspect a bounded, correlated history of compaction and continuation in local Pi 1.1.0
sessions, annotate recovery incidents and compare policies with honest denominators, cost and uncertainty.
Investigation uses protected evidence references without making telemetry a source of authority or ingesting
private session contents into ordinary logs.

## Scope

- Consume the lifecycle facts from [TASK-00168](00168-TASK.md) and its checkpoint dependency under the accepted
  [WF-030 observation contract](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-030-define-context-retention-and-resume-contract.md#compaction-observability-and-improvement)
  and WF-017 ownership/history rules. Target **Pi 1.1.0**. Deliver bounded local inspection and sanitized protected
  records without requiring a future browser Dashboard, managed execution service or new general observability platform.
- Persist bounded observations through a versioned local representation compatible with the established contract,
  separated from recoverable checkpoint storage. Reuse existing owning observation facilities where available;
  do not pretend a future managed service already exists or introduce a second Planning authority/database.
- Correlate actual session/lineage, role/Agent, authorized parent-child links, attempt/checkpoint revisions and
  TASK/Workflow/phase/process identifiers only where they exist. Local sessions never manufacture managed IDs.
  Preserve initiating actor, automatic/manual mode, timestamps/accounting source, policy/rubric/Harness/runtime/
  evaluator versions, bounded reason identifiers, digests and protected references.
- Inspect resolved thresholds and crossings, early requests, recommendation/snooze/cancel, candidate/validation/
  activation, compaction, resume, fallback/repair and failure. Distinguish a recommendation, saved checkpoint,
  successful compaction and successful continuation. Never infer a missing terminal outcome as success.
- Accept compatible Jev evaluation and optional selection/repair facts when their producers are installed.
  Display unsupported/not-installed, unavailable, incomplete and absent observations explicitly; do not synthesize
  such data or require TASK-00169/00165/00166 solely to inspect the baseline lifecycle.
- Retain bounded optional segment identifiers, inclusion/exclusion outcomes and required-field validation facts
  when available, not segment bodies. Show before/after context counts with measured/estimated/unavailable labels;
  separate Jev/checkpoint usage and latency while comparing total available cost. Use stable usage identities to
  avoid double counting repeated or cumulative values; unavailable is never zero.
- Provide bounded per-session inspection and scope-filtered comparisons across compatible policy versions:
  observed compaction/resume outcomes, fallback and blocked-resume rates, context reduction, available total
  cost/latency and attributed recovery incidents. Name populations, observation windows, exclusions, missing
  outcomes and denominators. Do not combine incompatible measures or present partial evidence as a full cohort.
- Allow attributed annotations for lost constraints, missed questions, repeated work, recovered context and
  uncertain/replayed operations, with authorized protected evidence references. Corrections retain attribution
  and original facts instead of silently rewriting history. Distinguish measured events, human/Agent reports,
  reviewer-verified evidence and causal claims; association alone does not show compaction caused an incident.
- Enforce bounded query ranges/results, aggregation work, record/annotation size and local storage growth under
  operator-owned limits. Define and record concrete bounds before activation. Follow WF-017's separate history/
  usage retention categories; do not inherit the checkpoint's 30-day content grace as an invented metadata policy.
- Handle delayed, missing, duplicate, out-of-order and corrupt observations honestly, with stable correlation and
  idempotent ingestion where applicable. Gaps do not authorize resume, replay tools or imply checkpoint bytes are
  unavailable. Observation delivery failures remain separate from failures to persist required recovery state.
- Resolve protected evidence through the current authorized owning capability. Missing/purged checkpoint/source
  contents remain explicit even when metadata survives. Raw conversations, checkpoint prose, source/full-tool
  bodies, hidden reasoning and credentials stay out of normal records, annotations and presentation. Bounded
  incident descriptions cannot become a backdoor transcript or secret-ingestion path.
- Maintain parent/child and independent-review privacy. Access to one session or aggregate does not grant access
  to another's private context or references; do not leak excluded sessions through aggregate metadata.
- Exclude automatic policy tuning, causal/optimal-intelligence claims, checkpoint storage/retention ownership,
  automatic repair/effect replay, new authority grants, general browser Dashboard delivery and runtime enrollment.

## Validation and permissions

Authorize both inspected scopes and each resolved reference using actual current identity. Apply role/session
boundaries to aggregation as well as individual views. Restrict annotation/correction/policy operations to their
permitted actors; an Agent may report an incident without its report becoming a verified cause or operational
approval. Reject oversized/prohibited content and untrusted references; records cannot execute commands or
change compaction policy. Protect local storage/configuration through the supported guard routes and retain their
same-user isolation limitations. No provider call is required just to inspect deterministic history or compute rates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real Pi 1.1.0 baseline lifecycle observations can be followed from tier/request through checkpoint and
  compaction to resumed/blocked/failed outcome, with actual identities, versions and provenance preserved.
- [ ] Inspection works without semantic timing/optional selection/repair producers installed and labels absent
  capabilities honestly; compatible producer facts can be included without fabricating earlier history.
- [ ] Duplicated/delayed/out-of-order/missing/corrupt records produce stable counts or explicit gaps, never false
  success, doubled cost or permission to resume/replay work. Durable checkpoint recovery remains independent.
- [ ] Comparisons show explicit populations/windows/denominators, outcome coverage, measured/estimated/unknown
  context and usage, exclusions and available totals. Smaller context alone is not claimed as improved recall.
- [ ] Authorized incident annotations and corrections retain attribution and source facts, distinguish reports
  from verified evidence, and cannot silently rewrite lifecycle outcomes or automatically tune policy.
- [ ] Cross-session/role/parent-child and reviewer access checks protect both detail and aggregates; missing/purged
  references do not masquerade as recoverable content. Ordinary records contain no private bodies or credentials.
- [ ] Configured bounds and applicable history/retention categories hold for queries, records and aggregation;
  representative continuation incidents can be investigated without unsupported causal claims or transcript ingestion.
