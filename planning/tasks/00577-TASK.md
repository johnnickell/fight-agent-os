- Replace command-shaped IssueInvitation with genuine owned Domain intent and registered void handler for its
  independent invitation/assignment/audit coordination. HTTP generates the UserId before dispatch; trusted actor,
  email/mode and deliberately validated intent are supplied, not raw AuthenticationContext or transport objects.
- Preserve direct nested InvitePendingUser and AssignRoleToUser dispatch, canonical email claims and separate
  membership evidence. Do not wrap package handlers in an outer transaction. Required owned audit coordination
  remains explicit; no fictitious atomic rollback across already committed steps or mutable command result.
- Put authentication, INVITE_USERS, independent ASSIGN_SUPER_ADMIN plus explicit elevation confirmation, target/
  installation admission and existing issuance/status/recovery rate limits in adapters. Use authoritative package
  inputs; no repeated blanket Application authorization or solely ordinary revocation-race serialization.
- Dispatch RetryInvitationDelivery, ResendInvitationDelivery, CorrectPendingInvitation and
  FindInvitationDeliveryStatus directly where they express the use case. Keep only independently justified owned
  coordination; remove RecoverInvitation/ReadInvitationStatus admission/forwarding layers after replacement proof.
- Preserve existing retry/same-generation, resend/successor and pending-address-correction contracts, purpose and
  claim consistency, required audit and post-commit delivery timing. Delegate package result identity/type and
  transition guarantees to the qualified owner, rather than reproducing them through consumer bus/result shims.
- Remove broad LogicException-to-conflict handling and fabricated empty/not-found successes. Use intentional
  qualified package failures; preserve real missing/optional status distinctions, safe denial and uncertain commit/
  post-commit outcomes. Unknown internal faults cannot become expected recovery conflicts.
- Present safe package Views directly and justified typed partial issuance results where actual coordination
  requires them. Preserve known retained identity, assignment/audit/delivery meanings and explicit operator recovery;
  a lost response never authorizes automatic reinvitation, compensation or original-response journals.
- Correct limiter/policy ownership, descriptive collaborators, capability namespaces, actual Actions/Data/Responders,
  composition, OpenAPI, affected browser callers/docs and meaningful tests; remove superseded types. Coordinate
  package-required correction authorization hooks through their actual supported contract, never a null/no-op shim.
