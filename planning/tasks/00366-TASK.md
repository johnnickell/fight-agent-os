# Share and hand off protected Planning history

## Outcome

A conversation owner explicitly shares read or continue access, or hands off bounded Planning work, without making
private transcript/drafts public to all repository planners. Narrowly authorized retention/redaction remains
available through its existing owner; normal users cannot permanently delete history.

## Scope

- In scope: Scoped recipient selection, explicit read/continue sharing and handoff, current access/use enforcement,
  revocation/conflict presentation, private-history inspection and guarded retention/redaction interactions.
- Out of scope: Notifications/comments/mentions, simultaneous free-form co-editing, credential transfer, another
  grant engine or transcript writer, public share links and ordinary-user permanent deletion.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Share or hand off | Explicit exact-scope expected-state share/handoff/revocation operation | Owner, authorized recipient and read/continue scope | Committed owning sharing/handoff/audit facts | Deliberately bounded access, never repository-wide transcript visibility |
| Read or continue | Existing protected reads or explicit conversation input | Current recipient rights, delegation, active owner and generations | Existing input/attempt facts only for authorized mutation | Read-only recipients cannot act; continuation remains serialized |
| Retain or redact | Narrow confirmed TASK-00354 operation | Exact supported content/hold scope and current authority | Existing retention/redaction intent/outcome facts | Safe retained/redacted/uncertain result, not blanket deletion |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00361's scoped conversation UI and TASK-00354's protected history/retention contract. The initiating
human's provenance remains immutable; sharing does not rewrite it or transfer Agent credentials, snapshots or prior
human approvals. Authorize owner action, recipient Repository visibility, read versus continue rights and every
subsequent access independently. Management rights, role names, accepted-artifact visibility and possession of an ID
are not transcript grants. Denied/missing targets do not expose private existence or recipient details.

Persist exact bounded sharing/handoff changes, audit and required intent on the canonical transaction with current
owner/generation checks. Use existing conversation authority participants rather than a parallel authorization
platform. Revocation and concurrent handoffs fence stale reads, submissions and callbacks; expected-state conflicts
preserve the original outcome. Continue access never implies simultaneous free-form editing: retain one active turn,
serialize accepted input and require exact current candidate/answer revisions. Handoff must revalidate current
human/Agent delegation and scope before further work, not renew consumed/revoked approval from history. If a new
assignment or linked continuation is required, use its existing owner and hold until supported.

Other authorized planners see only accepted artifacts/decisions and safe progress by default. Shared transcript,
rejected ideas and candidates remain protected, bounded and no-store. ADR 0003/0004 invalidation retires local data;
late pages/streams cannot restore a revoked view or another identity's content. No share data or drafts in URLs,
browser persistence, ordinary logs or cross-tab messages.

Expose only currently authorized retention/hold/redaction actions with exact scope, confirmation and safe status.
TASK-00354 retains eligibility, reference/hold fences, protected storage and original deletion recovery. Abandonment
is not deletion authority. Distinguish logical access denial, deletion requested, confirmed removal and unsupported
backup erasure; missing participants preserve content. Do not retain prohibited payloads in redaction audit or allow
client-supplied object/filesystem paths.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Owner-first visibility hides transcript/rejected ideas/drafts from ordinary authorized planners; explicit
  read/continue grants expose only the approved scope to currently authorized recipients.
- [ ] Read-only recipients cannot submit/Apply; handoff preserves initiating provenance and rechecks current
  delegation without credentials, inherited approval or simultaneous co-editing.
- [ ] Concurrent handoff/revocation/late read or input cannot bypass expected state or current authority; duplicate
  requests resolve original outcomes and private caches retire safely.
- [ ] Narrow retention/redaction uses the actual owner and honest pending/unsupported status; ordinary users cannot
  permanently delete history or turn Abandon into cleanup authority.
- [ ] Owned multi-user authorization/transaction/client tests and actual accessible browser evidence pass.
