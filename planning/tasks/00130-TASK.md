## Validation and permissions

Only the current project's decisions grant planning and engineering authority. A same-session builder's two internal passes are not independent approval; until sub-agent support exists, a separate reviewer invocation may perform two named passes without claiming two independent agents. Do not require tests of skills, Markdown text, configuration or quality tools in the product suite; verify guidance and planning with their owning tools. Existing security and server-side package/application policy remain authoritative regardless of transport guards.
