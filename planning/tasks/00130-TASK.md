## Outcome

Project-local standards and skills give implementers and independent reviewers precise placement, CQRS/DDD ownership, proportionate behavior-testing, and separate Spec and Standards review checks. A bounded comparison inventories Factory skills and identifies missing project-local capabilities. The maintainer selected audit, fix (diagnosis and standalone bug-TASK drafting only), architecture and adopt, plus a TASK-numbered PR title/body template and TASK-numbered feature-branch convention. Their implementation scope needs an explicit split decision before work resumes.

## Validation and permissions

Only the current project's decisions grant planning and engineering authority. A same-session builder's two internal passes are not independent approval; until sub-agent support exists, a separate reviewer invocation may perform two named passes without claiming two independent agents. Do not require tests of skills, Markdown text, configuration or quality tools in the product suite; verify guidance and planning with their owning tools. Existing security and server-side package/application policy remain authoritative regardless of transport guards.
