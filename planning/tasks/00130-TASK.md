# Strengthen engineering guidance and TASK delivery conventions

## Outcome

Project-local standards and skills give implementers and independent reviewers precise placement, CQRS/DDD ownership, proportionate behavior-testing, and separate Spec and Standards review checks. A bounded comparison inventories Factory skills and identifies missing project-local capabilities. This TASK delivers the guidance and TASK-numbered branch/PR conventions; the maintainer-approved skill implementations are separate [TASK-00131](00131-TASK.md), [TASK-00132](00132-TASK.md), [TASK-00133](00133-TASK.md) and [TASK-00134](00134-TASK.md).

## Scope

- Completed first scope: Compare relevant Fight Software Factory architecture, HTTP, naming, testing, work and review guidance with current Agent OS standards and skills; adopt only project-specific, compatible rules; update `docs/engineering/STANDARDS.md`, `docs/engineering/REVIEW.md`, and applicable `.pi/skills/work/` and `.pi/skills/review/` instructions; document a prioritized, scoped gap map for missing audit and architecture-review capabilities and other relevant engineering skills.

This TASK keeps the gap map as a comparison, not an implementation of those skills.

Align execution, review, landing and planning examples without renaming the existing unpublished TASK-00130 branch or unrelated branches as a side effect.

## Validation and permissions

Only the current project's decisions grant planning and engineering authority. A same-session builder's two internal passes are not independent approval; until sub-agent support exists, a separate reviewer invocation may perform two named passes without claiming two independent agents. Do not require tests of skills, Markdown text, configuration or quality tools in the product suite; verify guidance and planning with their owning tools. Existing security and server-side package/application policy remain authoritative regardless of transport guards.
