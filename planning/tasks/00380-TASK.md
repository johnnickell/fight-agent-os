# Redact prohibited content across retained copies

## Outcome

An authorized exact-content redaction makes prohibited retained evidence inaccessible and reconciles its removal
across supported copies, preserving only secret-free attribution and honest storage/backup limits rather than
retaining the prohibited payload for audit or promising instantaneous physical erasure.

## Scope

- In scope: Bounded redaction admission, current access denial/invalidation, copy-owner participation, recoverable
  external deletion/safe replacement, secret-free tombstones/placeholders and precise coverage/status reporting.
- Out of scope: General DLP/telemetry platform, transcript ingestion, unrestricted content inspection, source repair,
  ordinary-user history deletion, retroactive verdict rewriting and pretending unsupported backups are erased.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restrict detected prohibited evidence | Authorized exact-content redaction under expected state | Current content identity, authority and affected copy inventory | Committed secret-free restriction/intent facts | Supported disclosure routes deny; durable removal intent survives restart |
| Reconcile supported copies | Existing owner operations for exact projection/index/preview/export/object targets | Original operation and actual copy/storage outcomes | Confirmed redaction/tombstone or unresolved facts | Bounded external removal/replacement without retaining prohibited audit payload |
| Inspect correction/coverage | N/A — authorized safe status | Attribution, coverage, pending/confirmed deletion and backup expiry | N/A | Honest status and next action, never hidden content or a fabricated completion |
