# Attach and inspect the same conversation

## Outcome

An authorized terminal or API client attaches to the original conversation and reloads durable status, pending
questions and owner-first history without another Agent, credential holder or turn. Later browser Planning consumes
these same operations rather than inventing a client-specific conversation authority.

## Scope

- In scope: Actual terminal/API attach/detach and state resynchronization, safe status/progress and bounded private
  history retrieval, current visibility checks and original conversation/session lineage.
- Out of scope: Full browser journey, sharing/handoff product decisions, co-editing, another transcript store,
  automatic resume/provider work, cancellation semantics, provider switching and Workflow transcript ingestion.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attach/detach a client | Authorized attachment lifecycle where state is owned | Original conversation, current access and actual lifecycle | Safe attachment observations only | Client view of existing state; no Agent, holder, turn or grant creation |
| Reload durable state | N/A — authorized read | Current committed generation, result/progress and pending question | N/A | Coherent baseline before following optional observations |
| Read private history | N/A — bounded authorized read | Owner-authorized transcript/input/history pages through runtime/storage owner | N/A | Private bounded content, not a copy into ordinary Workflow observation |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00351's production conversation/turn authority. Authenticate actual clients and recheck current intent-
specific visibility/continuation access separately from Agent-management rights. Owner-first transcript/draft access
is distinct from safe progress/accepted artifact visibility. Consume explicit sharing/handoff authority only where
the owning journey supplies it; no default Repository-wide transcript grant or new collaboration policy here.

Closing a terminal/tab, logging out or losing transport detaches; it does not cancel an accepted turn or dispose of
storage. Live authority revocation still denies subsequent protected actions/reads. Reattachment resolves original
conversation and committed state, not a fresh Agent, holder, session grant or replacement model call. Unknown
liveness displays unknown/held, not idle/success. Powered-off machines do not execute and no cross-machine migration
is inferred from retained state.

Bound history pages, attachments, payload and observation work; use stable identities/current generations rather
than assuming ordered complete streaming. Reload durable state before live observations; duplicates/missing/reordered
progress cannot become authority or trigger repair. Concurrent clients share TASK-00349's serialized submission
contract and display explicit conflicts without losing accepted input. Preserve pending question and proposal
references; do not claim recovery of unsaved browser preview edits or accept model-authored completion.

Private history uses the existing protected runtime storage/access owner, with no rival application transcript
writer, ordinary-log export or ingestion into Workflow history. Exclude raw secrets/hidden reasoning and sanitize
unsafe content/errors. Scope-filter status/detail even for missing IDs. Reads never launch/retry work, poll a paid
provider, refresh snapshots, switch models or cancel. TASK-00353 owns explicit stop/recovery; TASK-00342/00325 own
material-change continuation/switching.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual supported terminal/API clients detach/reconnect to the same running or completed conversation with
  durable accepted input, pending question and exact lineage; no additional Agent, holder or turn is created.
- [ ] Current owner/private-history policy separates detailed transcript access from safe observation; revoked,
  wrong-Repository or forged attachments deny without private existence/content leakage.
- [ ] Concurrent clients and incomplete progress reload authoritative state and expose conflicts rather than
  duplicating input, claiming lost work or triggering paid retry.
- [ ] Disconnect/logout does not cancel accepted work; machine-off/unknown-liveness limits remain explicit, and
  reads cannot launch, cancel, repair or silently refresh configuration.
- [ ] Real reconnect evidence uses a selected qualified provider path where needed, not both providers configured
  or a new demo/browser product; component fixtures are not live conversation evidence.
