# Write and verify immutable operational archives

## Outcome

Enrolled archive intake continuously preserves captured records independently of live search and returns a durable
verified receipt only after exact stored content has been read back. This approved slice B of
[TICKET-00114](../tickets/00114-TICKET.md) owns intake through immutable storage, verification and catalog publication;
TASK-00460 owns the separate collector binding and queue settlement.

## Scope

- In scope: Versioned gzip UTF-8 JSONL envelope/manifest, bounded segment admission, durable original intent,
  immutable private write, readback verification, original-operation retry/recovery and catalog/receipt publication.
- Out of scope: Another storage stack or queue, Loki-chunk archives, day-14 export, public catalog/browser, restore,
  bulk download, final retention/purge operations or a permanent Artifact per log.

Follow [WF-039](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-039-define-searchable-retention-and-verified-archives.md)'s full archive
contract. Preserve native fields and exact captured bytes through reversible encoding, including malformed/binary/
fragment records; parsed-and-reserialized JSON is not original bytes. Retain source generation/stream/position,
record/fragment identities, original collection/event clocks, unknown provenance and existing preservation-owner
references. Segment identity comes from an owning ID factory/UUID library and survives retries. Partition compatible
authorization/preservation categories; mixed segments inherit strictest/latest eligibility.

Close at 256 KiB uncompressed framed bytes or five seconds, whichever first; at most 16 open segments. Split at
record/fragment boundaries so compressed object plus metadata/request envelope fits 1 MiB. Reuse TASK-00457's 2-GiB
archive queue inside the 5-GiB collection allocation; open-segment staging counts there, not in a hidden spool.
One verifier, 10-second request deadlines, bounded WF-038 backoff and recovery passes of at most 100 segments or
30 seconds with one pass in flight. Persist progress. Account archive objects/catalog/manifests/versions/compaction
against the 50-GiB admission target and 64-GiB physical archive allocation; separate namespaces are not hard isolation.

Commit intent and expected compressed/raw SHA-256 digests, sizes and count before immutable upload. External storage
calls occur after commit. Reconcile uncertain writes under the same identity; different bytes at that identity are
a conflict, never overwrite permission. Read back and verify compressed size/digest, bounded decompression, raw
size/digest, framing and count before publication. HEAD, ETag, metadata or upload ACK alone is insufficient. Reject
actual expansion/size/count excess rather than trusting declared values. Publication and verified receipt must
agree with the durable original outcome and current owner fence; crashes remain pending until reconciled.

At capacity, request only owner-eligible reclamation; otherwise stop archive admission and expose degraded state
through the existing independent status contract. Never shorten archive retention or delete held sole copies to
meet capacity. The collector may apply its separate eligible queue-loss policy, never label loss as archival.
Verification proves captured content currently retrievable, not capture completeness, restore readiness, power-loss
safety or host-loss recovery. Actual storage durability/private access/resource fit requires direct qualification.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admit captured segment work | Enrolled exact bounded archive intent | Current owner/capacity and original intake identity | Committed archive intent | Expected immutable bytes and recoverable pending work |
| Write and verify | Execute original transfer/verification claim | Actual stored bytes and independent expected manifest | Verified/pending/conflict facts after commit | Catalog availability and receipt only for proven content |
| Recover interruption | Reconcile original intake/upload/verification/publication | Existing original outcome and bounded readback | Reconciled lifecycle facts | No new identity, re-aging, blind overwrite or duplicate truth |
| Handle full/corrupt/read-only storage | Bounded retry/status, no unsafe purge | Actual pressure, error class and current preservation | Secret-free degradation facts | Finite pending/admission with honest gaps and no false receipt |

## Validation and permissions

Only enrolled archive writers/verifiers use scoped private storage credentials. Reuse the qualified existing
SeaweedFS/S3 capability, isolated from authoritative Artifacts and Loki by namespace, credentials and physical
accounting. No caller object paths/URLs, public bucket or Agent storage powers. TASK-00467 supplies current ownership
and disclosure/restriction fences; private manifest fields remain private. Raw content is not pipeline-redacted or
copied into ordinary errors/audit. ADRs [0001](../adr/0001-application-ownership-and-orchestration.md) and
[0002](../adr/0002-postgresql-consistency-and-durable-effects.md) govern transaction/intent/provider boundaries.
Runtime state belongs under ignored `var/`, not scratch `.runs/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual private immutable storage preserves exact raw/native/fragment content and original identities/clocks;
  conflicting same-identity writes cannot replace accepted bytes.
- [ ] Every intent/upload/readback/verification/publication interruption reconciles the original operation; receipt
  and catalog availability require full actual compressed/raw digest, size, framing and count verification.
- [ ] Actual staging, open-segment, request, verifier, recovery and aggregate physical bounds hold without another
  spool; full/read-only/corrupt storage remains honest pending/conflict/degraded state.
- [ ] Current owner restrictions prevent stale publication and protected-copy release. Intake works independently
  of live search and does not require TASK-00460's completed collector binding.
- [ ] Exact storage durability/privacy/resources and retained-volume fit are directly qualified; owned tests and
  full `./bin/build` pass without representing the 50-GiB target as proved 90-day capacity.

## Verification and evidence

Use manifested safe native/malformed/binary/oversized/tied records, actual PostgreSQL and private S3 integration;
compare reconstructed bytes and source identities. Interrupt each owned boundary, lose acknowledgements, conflict
retries and exercise corrupt/expanded/read-only/full results on separately authorized isolated targets. Directly
qualify exact storage API/version/security/license/sync/restart and physical isolation with owning tools, not product
configuration/migration/server-internal tests. Measure archive publication against WF-042's p95 <=60-second objective
and disclose workload, retained-volume compression/overhead/hold headroom and untested failure classes. Run focused
wrappers and full `./bin/build`; runtime experiments require separate authority.
