# Review ancestor updates and restore retained template revisions

## Outcome

An authorized administrator can deliberately restore a retained template revision or review an ancestor change
without losing custom choices, rewriting history or silently updating existing Agents. This completes controlled
template evolution and recovery under TICKET-00076 through TASK-00300's existing editor/publication boundary,
normally one independently reviewable PR. It is not a prerequisite to initial human editing or AI proposals.

## Scope

- In scope: Authorized retained-revision selection with current validation and impact review; exact three-way
  ancestor comparison; keep-current or human-reviewed candidate publication; bounded browser conflict/recovery
  and affected transaction/reference evidence through the existing Harness authority.
- Out of scope: Another editor/publisher/diff registry, automatic ancestor propagation, history rewrite, Agent
  grant/revoke/rebind or session changes, generalized resource inheritance, AI/provider calls, source rollback,
  template purge, Workflow eligibility or a separate proof product.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inspect a retained revision for restoration | N/A — read-only | Authorized exact retained bytes/references, current selection generation, validation and impact through TASK-00300 | N/A — inspection changes nothing | Complete reviewed restoration candidate or explicit safe refusal |
| Restore an eligible retained revision | Owned explicit select-retained-revision operation through the same publication boundary | Fresh actor/scope/reference validation and expected current state | Owned selection fact only after a committed change | Current selection generation and required audit change atomically; historical bytes do not |
| Compare an ancestor change | N/A — read-only | Previous pinned ancestor, proposed new ancestor and current custom revision/differences | N/A | Bounded exact three-way comparison and visible conflicts/provenance |
| Keep current or publish reviewed changes | Keep-current dispatches no mutation; accepted edits use TASK-00300's existing publication operation | Fresh custom/base/ancestor/current-selection facts and complete candidate validation | Existing owned publication fact on committed change only | Custom revision stays unchanged or an explicitly reviewed immutable revision is selected for future use |

## Validation and permissions

Reuse TASK-00300's current MANAGE_AGENT_TEMPLATES SUPER_ADMIN_ONLY authority, trusted human/target/scope policy,
complete candidate validation, actual Agent-reference inspection with separate authorization, original-outcome
handling and canonical transaction composition. No additional management Permission, new store or direct Agent

intent, expected revisions or scoped disclosure.

### Deliberate retained-revision selection

Require the exact template identity, retained revision and expected current-selection generation. Load authoritative
history; never accept client-supplied historical bytes or substitute another identity by name. Show current versus
retained content, requested Permissions, resource/model/scope constraints and attributable affected-Agent references
before confirmation. Revalidate current authority and references at the final write, even for a requested no-op.
A formerly valid revision is not permanently eligible: removed/protected Permissions, inaccessible resources or
changed scope must prevent unsafe restoration rather than be silently dropped or replaced.

If correction is needed, the human can create a reviewed new candidate through TASK-00300; do not mutate the
retained revision and call it rollback. A successful selection preserves the exact historical content/ancestry and
advances current-selection generation with required audit in one transaction. Selecting the already-current
revision after valid checks has no change event or fabricated revision. A pointer that moved away and back does
not validate an old confirmation. Stale state requires reread/review, not replay against a newer generation.

### Exact ancestor comparison and human choice

Identify the three inputs by immutable identity/revision: the previous ancestor pinned by the custom revision,
the explicitly proposed new ancestor, and the custom revision being edited. Resolve original ancestry from the
stored provenance, not caller-selected convenient baselines. Check authorization to every input, scope compatibility,
ancestry validity and bounded traversal. Missing/inaccessible history, cycles, ambiguous origins or incomplete
comparison remain explicit blockers; an unavailable ancestor is not an empty ancestor or permission to fall back
to source. Preserve the custom identity and origin while showing changes to prompts, complete requested Permission
sets, resource/model constraints and scope, not just a textual prompt diff.

Keep inherited and custom changes distinguishable, with conflicting or invalid items available for explicit human
resolution. No automatic merge is itself publication or grant authority. A human may retain the current custom
revision without side effects or review/edit a complete candidate and explicitly publish through TASK-00300.
Bind review to all three exact inputs and current-selection generations; concurrent custom/ancestor changes
invalidate the affected review rather than substitute newer content. Never silently propagate an ancestor change
into descendants, Agents, prompts already bound to Agents or active session snapshots.

The existing-Agent review choice belongs to TICKET-00078. Present its actual available, separately authorized
handoff when delivered; until then explain its unavailability without an enabled dead action or a fake successful
sync. Do not block this template-only outcome on implementation of Permission reconciliation or duplicate it here.
Publication alone still leaves every Agent's grants and bound revision unchanged.

### Browser, transactions and uncertainty

Use the existing editor/history/impact components and ADR 0003/0004's volatile drafts, sole current authority and
private-data generations. Protect read loaders, controls and callbacks with current permissions. Clear/retire stale
confirmations and late successes/errors; they cannot restore an old login, overwrite a newer custom draft or
reactivate cancelled review. No browser-persisted diff/draft, source bodies in URLs/history, auto-publication on
refresh, cross-tab authority broadcast or automatic mutation replay. Render content inertly and retain no-store/
redaction and accessible keyboard/focus/error recovery from TASK-00300.

Reuse the same qualified reference/current-authority fences and single canonical transaction for selection and
required review/audit state. No outer/nested transaction, copied package policy or repository-hidden side effects.
Post-commit event failure or lost response is not rollback; inspect the original authorized operation through
TASK-00300's outcome mechanism before another explicit action. Equal current content alone is not original-outcome
proof. Preserve unresolved outcomes and all immutable history rather than compensate with another selection.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual authorized browser/API restoration selects an eligible retained revision after current validation and
  impact review, preserving historical bytes and advancing only current-selection/provenance/audit as specified.
- [ ] Exact three-way comparison shows old ancestor/new ancestor/custom changes across prompt, requested authority
  and resource/scope constraints. Keep-current mutates nothing; publication uses the existing complete review path.
- [ ] Missing, inaccessible, invalid or conflicting references/ancestry and stale custom/ancestor/current state
  cannot be silently substituted, omitted, auto-merged-and-published or treated as an empty successful comparison.
- [ ] PostgreSQL races, pointer return, no-op checks, rollback and response/event failures preserve current authority,
  exact intent and original-outcome distinctions without history rewrites, duplicate effects or blind retry.
- [ ] Existing Agent grants/bindings and active snapshots remain unchanged; separately owned Agent reconciliation
  is a truthful authorized handoff or visibly unavailable, never an implicit effect or reverse blocker.
- [ ] Accessible browser success/denial/conflict/disconnect and newer-login/draft races pass owned checks and
  `./bin/build`, with exact evidence, warnings and limits. Human-only editing remains independent of AI availability.
