# Enforce same-Team delegation and reviewer independence

## Outcome

Existing Workflow phase-admission operations accept only currently eligible Agents from the Workflow's recorded
Team under bounded authority. Team Lead cannot borrow another Team's member, grant itself roster control or assign
a contributor as independent reviewer. Missing or uncertain eligibility holds rather than changing roles or scope.

## Scope

- In scope: Reusable Team/Agent checks and binding at the existing owning phase-delegation/admission boundary,
  current grants/commitments, contribution/subject independence, historical Team provenance and safe outcomes.
- Out of scope: New phase engine, claims/session store, Review/QA/publication implementation, complete role journeys,
  roster administration, cross-Team peer dispatch, emergency approval or granting new direct Permissions.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delegate an eligible phase | Existing owner accepts bounded delegation/phase-admission intent | Current Workflow Team, candidate authority/profile, grant, commitment and subject facts | Existing owning assignment/handoff facts only after commit | One same-Team assignment and its bounded owning commitment/grant, no second TASK claim |
| Select independent reviewer | Existing owning review-phase admission, not a review verdict | Exact subject and contribution lineage, same-Team candidate and fresh-session requirement | Existing phase facts only for accepted admission | Eligible independent assignment or explicit hold, not technical acceptance |
| Refuse or reconcile stale delegation | Existing owner refuses/resolves original intent | Current recorded assignment, revisions and actual outcome | No fabricated accepted assignment | Safe no-op/conflict/uncertainty, not cross-Team substitution or duplicate session |

## Validation and permissions

Consume the immutable Team established by TASK-00263's normal intake or the qualified existing confirmed-hotfix
owner, plus TASK-00261's current facts; revalidate at each actual phase admission. Hotfix does not run normal
Team Lead selection to obtain that binding.
The permitted delegator needs the existing current bounded Workflow capability, not a Team-management Permission
or role-name bypass. Intersect initiating human/Workflow grant, actual Agent direct Permissions, Team/Repository/
context/profile/session/host limits and exact phase/subject. A template, prompt, old signature, cached roster or
prior phase acceptance grants nothing. A Team Lead may narrow authorized delegation but cannot expand the root
grant, create/directly reassign Permissions, recruit members or administer the roster.

Reject a candidate not currently a member of the Workflow's assigned Team even if both Teams serve the Repository.
A whole-TASK alternative is an intake choice, not permission to move phases across Teams later. If no eligible
member exists, report the hold and owning human next action. Preserve exact historical Team/Agent/template/session
bindings; a later transfer back or revoke/regrant cannot restore an old admitted epoch. Do not silently rebind or
resume an old session against its Agent's new Team. All accepted claims/commitments and their release/recovery
remain with the existing owners, not a selector-owned busy flag.

Check actual design, implementation and acceptance-evidence contribution against the exact review subject and
lineage. Different names, a senior-capability template, new credentials or a fresh session do not erase a
contributor's disqualification. Unknown lineage cannot establish independence. Every review attempt uses a fresh
conversation/session, including re-review by the same otherwise eligible identity. QA remains separate after
technical review; Release Manager remains separate after qualified acceptance/QA. This TASK supplies admission
constraints, not their full Workflow phases or a review/QA verdict. Hotfix uses its existing explicitly confirmed
human route and non-contributing same-Team reviewer without inserting a Team Lead into the direct repair path.

Use cohort-first current-authority/reference and actual commitment fences on the canonical transaction through
commit/rollback. Shared Agent capacity must serialize across other TASKs/Repositories and competing transfers,
not just within the current phase. Revalidate no-ops and full expected state. Record only one actual phase
assignment/commitment and required audit/intent atomically through the owner; process/provider effects occur later
outside the business transaction under current runtime readiness. A failed acknowledgement does not prove rollback;
resolve the original assignment before any replacement or launch. Never advance intake fairness on a phase handoff.

Return bounded safe actor-visible reasons and provenance, excluding private contribution bodies, unrelated TASKs,
credential/claim/lease data, protected holder details or raw provider errors. Read-side inspection alone creates
no grant, session, accepted assignment or acceptance evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actual owning delegation admits only currently eligible members of the recorded Team under exact phase/
  subject/root-grant limits; direct forged calls, cross-Team borrowing and role-name escalation deny.
- [ ] Independent reviewers have no disqualifying design/build/evidence contribution, with exact subject lineage
  and a fresh review session each attempt. Missing/ambiguous history holds rather than infer independence.
- [ ] Competing assignments/transfers and credential/Permission/grant changes serialize across scopes; rollback,
  no-ops, transfer-back ABA and uncertain results cannot double-book, duplicate launch or revive old admission.
- [ ] Missing members stop visibly without editing roster or Workflow Team. Historical sessions remain immutable,
  hotfix keeps its direct human-confirmed route, and no Review/QA/publication outcome is fabricated.
