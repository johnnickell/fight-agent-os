# Execute bounded finding-driven revisions

## Outcome

Let the existing Workflow repair persisted blocking findings through its original Software Engineer lineage and
fresh independent Review, without a human coordinating each cycle or an unbounded technical/QA retry loop.

## Scope

- In scope: Finding-driven revision admission, durable cycle accounting, Engineer dispositions and new exact
  commit/check handoff, fresh re-review, limit inspection and reasoned human continuation.
- Out of scope: A second Engineer/Workflow engine, automatic scope expansion, reviewer/QA repair, separate QA
  counters, non-blocking preference repairs, publication and automatic continuation of terminal Workflows.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revise blocking findings | Existing Workflow revision admission and bounded Engineer delegation | Current persisted blocking set, lineage, authority and limits | Revision admitted/held and consumed-cycle facts | One authorized Engineer repair cycle |
| Submit repair | Record dispositions and exact revised handoff; admit fresh Review | New commit/check subject and unresolved findings | Revised handoff/re-review facts | New immutable implementation subject and fresh reviewer session |
| Explain or extend a hold | Reasoned human continuation grant under existing lifecycle | TASK, instructions, findings, evidence, resources and proposed limits | Granted continuation or retained Needs Human | Same recoverable Workflow only, no automatic limit increase |

## Validation and permissions

Apply EPIC-00008's shared revision contract. Only persisted unresolved blocking technical or QA findings authorize
automatic revision. Team Lead coordinates the normal path; authorized hotfix consumers retain their direct-human
route without inserting a Team Lead. Bind the admitted blocking set, subject and expected Workflow revision.
Non-blocking findings remain visible and neither start nor consume a cycle. Contradictory findings, unsafe state,
new scope/exception/authority and unsupported judgment hold for a human rather than create repair work.

Default to five cycles with an initial installation maximum of ten. Apply
[TASK-00496's allowance precedence](00496-TASK.md#allowance-precedence-and-human-continuation): permitted Repository
overrides establish the effective standing allowance within installation limits; one explicit positive Start
choice may only narrow that allowance. Omission inherits it; an above-standing request fails rather than being
silently clamped or admitted merely because it is below ten. Display installation default/ceiling, Repository
source, requested/effective standing and run values, consumed and remaining counts. Snapshot and enforce them;
time, cost and process-attempt limits remain separate. Admission is atomic and idempotent, so replay cannot charge
another cycle. QA failure does not reset allowance and continuation cannot erase consumed resources or history.

John approved explicit reasoned human continuation raising the total shared repair allowance up to the run's
pinned installation maximum, initially ten, even when its starting standing allowance was five or its Start choice
narrowed further. Preserve consumption: extending five consumed out of five to a total of ten leaves five cycles,
not ten additional cycles. Append the authorized grant extension and original result under current authority and
expected state; duplicate/replayed requests cannot add another increment. Policy/instruction revisions and the
pinned ceiling remain unchanged. Automatic resume never extends limits, and publication alone changes no active
run. Independent time/cost/concurrency/process limits and all current revocation/safety checks still apply.

The existing Engineer lineage alone repairs and records each finding disposition. Under the reconciled
[tool-mediated contract](../../docs/engineering/TOOL_MEDIATED_EXECUTION.md), the Team Lead requests permitted fixed
checks and trusted application operations execute them and prepare the exact implementation commit; no Engineer
shell or arbitrary Git authority is implied. Every material repair goes to TASK-00445's fresh independent Review. Never
accept a model assertion that a finding was fixed. The semantic acceptance and QA owners decide subsequent
eligibility; a successful repair process is not acceptance. Mechanical reconciliation alone consumes no cycle.

Revalidate current Agent/human delegation, original scope, Repository/context/session and expected revisions at
each authority-bearing operation. Durable state/audit/dispatch intent precede effects; reconcile unknown original
outcomes before another launch. Pause/cancel/takeover preserve the existing claim/quiescence and attempt semantics.
A new reasoned human grant resumes only an eligible recoverable Workflow, never reopens terminal failure/cancellation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Only current persisted blocking findings admit repair; non-blocking preference and ambiguous findings do
  not trigger or consume cycles.
- [ ] Five-cycle default, narrowing-only positive Start choice, initial ten-cycle installation maximum and
  independent resource limits are enforced with visible provenance/requested/effective/consumed/remaining values;
  a request above the effective standing allowance refuses even when below the installation maximum.
- [ ] Technical and later QA findings consume the same allowance; duplicates, restart and continuation cannot
  double-charge, reset it or lose Engineer lineage.
- [ ] Repair produces attributable dispositions and a new commit/check subject before fresh independent Review;
  reviewer/QA cannot repair and Engineer cannot accept its own work.
- [ ] Exhaustion and scope/authority uncertainty enter Needs Human. An explicitly authorized reasoned human
  extension may raise the total repair allowance only up to the pinned installation maximum; consumption remains
  counted, duplicate extension requests do not add allowance again, and policy/instructions stay pinned.
  Automatic execution cannot extend limits, waive independent restrictions or reopen an ineligible terminal Workflow.
