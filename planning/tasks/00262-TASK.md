# Recommend fair default or alternate-Team intake

## Outcome

For one human-selected TASK, recommend an eligible idle Lead on its Repository's default Team or a fair,
repeatable whole-TASK intake by another explicitly associated Team. No recommendation claims work, advances a
fairness turn or borrows a peer from another Team. Genuine eligibility uncertainty remains a visible hold.

## Scope

- In scope: Deterministic protected intake recommendation from current owned candidate facts and authoritative
  committed intake history; default preference, fair equivalent choices, exact policy/provenance and safe holds.
- Out of scope: TASK autopick/ranking, AI route choice, scheduling/dispatch, another queue/history authority,
  cross-Team phase delegation, dynamic roster administration or actual admission/fairness writes.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prefer an eligible default-Team Lead | N/A — read-only | TASK-00261's current complete candidates, configured default and intake policy/history | N/A | Exact Team/Lead recommendation or explicit hold, not a reservation |
| Recommend alternate whole-Team intake | N/A — read-only | Eligible associated Teams and each Team's eligible Leads, current committed intake order | N/A | Repeatable permitted alternative for the same selected TASK |
| Explain a hold or policy result | N/A — read-only | Safe exclusion reasons, policy revision and authoritative observation provenance | N/A | Truthful explanation without private actor history or hidden candidates |

## Validation and permissions

Use TASK-00261's current selected-TASK authority and complete candidate facts. Prefer the registered default Team
only when it has a currently eligible idle Team Lead and all applicable constraints hold. If it cannot serve the
TASK, consider eligible explicitly associated alternatives; never infer Repository permission from association or
invent an unassociated fallback. Missing/unknown required facts are not proof that the default is simply busy.
A real dependency, resource, grant or independence ambiguity holds instead of being solved by a semantic guess.

Apply the approved repeatable fairness rule among equivalent eligible choices: least-recently-admitted Team,
then least-recently-admitted eligible Lead within that Team, with stable identity tie-breaks. Prefer eligible
never-admitted candidates before previously admitted equivalents and use the same stable ties among them.
Within an eligible default Team, choose its Lead by that rule rather than weakening default preference. Select
Team before Lead so a Team with many Leads does not acquire extra alternate turns just by roster size.

Scope intake ordering to the Repository and use authoritative committed order from the owning Workflow history,
not wall-clock comparisons, mutable labels or an independent selector ledger. Record the policy identity and
relevant history revision in the result. Reliable absence of prior intake differs from missing history. Any
needed durable fairness state/projection belongs to the Workflow owner and TASK-00263's atomic admission;
recommendation is a pure read. No counter advance for preview, denial, rollback or duplicate original-command
resolution. Bounded repeatability is not a universal starvation guarantee under changing eligibility or capacity.

The recommendation is for a new whole TASK at intake. Once assigned, the Workflow keeps its recorded Team;
this operation cannot migrate an active TASK or grant cross-Team peer control. Current human intent must permit
the chosen scope/alternative; the start owner revalidates it. Triage can display this deterministic result but
cannot claim, expand consent, grant emergency authority or select a different TASK. No extra model call or routine
normal-route confirmation is introduced merely to break an equivalent tie.

Return safe Team/Lead identities, policy/revision provenance and bounded reasons only under current visibility.
No credential, claim/lease, protected holder, private session/tool data or inaccessible contribution history is
returned. Stable candidate ordering and paging cannot hide alternatives or convert partial reads into certainty.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Eligible default-Team intake wins; otherwise eligible associated alternatives use least-recent committed
  Team/Lead intake with stable identity ties, including several Leads and never-admitted equivalents.
- [ ] Repeated reads of identical facts/history yield the same result with zero writes; preview, denial,
  duplicate request and rollback cannot consume turns or alter authored TASK priority.
- [ ] Default/roster/history changes invalidate old observations; unknown facts or unavailable dependencies hold
  with safe reasons rather than invoke AI, choose an unassociated Team or pretend another TASK is eligible.
- [ ] A recommendation never reserves work, changes grants, transfers an active assignment or permits cross-Team
  delegation. A later owning start must still validate the exact current consent, facts and history.
