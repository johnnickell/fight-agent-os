# Inspect and recover the original attempt in the browser

## Outcome

An authorized human sees durable creation progress and retained resources, then explicitly retries, continues
under revised consent or cancels future effects on that same attempt. The production browser exposes truthful
partial/unknown outcomes without automatically replaying work or erasing the original plan.

## Scope

- In scope: Server recovery/continuation/cancellation commands, original-attempt reconciliation, safe progress and
  allowed-action queries, production browser controls and interruption/current-authority behavior.
- Out of scope: Effect-specific repair, another state machine, automatic compensation, cleanup execution,
  support Agent, final completion receipt or automatic Planning/Agent startup.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inspect an attempt | N/A — read-only | Durable verified stage/resource facts and allowed next actions | N/A | Truthful current-authorized progress without effect/provider calls |
| Retry original work | Explicit reconcile/retry command | All completed effects, uncertainty and current policy | Attributed recovery/hold facts | Resume only the first proved-safe incomplete stage |
| Continue with revised scope | Exact preview and explicit continuation confirmation | Original plan, material changes and retained effects | Immutable linked continuation/consent facts | Narrow newly authorized work without rewriting prior history |
| Cancel future work | Current-authorized cancellation | Current admission/in-flight/result state | Confirmed cancellation facts | Close successor admission; retain truthful running and completed effects |

## Validation and permissions

Extend TASK-00412/00414's single attempt authority and TASK-00415's browser boundary. Use operation-specific
view/recovery/continuation/cancel permissions and current Workspace/Host/Runner/stage scope. A receipt, old grant,
role label or formerly enabled button is not authority. Recheck all material inputs and actual owner observations;
unknown resources/process/provider outcomes hold rather than infer safe repetition from a missing process/lease.

Explicit retry first revalidates every completed effect through its owner and reconciles original uncertainty.
Resume only the first incomplete safe stage with original correlation, not a new attempt/directory/commit/remote/
registration/link/context revision. Duplicate controls and lost responses return original outcomes. Unrelated or
changed resources cannot be reset, adopted, overwritten or repaired automatically.

Policy-permitted continuation may complete without publication or deliberately skip failed preparation only with
an exact revised preview and explicit consent. Preserve the immutable original plan and link attributed revised
scope; retain any remote or local effects already created. Newly requested public visibility or other material
effects require their exact warnings/acknowledgements. Never disguise an uncertain earlier effect as skipped.

Cancellation atomically closes future admission through shared stage/resource fences. If an effect already won
admission, report running/completed/unknown truthfully and reconcile it; never claim a rollback or immediate stop.
Browser disconnect, navigation or logout is not cancellation. Revocation denies future unauthorized work while
preserving safe original result evidence. No cancellation/continuation implies cleanup or releases uncertain
ownership. TASK-00417 owns separate cleanup consent and effects.

Commit required state/audit/original results/next intents together under ADR 0002; external reconciliation stays
outside transactions. Use deterministic expected-state fences for competing retry/cancel/outcome/continuation.
Late outcomes cannot reopen closed admission or erase newer scope restrictions. Optional source/preparation paths
remain independent; no provider outage blocks reading stored progress or independently permitted local controls.

Use existing shell/current `/me`/volatile caches and generation fences. Read progress from durable facts, not
browser timers or optimistic success. Fence late private responses and errors, clear data on authority/context
loss, and never replay commands after refresh/reconnect. Show denied, stale, unavailable, interrupted and Needs
Human separately, with bounded sanitized diagnostics and explicit actions. TICKET-00103 alone supplies the final
receipt/Dashboard handoff; no second receipt or automatic Planning conversation is introduced.
