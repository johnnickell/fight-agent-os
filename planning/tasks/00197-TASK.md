# Remove prohibited content across published lineage

## Outcome

A separately authorized safety-removal operation follows a prohibited source through promotion proposals,
published Repository/Workspace copies and rebuildable derived results, blocking disclosure immediately and
removing offending payload from every affected owned surface while retaining only safe lineage and audit.
Owners receive a safe notice of affected links without the prohibited bytes. This independently reviewable
extension must be qualified before any broader publication is exposed as live guidance; it is not routine
shared-history retraction or an impossible promise of third-party erasure.

## Scope

- Extend [TASK-00196](00196-TASK.md)'s quarantined, idempotent Application safety operation over
  [TASK-00191](00191-TASK.md)'s proposal, review, evidence and published-entry lineage. Traverse source
  revisions and attributed links to find copies of prohibited content in pending/rejected/accepted proposals,
  published originals, corrections, summaries, citations and bounded derived views. A link is not proof
  that bytes were copied: inspect and remove/block only affected content while safely retaining unrelated
  independently supported conclusions. No search/graph index becomes a second truth source.
- Quarantine every affected source and owned linked copy before further normal, historical, exact-ID or
  provider disclosure. Redact the prohibited payload from the affected records, views and applicable
  exports; invalidate or rebuild any derived view without the bytes. Tombstones and access receipts contain
  safe actor/target/action/lineage metadata only. A failed partial cleanup stays blocked; record a
  recoverable safe remediation state across process interruption, without retaining the payload in an
  outbox, event, exception, log or replayable receipt.
- Alert each affected Repository/Workspace owner through a durable, permissioned, text-free impact record
  naming safe references that need independent inspection. Reuse TASK-00192's derived-owner notice model if
  available, but do not add it as a prerequisite: safety cannot wait for an ordinary source-correction
  workflow. Normal correction/retraction does not remove prohibited history. A steward's choice to keep an
  independently supported conclusion cannot keep copied prohibited bytes.
- Inventory the real installation's owned memory exports, local backups, provider request/receipt handling
  and retention capabilities before claiming full remediation. Direct local copies must be erased or held
  inaccessible pending verified remediation; address existing backups according to qualified operational
  capabilities and document bounded recovery/deletion. External providers or immutable retained third-party
  copies may not be erasable on demand: prevent further disclosure, follow available removal channels where
  authorized and state residual retention truthfully. No new general backup platform is presumed.
- Provide explicit operator status and recovery through the same guarded Application/first-party path, not
  a browser-side authority shortcut. TICKET-00049's later Jev path must independently screen permitted
  source before provider egress and never serve cached unsafe judgments; do not wait for Jev to implement
  safety deletion. No live publication/Jev disclosure until both safety and provider admission are qualified.
- Out of scope: human presentation (TASK-00198), routine archive or age deletion, updating source code or
  Planning, arbitrary SQL/file operations and automatic publication/review approval.

## Validation and permissions

Only a separately authorized operator may request safety removal; target/source ownership and scope must be
validated without granting a normal Agent permanent deletion rights. Treat proposal text and publication
lineage as data, not instructions. Protect unrelated independent evidence while removing **actual** copied
prohibited content; where safety is uncertain, block disclosure pending verification rather than pretend
a substring match is complete. An unknown backup/export provider, inaccessible third-party retention or
failure to prove removal means a bounded recovery action and a blocked live-publication claim, not a
successful complete-erasure receipt. Verify authorization, idempotency and safe projection after every
state transition under ADR 0001/0002; never hold an external deletion effect inside a business DB
transaction or use a best-effort in-memory callback as recovery authority.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Removing a controlled prohibited source blocks linked current/historical published copies and
  affected pending/rejected proposal bodies, summaries, citations, exports and derived views without
  erasing an unrelated independently supported claim.
- [ ] A published entry known by ID, a stale search result and a later Jev selection cannot retrieve
  quarantined bytes; all affected safe owner notices omit the forbidden text.
- [ ] Competing reads/removals, exact retries and interruption/restart do not disclose partially removed
  material or duplicate changes; unauthorized principals cannot invoke exceptional deletion.
- [ ] Actual backup/export/receipt and provider-disclosure routes are inventoried with verified remediation,
  blocked exposure or explicit third-party retention limits. Do not report complete removal from surfaces
  that cannot be inspected or controlled.
- [ ] Broader publication remains unavailable for live guidance until the qualified cross-lineage removal
  and secret-safe provider admission requirements hold; source change alone neither auto-retracts an
  independently supported published entry nor preserves prohibited bytes.
