# Reconcile rejected proposals without silent application

## Outcome

An authorized planner explicitly requests bounded AI-assisted reconciliation of a rejected single- or multi-record
proposal and reviews a new candidate against current state. Exact base/current/rejected history survives conflict;
no model silently chooses product preferences, overwrites current work or applies its own reconciliation.

## Scope

- In scope: Exact conflict inspection, explicit reconciliation admission, typed/relationship/narrative conflict
  presentation, new current-base candidate lineage and re-entry to existing human review/Apply.
- Out of scope: Automatic merge/apply, automatic paid retry, duplicate provider/attempt owner, generic text merge,
  persisted unsaved browser drafts, workflow execution, publication or template conflict policy.

## Use cases and contracts

| Use case | Commands | Queries | Events | Expected side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inspect rejected changes | N/A — protected read | Exact base, current and rejected versions, member set and safe conflicts | N/A | Preserved comparable history; no model request |
| Request reconciliation | Explicit new bounded proposal admission | Current authority, disclosure, original Apply outcome and provider limits | New attributable proposal-attempt facts | One admitted reconciliation linked to originals, never a replayed unknown Apply |
| Review reconciled candidate | Explicit candidate correction/discard or existing human Apply | New candidate/current revisions and remaining choices | Candidate facts or actual owning Planning facts on separate Apply | New human review before any accepted change |

## Validation and permissions

Consume TASK-00355–00359 through the accepted dependency chain. Resolve uncertain original Apply before offering
another; do not treat timeout as rejection. Correlate each new attempt with exact original command/result, rejected
candidate, base/current revisions, member identities, accepted-answer revisions and human draft/selection generation.
Recheck current permission to read every version, propose and disclose minimized content; prior access/approval does
not preserve revoked visibility. If required versions are inaccessible or redacted, report incomplete comparison
and hold rather than reconstructing hidden content or guessing history.

Reconciliation is a new explicit provider admission with finite limits and preserved original liabilities, not an
automatic response to conflict. Reuse the selected qualified provider and original-outcome accounting; never reset
budgets, silently switch provider/context or regenerate an uncertain call. Currentness/host/delegation controls
still apply. Status/history reads never call a model, repair state or replay mutations.

Distinguish compatible combinations from unresolved conflicts in typed fields, relationships, ordered members and
narrative sections. Keep source/decision provenance visible and reject fabricated references or partial output.
An AI suggestion cannot choose product, priority, boundary or risk preferences: retain explicit human decisions.
Strict Planning candidate validation remains TASK-00357's owner, with domain constraints enforced again on Apply.

Create a new linked candidate bound to the observed current revisions. Never alter base/rejected history or claim
that the current version will remain unchanged. Human edits, concurrent changes, cancellation, discard and late
results invalidate adoption as appropriate; late output cannot overwrite a newer draft or revive stopped work.
A further conflict returns to inspection, not an automatic request loop. Human Create/Apply uses TASK-00358/00359
with fresh exact review and current authority. Multi-record candidates retain all-or-none semantics.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Exact base/current/rejected comparison preserves original history and distinguishes compatible changes from
  typed, relationship and narrative conflicts without last-write-wins or hidden product choices.
- [ ] Only explicit authorized reconciliation invokes bounded provider work; reads and conflict detection make no
  paid request, and unknown original Apply/generation outcomes remain held until safely reconciled.
- [ ] A real selected-provider reconciliation returns a new current-base candidate requiring human review; separate
  Apply revalidates all revisions/permissions and commits atomically or rejects with history intact.
- [ ] Concurrent edits, late output, revocation, cancellation and another stale Apply cannot overwrite human work,
  reveal inaccessible versions, duplicate paid work or trigger automatic regeneration/application.
- [ ] Owned comparison/admission/recovery tests and real conflict-to-reviewed-Apply evidence pass.
